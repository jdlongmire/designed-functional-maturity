# Designed Functional Maturity (DFM)

**A research programme for distinguishing physical maturity from traversed history.**

Designed Functional Maturity (DFM) investigates a simple but consequential question:

> Does a mature physical state necessarily imply that every natural process capable of producing that state was historically traversed?

DFM answers: **not by logical necessity**. It proposes that an intentionally initialized world may begin in a physically coherent, functionally mature state and thereafter operate under ordinary physical law.

DFM is developed here as a **Christian methodological-designist research programme** within the broader Biblical World Model (BWM). Scripture supplies the governing historical boundary conditions; observations of nature supply real constraints on model construction. The programme is intended to be exposed to severe empirical testing rather than insulated from it.

## Core model

The initialization boundary is represented as:

```text
S_0 = G(Phi, L, B)
```

where:

- `S_0` is the initialized physical state;
- `Phi` is the set of commissioned functional requirements;
- `L` is the governing physical law structure;
- `B` is the boundary conditions required for coherent operation.

The central reconstruction distinction is:

```text
Observed state + model + auxiliaries + boundary conditions -> retrodictive history
```

A successful retrodiction is evidence. It is not logically identical to actual history without additional provenance warrant.

Accordingly DFM distinguishes:

- **Historical age (T_H):** actual elapsed duration since an event or origin.
- **Retrodictive age (T_R):** duration reconstructed from present state under a specified model and boundary conditions.

The proposition `T_R = T_H` is a historical inference to be warranted, not an observation.

## Biblical and methodological boundary

DFM takes the Genesis creation sequence as historical and treats created systems as capable of immediate commissioned function. It does not require a minimally initialized universe to manufacture all mature state through antecedent natural runtime.

The programme therefore rejects the premise that deep retrodictive time must be actual traversed time merely because present states can be generated by projecting current laws backward.

This is not permission for arbitrary initial conditions. DFM uses a **no-free-lunch rule**: proposed initialization states must be independently constrained by physical coherence, chemistry, function, canonical boundary conditions, and observation.

## Hard core

The current DFM hard core is:

1. Creation is intentional rather than ontologically autonomous.
2. The creation sequence establishes real historical boundary conditions.
3. Created systems may be initialized in functionally mature states.
4. The initialized state must be physically coherent under the laws governing subsequent operation.
5. Ordinary observed physical law is the default for post-initialization evolution unless independent evidence warrants otherwise.
6. Present state does not uniquely entail a traversed antecedent history merely because such a history can be retrodictively reconstructed.
7. Observations and model-conditioned historical inferences must remain explicitly distinguished.

## Radiogenic systems

DFM retains experimentally measured radioactive decay as its ordinary prospective physics. Accelerated decay is not required by the programme.

For terrestrial radiogenic systems:

```text
S_0^rad = G(Phi_thermal, L_nuclear, B_geo)
```

Long-lived radionuclides participate in Earth's thermal and geochemical architecture. Their initialized inventory therefore cannot be arbitrary.

The current radiogenic hypothesis is:

```text
constrained radiogenic architecture
    -> coherent initialization
    -> expected cross-system order
    -> ordinary decay thereafter
```

Radiometric concordance is consequently investigated as an **orderly marker** of a coherent physical state. Its interpretation as a chronometer of pre-initialization elapsed history requires additional historical-provenance warrant.

The heat budget is an anchor, not a universal explanation. It directly motivates U/Th/K architecture but does not automatically derive U-Pb concordia, isochrons, extinct-radionuclide signatures, mineral closure histories, or non-heat-producing isotope systems. Those are severe tests of the model.

See [WP-002](work-packages/WP-002-retrodiction-initialization-radiogenic-inference.md) and [WP-004](work-packages/WP-004-radiogenic-coherent-initialization.md).

## Cosmological application

DFM applies the same distinction to cosmology. Functional astronomical maturity need not imply that all observed states were assembled through the full conventional antecedent history inferred from present processes.

This creates research questions around stellar light, redshift, supernova products, elemental inventories, low-entropy initialization, early galaxy maturity, and the relationship between created boundary conditions and subsequent ordinary cosmic evolution.

DFM does not treat every surprising observation as confirmation. A useful DFM prediction must follow from the model more strongly than from unconstrained retrospective accommodation.

## Research discipline

DFM is explicitly Lakatosian in programme structure.

**Negative heuristic:** do not abandon the programme's hard core merely because a conventional reconstruction conflicts with its historical boundary conditions.

**Positive heuristic:** develop independently constrained initialization models, derive consequences, compare them against observations, and prefer revisions that increase explanatory reach and predictive content.

Auxiliary hypotheses are permitted under the same standard applied to competing programmes. They incur explanatory cost when introduced only to protect chronology.

### No-smuggling rule

Neither DFM nor a competing programme may describe an inferred historical trajectory as an observation. DFM likewise may not call an inconvenient observation “created maturity” without independent reason.

### Severe-test rule

Where observations could discriminate coherent initialization from traversed natural history, DFM should seek those observations rather than avoid them.

## Epistemic status

DFM is a developing research programme, not a completed physical theory. Several of its strongest claims currently function as constrained hypotheses requiring quantitative development.

In particular, the programme has not yet demonstrated that independently constrained initialization quantitatively reproduces the full observed structure of multi-system radiometric concordance. That is an explicit research burden, not a result to be assumed.

## Relationship to the Biblical World Model

DFM is the cosmological and physical-initialization component of the broader Biblical World Model. It should not be treated as a complete account of geology, biological diversification, post-Flood history, or every question of biblical chronology.
