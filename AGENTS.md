# AGENTS.md

## Mission

Maintain the canonical Designed Functional Maturity research programme with explicit separation among foundations, framework, research controls, domain hypotheses, work packages, and publication artifacts.

## Authority order

1. Canonical documents under `00-governance/`, `01-foundations/`, `02-framework/`, and `03-research-programme/`.
2. Domain material under `04-domains/`.
3. Work packages as execution records and provenance.
4. Draft papers as publication-development artifacts.
5. Archived or superseded material.

Conflicts are recorded and resolved by canonical ownership, never silently reconciled.

## Required discipline

- Preserve the distinction between observation, inference, retrodiction, and historical claim.
- Enforce no-smuggling and no-free-lunch.
- Apply symmetric methodological standards to DFM and competing programmes.
- Do not reject an auxiliary merely because it is auxiliary or arose during model development.
- Charge unconstrained chronology-protective accommodation as explanatory cost.
- Preserve unresolved accounting explicitly.
- Prefer primary sources for empirical claims. Never fabricate citations.
- Distinguish revealed commitments, empirical hypotheses, and methodological heuristics.
- Do not promote a domain hypothesis into the DFM hard core without an explicit canonical decision.
- Preserve work-package provenance during promotion.

## Writing conventions

Markdown is canonical. Do not use em dashes. Define variables. Use `retrodictive age` formally and `extrapolated age` where public-facing language benefits. Avoid `apparent age` as technical terminology.

## Change control

Substantive changes to the hard core, inferential rules, or repository boundaries require a decision-register entry and canonical update. Work packages may propose changes but are not canonical merely by containing them.
