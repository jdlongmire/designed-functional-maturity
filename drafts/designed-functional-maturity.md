# Designed Functional Maturity as Methodological Designism: An Explanatory Framework for Foundational Features of Reality

**Abstract**
Methodological naturalism, the restriction of scientific explanation to undirected natural causes, repeatedly encounters explanatory shortfalls when applied to the foundational structure of reality. These include the logical and informational character of the universe, low-entropy initial conditions, fine-tuning of physical constants, quantum measurement and selection, the origin of programmatic life, consciousness, mechanisms of macroevolution, unexpected biomolecular preservation, non-homogeneities in radiometric frameworks, and the unexpectedly mature high-redshift galaxies revealed by the James Webb Space Telescope. In contrast, a designed functional maturity (DFM) approach—illustrated by a systems reading of Genesis 1—treats these features as the expected outputs of intentional, sequential deployment of functionally mature systems. This paper argues that methodological designism provides greater explanatory coherence at the foundational level precisely where methodological naturalism leaves residual gaps or requires post-hoc accommodation. The claim is methodological rather than a demonstration of any particular historical chronology.

**1. Introduction**
Scientific inquiry operates under methodological constraints that shape what counts as a legitimate explanation. Methodological naturalism has long functioned as the default filter in the natural sciences: explanations are restricted to undirected physical processes, and agency or design is excluded a priori from the set of admissible causes. While this filter has proven productive for secondary, local processes, it generates persistent difficulties when applied to the boundary conditions and foundational features of a coherent, life-bearing, self-aware universe.

An alternative posture—methodological designism—begins with the expectation that certain classes of phenomena (information-rich systems, functional maturity, fine-tuned boundary conditions, subjective awareness) are the product of intentional specification. One concrete illustration of this posture appears in systems readings of Genesis 1 that treat the six-day sequence as an ordered deployment of functionally mature subsystems (the Designed Functional Maturity, or DFM, model). Each phase adds the content and commissioned functions required for subsequent operation, with maturity present from the moment of deployment rather than achieved through long antecedent natural runtimes.

This paper does not attempt to adjudicate the historical status of the Genesis narrative. It examines the comparative methodological adequacy of the two frameworks. The central claim is that designism absorbs a suite of well-documented features of reality with fewer residual explanatory deficits than strict naturalism. Each subsection of §3 below engages the strongest available naturalist response to the gap it identifies, not merely the weakest, so that the comparison in §4 is a comparison of live positions rather than of a straw opponent.

**2. The Designed Functional Maturity Framework**
The DFM reading interprets Genesis 1 as a six-phase systems deployment sequence. Pre-creation (S₀) is unactualized; successive days bring online the physical domain and temporal ordering (Day 1), environmental differentiation (Day 2), habitable substrate and primary producers (Day 3), astronomical regulators (Day 4), marine and aerial populations (Day 5), and terrestrial animals together with morally capable agents (Day 6). Day 7 marks the transition from generative deployment to ordinary providential operation.

*Forming–filling symmetry.* The six days exhibit a two-column structure long noted in Genesis commentary independent of any design-argument use of it (Wenham, 1987): Days 1–3 establish domains (light/darkness, sky/sea, land) and Days 4–6 populate each domain with its corresponding occupants in the same order (luminaries for the light/darkness domain, sea and air creatures for sky/sea, land animals and humans for land). On a systems reading, this is not merely a literary device but a dependency architecture: each "filling" phase presupposes the "forming" phase that defines the domain it operates in, and the text presents both halves as instantaneously functional rather than as the terminus of a developmental process internal to the domain.

*Internal dependencies.* The sequence carries functional dependencies that are load-bearing for the DFM reading regardless of the domains'/fillings' temporal spacing. Day 3 vegetation is introduced as "seed-bearing" and immediately fruit-producing — a functionally mature reproductive state — prior to the Day 4 introduction of the sun as the regular light-source regulator, which on a strict material-process reading would be the resource photosynthetic life depends on to originate and sustain itself. A systems account treats this ordering as consistent with deployment of a functioning subsystem (vegetation, already fruiting) ahead of the regulator subsystem (solar cycle) that will govern its *ongoing*, not its *originating*, operation — i.e., the text distinguishes what a system needs to run continuously from what it needed to be instantiated in the first place. Day 6 similarly deploys land animals and humans together, with humans immediately assigned dominion and a food-provision mandate (Genesis 1:28–30) that presupposes an already-functioning ecological structure (plants bearing seed, trees bearing fruit) rather than a food web still assembling itself. In each case the claim is architectural: the deployed state includes what is required to perform the system's commissioned function immediately, not a precondition for that function to develop later.

Key methodological commitments of the model include:
- Each phase supplies what is required for its commissioned functions in a state already capable of performing them.
- Apparent maturity does not presuppose long developmental histories.
- The sequence exhibits internal logical dependencies and a forming–filling symmetry (Days 1–3 / 4–6).

These commitments treat functional completeness and informational specification as design parameters rather than as outcomes that must be generated by undirected processes.

**3. Persistent Explanatory Shortfalls of Methodological Naturalism**

**3.1 Logic, Information, and Actualizability**
Reality is amenable to coherent logical and mathematical description, and abstract formalisms map effectively onto concrete physical processes (Wigner, 1960). Information is not a free-floating natural substance; every known instance requires a substrate, encoding conventions, and prior specification (Landauer, 1996; Wheeler, 1990; Shannon, 1948 provides the founding formalism, which itself presupposes a shared code between source and receiver rather than deriving the need for one). Naturalism can describe the mapping but offers no internal account of why reality is logical, informational, and actualizable in the first place.

The strongest naturalist replies do not deny the puzzle so much as relocate or dissolve it by revising the premise. Smolin (2015) denies that mathematics' effectiveness is *unreasonable*: it works well only for isolated, low-complexity, short-timescale subsystems — the domain physics historically selected for study — so the fit is explainable as an ordinary product of inquiry tracking locally available regularities, without further metaphysical grounding. Tegmark (2007) goes further, identifying physical and mathematical existence outright (the Mathematical Universe Hypothesis): under this view nothing is being "mapped" onto anything else, because the universe simply *is* a mathematical structure, and the correspondence becomes tautological rather than mysterious. Neither move eliminates the residual gap; each relocates it. Smolin's narrower domain still needs explaining within its own bounds. Tegmark's identification purchases dissolution of Wigner's puzzle only by adopting a stronger, independently controversial ontological commitment — that mathematical existence is physical existence — which carries its own unresolved costs (the measure problem chief among them) and is not obviously cheaper than positing intentional specification.

**3.2 Low-Entropy Initial Conditions**
The early universe possessed extraordinarily low entropy (Penrose, 1979, 2004). Undirected dynamical accounts typically relocate the problem: Penrose's own analysis (1989) argues inflationary scenarios do not solve the initial-entropy problem but require an even lower-entropy starting condition to get inflation going in the first place. Albert (2000) treats the low-entropy boundary condition as requiring a further postulate beyond dynamics and statistical indifference — the "Past Hypothesis" — that is not itself derived from more basic principles.

The strongest naturalist reply denies the demand for a dynamical derivation is coherent at all. Callender (2004) argues boundary conditions are not the kind of fact physics explains dynamically — every dynamical theory needs *some* initial specification, and singling out entropy's initial value for special explanatory burden begs the question about what needs explaining; on his view we should simply posit the low-entropy state and stop looking for a further account. This is a genuine, non-evasive philosophical move, but it reframes rather than resolves: it dissolves the puzzle only by revising what counts as needing explanation, not by deriving the Past Hypothesis from anything more basic. Ainsworth (2008) shows the more concessive alternative — reconstructing inflation as a genuinely explanatory *global* condition — does not succeed on the standard inflationary story, implicitly conceding Penrose's and Callender's shared point that the mainstream cosmological account does not supply the missing derivation either way.

**3.3 Fine-Tuning**
Life-permitting physics is highly sensitive to the values of fundamental constants and initial conditions (Barrow and Tipler, 1986; Rees, 1999; Barnes, 2012). Anthropic selection within a multiverse or claims of metaphysical necessity shift rather than dissolve the explanatory burden and remain currently untestable.

Two distinct, serious naturalist replies exist. The multiverse/landscape response (Susskind, 2005) proposes string theory's vast number of possible vacuum states populate an actualized "megaverse," so life-permitting regions are simply an actualized subset rather than a designed one — but this trades "why these constants?" for "why this landscape-generating mechanism, and why does it actualize enough domains?", a mechanism that is itself unconfirmed and largely untestable (Collins, 2009; Barnes, 2012). The second, arguably stronger reply disputes the premise directly: Stenger (2011, 2012) argues the standard fine-tuning claims rest on an unjustified probability measure over the constants and that life-permitting ranges are wider than typically presented, which — if correct — would dissolve rather than relocate the burden for at least some instances. Barnes's direct technical reply (2012) argues this critique does not survive scrutiny for the strongest fine-tuning cases (notably the cosmological constant). Both lines remain live, contested exchanges rather than settled defeaters of the fine-tuning observation itself.

**3.4 Quantum Measurement and Selection**
The measurement problem and the emergence of definite classical outcomes sit awkwardly inside purely unitary, observer-independent dynamics (Maudlin, 1995, formulates the canonical trilemma: wavefunction completeness, linear/unitary dynamics, and determinate outcomes are jointly inconsistent). Explicit-collapse proposals such as GRW (Ghirardi, Rimini and Weber, 1986) add new, non-unitary physics precisely to secure definite outcomes — direct evidence that "selection" is treated in parts of the literature as requiring resources beyond the bare unitary ontology. Decoherence (Schlosshauer, 2004) explains the *appearance* of classicality by suppressing interference between branches but explicitly does not by itself deliver a single definite outcome.

The strongest reply is Everettian: on the many-worlds interpretation there is no collapse and nothing is added to the dynamics — the universal wavefunction evolves purely unitarily, decoherence picks out a quasi-classical preferred basis (Wallace, 2002), and all outcomes are realized as branches, so "selection" is an indexical fact about which branch an observer-descendant occupies rather than a physical event needing new resources. Wallace further argues the observed Born-rule probabilities can be derived from decision-theoretic rationality axioms rather than posited as extra physics. This genuinely dissolves the *collapse* half of the problem, but it relocates the "selection" burden onto two contested fronts that remain unsettled in the literature: the preferred-basis/branch-counting problem (why decoherence-defined branches are the physically relevant carving), and the meaning of probability in a theory where every outcome occurs with certainty — critics including Albert, Price, and Maudlin argue the decision-theoretic Born-rule derivation covertly imports an epistemic indifference principle that does the real explanatory work. Whether this counts as solving selection or moving the unexplained resource from physics to a contested epistemic assumption is precisely the point still under dispute.

**3.5 Initial Conditions for Programmatic Life**
The origin of systems capable of storing, replicating, and executing functional information under realistic prebiotic conditions lacks a detailed, empirically grounded undirected pathway. Koonin (2007) calculates the combinatorial improbability of a chance-origin coupled replication-translation system at roughly 10⁻¹⁰¹⁸ and resorts to an eternal-inflation multiverse to absorb that improbability — itself an indication that the single-universe undirected pathway is not empirically accounted for. Orgel (2004) catalogues unresolved problems in nucleotide synthesis, polymerization, and RNA stability confronting the RNA-world hypothesis specifically.

The strongest current reply is not a completed pathway but real, peer-reviewed progress on discrete subsystems. Sutherland's group (Patel et al., 2015) showed that ribonucleotide, amino acid, and lipid precursors can all be derived from a shared prebiotic feedstock chemistry, answering the "three separate miracles" objection with one coherent reaction network rather than three unrelated ones; Joyce and Szostak (2018) demonstrate nonenzymatic RNA copying inside self-replicating protocells, addressing the compartmentalization/replication coupling problem. These are genuine advances, not merely asserted possibilities. But the field's own recent synthesis (a 2026 review in *Life*, DOI 10.3390/life16020240) states that even this chemistry "does not narrow the gap between prebiotic chemistry and biochemistry" across substrates, pathways, catalysts, and energy coupling. The honest characterization, on the research community's own terms, is significant subsystem-level progress without a demonstrated end-to-end pathway — which is what this subsection claims, not a claim that the field is static.

**3.6 Consciousness**
Neural correlates and functional architectures can be mapped with increasing precision (Tononi et al., 2016), yet the explanatory gap between third-person physical description and first-person phenomenology persists (Nagel, 1974; Levine, 1983, coins "the explanatory gap" specifically; Chalmers, 1995, formalizes the hard/easy problem distinction). Physicalist strategies either change the subject, stipulate emergence, or deny the data.

The strongest physicalist reply is illusionism (Frankish, 2016, the field's own consolidated statement, with Dennett as its principal earlier defender): phenomenal consciousness is treated as an illusion, and the explanatory task becomes explaining why it *seems* to exist rather than explaining phenomenality itself — the hard problem is replaced by the (tractable) illusion problem. Illusionists present this as taking consciousness more seriously than realist accounts, because it aims to explain the generating mechanism rather than positing an unexplained residue. Chalmers's rejoinder is a genuine dilemma, not a dismissal: either the phenomenal concepts illusionists use to explain the illusion are themselves physically explicable, in which case the account is circular, or they are not, in which case a new explanatory gap reopens one level up. Chalmers has called illusionism coherent but, in his judgment, empirically false — itself a marker that this exchange remains contested rather than settled in physicalism's favor.

**3.7 Mechanisms of Macroevolution**
Microevolutionary processes are well-documented. The origin of major morphological novelty, body-plan innovation, and coordinated higher-level transitions remains incompletely specified. The fossil pattern of relatively abrupt appearance followed by stasis (Eldredge and Gould, 1972) continues to pressure gradualist extrapolation, and mainstream paleontologists — not only design proponents — treat the Cambrian event's developmental-genetic origins as an open problem (Erwin and Valentine, 2013). Wagner (2014) argues from within evo-devo theory that the origin of novelty requires a distinct research program (Character Identity Networks) rather than standard adaptationist extrapolation, evidence that the gap is acknowledged inside the mainstream framework, not only asserted from outside it.

The strongest reply is mechanistic rather than merely promissory: Erwin and Davidson (2009) and Erwin (2020) argue body-plan novelty arises from reorganization of hierarchical developmental gene regulatory networks — co-option, duplication, and rewiring of conserved "kernel" toolkit genes into new spatial and temporal deployments — giving a molecular account of how modest regulatory change could produce large phenotypic effects, directly addressing the "microevolution plus vast time" gap rather than merely asserting more time suffices. This is an actively developed, comparative-genomics-grounded framework, not speculation. But its own principal architects (Erwin and Valentine, 2013) describe the actual assembly of the Cambrian body plans and their specific GRN kernels as unresolved: the framework explains how such change *could* work, not a reconstructed stepwise causal pathway from ancestral lineages to the derived phyla that has actually been traced. Both the design-side reading (Meyer, 2013, engaging this same literature at length) and the mainstream reading (Erwin, Wagner) draw on the identical evidentiary gap; they diverge on whether it is a principled absence or an ordinary open frontier being actively closed.

**3.8 Biomolecular Preservation**
Reports of flexible vessels, osteocytes, collagen, and other original organic material in Cretaceous fossils (Schweitzer et al., 2005; Schweitzer et al., 2009, with mass-spectrometric sequence replication across multiple labs) were unexpected under standard deep-time degradation kinetics. An early sequence-identification claim (Asara et al., 2007) drew a published challenge disputing whether the recovered sequence was correctly identified as collagen rather than contamination (Buckley et al., 2008); the 2009 multi-lab replication addresses this more robustly than the 2007 paper alone.

The mainstream field's own accommodation is the iron-mediated crosslinking hypothesis (Schweitzer et al., 2014): iron released from degrading hemoglobin catalyzes Fenton-type free-radical crosslinking that acts like a natural tissue fixative, potentially extending preservation "far beyond current expectations," with the mechanism experimentally reproduced in modern chicken collagen within 48 hours (Boatman et al., 2019). An earlier alternative explanation — that the structures are modern bacterial biofilm, not original tissue (Kaye, Gaugler and Sawlowicz, 2008) — was directly tested and reported as morphologically, immunologically, and chemically distinct from lab-grown biofilm (Moyer, Zheng and Schweitzer, 2016). This is real, substantive scientific engagement, not silence. It is nonetheless fair to note two limits on how settled the accommodation is: most of the confirmatory work, including the original discoveries, the mechanism paper, and the principal biofilm rebuttal, traces to the same research group, and at least one independent actualistic study (Ullmann, Voegele and Lacovara, 2023) found iron-enriched conditions accelerated rather than prevented modern collagen degradation under its own experimental parameters. A 2023 synthesis (Anderson, 2023) treats iron-crosslinking and a competing glycation-based hypothesis as sequential steps of one mechanism rather than settled rivals — indicating active theoretical consolidation, not closure. The accommodation is a genuine, partially independently-supported research program; it is not yet a broadly validated resolution of why deep-time degradation kinetics failed to anticipate the observations in the first place.

**3.9 Non-Homogeneity of Radiometric Frameworks**
Distinct isotopic systems rest on assumptions (closed-system behavior, initial conditions, constant decay rates) that are not directly observable for any given sample and are instead checked indirectly. Genuine, peer-reviewed discordance is documented: historic (known-age) lava flows have yielded false K-Ar/Ar-Ar ages, in some cases up to roughly a million years on rock less than two centuries old, from excess argon inherited from magmatic or mantle sources (Dalrymple, 1969; Krummenacher, 1970; mechanism further characterized in Kelley, 2002). U-Pb systems show comparable discordance from Pb-loss under metamorphism (Mezger and Krogstad, 1997).

The mainstream response is not to deny these anomalies but to point to diagnostics built into the method itself. The dual U-Pb decay chains (²³⁸U→²⁰⁶Pb and ²³⁵U→²⁰⁷Pb) give an internal concordance check — discordance is detected by the method's own structure, not discovered externally and then explained away (Wetherill, 1956). Radiocarbon calibration is cross-validated against independent chronometers (dendrochronology, varves, speleothems, corals) that converge on the same calendar dates (Reimer et al., 2020), which is the strongest empirical answer to a charge that calibration is unfalsifiable. Kelley's (2002) synthesis treats excess argon as a modeled, diagnostically-flagged artifact rather than a suppressed anomaly, and Vermeesch (2021) shows the community's treatment of discordant data is an active, self-critical statistical literature rather than a fixed rescue apparatus. This is a fair point that the earlier draft of this subsection did not concede: describing the framework's handling of discordance as "special pleading" overstates the case against a practice that is designed-in, published, and predictive rather than post hoc. The residual and more defensible claim is narrower — that interpreting an anomalous result still requires auxiliary geological judgment (is this excess argon, disturbed Pb-loss, or a genuinely young age?) that is not fully theory-independent, a limitation true of any empirical dating framework and not unique to radiometric methods, but real nonetheless. It is this narrower, methodologically-honest claim the paper relies on, not the stronger "special pleading" framing.

**3.10 Unexpectedly Mature High-Redshift Galaxies (JWST)**
Early JWST releases revealed a population of massive, evolved, and in some cases chemically enriched galaxy candidates at redshifts corresponding to less than a few hundred million years after the conventional Big Bang (Naidu et al., 2022; Labbé et al., 2023, reporting six CEERS candidates at z=7.4–9.1, one with a photometric stellar-mass estimate near 10¹¹ M☉). Boylan-Kolchin (2023) showed the most extreme candidates approached the baryonic mass ceiling of their host dark-matter halos under standard structure-formation timescales.

The strongest reply is a converging observational correction rather than a single mechanism. Spectroscopic follow-up confirmed several genuine z>11 galaxies while directly refuting at least one of the most extreme photometric candidates, a z≈16 object revised down to z=4.9 (Arrabal Haro et al., 2023). Adding mid-infrared JWST/MIRI photometry to spectral-energy-distribution fits systematically lowers stellar-mass estimates by roughly 0.4 dex on average and shows a substantial share of the reddest, apparently most-massive objects are active-galactic-nucleus-contaminated "little red dots" rather than pure evolved stellar populations, cutting massive-galaxy number densities by 36–55% (Wang et al., 2025). Top-heavy initial-mass-function and elevated star-formation-efficiency models close much of the remaining gap without new physics (Hutter et al., 2024/2025). This meaningfully walks back the most extreme early claims. It does not fully eliminate the excess: a genuine tension between the observed abundance of luminous, apparently evolved galaxies at z>10 and pre-Webb expectations persists in the astrophysics literature as of the most recent published follow-up, best characterized as an unresolved tension in galaxy-formation modeling rather than either a debunked artifact or an unaddressed crisis for the standard cosmological timescale.

Collectively, these ten features exhibit a recurring pattern, now traceable through the primary literature rather than merely asserted: data or theoretical difficulties arrive as genuine problems for undirected accounts, and the strongest available naturalist responses are substantive, peer-reviewed, and often mechanistically real — but in nine of ten cases (§3.9 being the partial exception, where the naturalist diagnostic apparatus is itself principled) they relocate, defer, or partially close the gap rather than deriving the observed feature from more basic undirected principles. That distinction — real engagement that nonetheless leaves a residual, structurally similar gap one level down — is the actual comparative claim this paper is making, not a claim that naturalist researchers have failed to try.

**4. Methodological Designism and Explanatory Advantage**
A design-oriented methodology begins with the expectation of intentional specification of information-rich, functionally mature systems. Section 3 identified, for each domain, a specific point at which the strongest naturalist reply relocates the explanatory burden rather than deriving it from more basic undirected principles. Methodological designism's advantage is that it absorbs each of those specific relocation points as an expected feature of intentional deployment rather than as a further problem requiring its own accommodation:

- Where naturalism relocates the "why is reality mathematical/informational" puzzle to a costlier ontological commitment (§3.1, Tegmark's MUH) or a narrowed domain still needing its own account (Smolin), design treats logic and information as anticipated outputs of a rational agent — the relocation is unnecessary because the mapping is expected from the start, not a residue to be explained away.
- Where naturalism treats the low-entropy Past Hypothesis as a further, undischarged postulate (§3.2) or dissolves the demand for explanation by definitional fiat (Callender), design treats the boundary condition itself as a specified parameter — the same move Callender makes to stop the regress, but without requiring the further, contestable claim that boundary conditions are in-principle inexplicable.
- Where multiverse and probability-measure responses to fine-tuning (§3.3) trade one untestable mechanism for another, design treats the constants as design parameters directly, without needing an unconfirmed landscape-generating mechanism to do the work.
- Where Everettian quantum mechanics dissolves collapse but reopens the selection problem at the level of branch-counting and probability (§3.4), design treats definite outcomes and rational evaluative capacity as deliberately instantiated rather than as an emergent byproduct of a contested decision-theoretic derivation.
- Where prebiotic chemistry demonstrates real subsystem-level progress without a demonstrated end-to-end pathway (§3.5), and where GRN/evo-devo mechanisms explain how macroevolutionary novelty *could* arise without a traced stepwise pathway to the Cambrian phyla (§3.7), design treats programmatic life and large-scale biological organization as deployed already-functional, removing the need for either pathway to be independently demonstrated.
- Where iron-crosslinking chemistry is a genuine but only partially validated accommodation for biomolecular survival (§3.8), and radiometric discordance is handled by a principled but judgment-dependent diagnostic apparatus (§3.9), design is compatible with these systems never having been required to endure the full span of conventional deep time — the anomalies are less surprising if the "expected" timescale itself is the contested premise.
- Where early massive galaxies remain a live, only partially resolved tension in galaxy-formation modeling after real observational correction (§3.10), design treats early cosmic structural maturity as the expected signature of functional completeness at deployment, rather than requiring hierarchical assembly from minimal seeds to be independently modeled into agreement with the data.

The DFM systems reading of Genesis 1 supplies one concrete illustration of this methodological stance: sequential deployment of subsystems already competent for their commissioned roles, developed in §2 above through the forming–filling architecture and the internal functional dependencies of the Day 3/Day 4 and Day 6 sequences. The broader designist posture does not depend on that specific narrative; it depends on the comparative adequacy, demonstrated case-by-case in §3, of starting commitments that treat specification and maturity as primary rather than as late emergent accidents requiring successive relocation.

**5. Scope and Limitations**
Methodological designism does not automatically validate any particular theological claim, young-earth chronology, or six-day historical sequence. It remains responsible for engaging the full range of empirical observations, including those that appear to support conventional deep-time frameworks. Design explanations must avoid ad-hoc "designer-of-the-gaps" moves and must specify, where possible, the expected empirical signatures of intentional deployment. The comparative analysis in §3 is offered in that spirit: several of the naturalist responses surveyed there are genuine, substantive, and in the case of §3.9's diagnostic architecture, more principled than the paper's own earlier draft credited them with being. Acknowledging that is a condition of the paper's own methodological credibility, not a concession that undermines its thesis.

Conversely, methodological naturalism is not a neutral default. It is a restrictive filter that systematically under-explains, or explains only by successive relocation, the very features that make a coherent, investigable, life-bearing, and conscious universe possible. Both frameworks carry metaphysical commitments. The relevant question is which set of commitments leaves fewer foundational facts as unexplained primitives or repeated retrofits.

**6. Conclusion**
The cumulative weight of the phenomena reviewed—logical and informational structure, low-entropy and fine-tuned initial conditions, quantum measurement, programmatic life, consciousness, macroevolutionary mechanisms, biomolecular preservation, radiometric non-homogeneity, and the unexpectedly mature high-redshift galaxies observed by JWST—reveals a systematic pattern in strict methodological naturalism: its strongest available responses are real and often substantive, yet in the large majority of cases examined they relocate or partially close the explanatory gap rather than deriving the observed feature from more basic undirected principles. A designed functional maturity approach, operating under methodological designism, absorbs the same phenomena as the expected results of intentional systems deployment, without requiring the successive relocation documented case-by-case in §3. At the level of foundational adequacy, designism is more explanatory precisely where naturalism's own best replies still leave residue. Further progress requires explicit comparison of the residual explanatory burdens each methodology continues to carry, engaged at the level of specific primary sources rather than general characterization.
