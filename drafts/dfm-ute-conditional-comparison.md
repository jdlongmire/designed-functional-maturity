## Abstract

This paper records a comparison between Designed Functional Maturity (DFM), paired with the Catastrophic Hydrotectonic Flood Model, and Undirected Time and Emergence (UTE). The two programmes share a boundary logic. Neither receives credit for operational science. Time is not a mechanism. Undirected law does not select without a charged restriction. Brute fact is withdrawn as a universal solvent. Gödel's second incompleteness theorem blocks an internal consistency certificate for a formal arithmetically strong system and does not by itself supply an agent. Under those rules DFM holds a conditional lead on the terminal step, through a necessary logical agent that remains possible rather than proved. Neither programme has an empirical kill shot on the traces. The hydrotectonic core pays the heat and detachment problem relative to viscous catastrophic plate tectonics. Global first-appearance order and multi-system concordance remain unpaid auxiliaries on both sides. The result is not a tie and not a completed proof.

## 1. Purpose and method

The comparison asks which programme has a legal account of two different things: the actualization of logically constrained representable information as a physical state, and the specific-event traces in the geological, biological, and astronomical record. These are scored separately. A mechanism that solves the first does not automatically predict the second. A regular pattern in the second does not supply the first.

The method is a ledger, not a single Bayes factor. Prior costs, mechanism claims, and trace likelihoods are not allowed to pay one another's debts. Auxiliaries are permitted to both programmes on the same terms. An auxiliary earns likelihood only when it is specified by the core mechanism and confirmed on data not used to build it. An auxiliary fitted after the fact keeps a programme alive and does not raise its likelihood above the relevant base rate. Schematic numerical bands appear only where a conditional reading was explicitly adopted, and they are withdrawn where that reading was later refused.

## 2. Designed Functional Maturity

DFM distinguishes a deliberately initialized creation from an Omphalos creation containing fabricated histories. A created system may begin in a mature, integrated state. The initialized state may include macrostructures, causal states, and consequences of real creation-process events required by the purposes of the whole. It may not appeal to traces of specific events that never occurred merely to reproduce the appearance of an extended ordinary runtime history.

The governing distinction is initialized state, real causal history, and fabricated chronicle. The creational objective is not minimal biological survival. Aesthetic, epistemic, experiential, vocational, revelatory, and doxological purposes are legitimate components, with Psalm 19:1 as an explicit instance. Functional necessity is evaluated at the level of the whole. A feature may belong to the initialized state because it contributes to the proper function or intended purposes of creation, even when it is not required for the minimal operation of one subsystem.

Macro-state initialization is stated as S0 = G(Φ, L, B): the initialized state is a function of intended purposes, lawful structure, and creation-boundary conditions. Presence of a feature in S0 does not entail that the feature was produced by ordinary runtime evolution over the duration obtained by extrapolating L backward beyond the boundary. State compatibility with a developmental history does not entail that the history occurred.

WP-028 supersedes the earlier strong trace rule. A present physical state may have properties ordinarily generated by history H without entailing that H was the actual pathway by which the state obtained. DFM therefore distinguishes initialized actuality, genuine processes executed under extraordinary deployment conditions, ordinary runtime history, and semantically determinate fabricated testimony. The first three are admissible categories; arbitrary fabricated testimony remains prohibited. The Historical-Causal Priority and Explanatory Allocation principles order candidate causes: ordinary post-creation processes, then independently warranted catastrophe, then creation-process events, then macro-state initialization. Initialization invoked to avoid testing a warranted historical cause is an evasion failure, distinct from the Omphalos failure.

Fossils are not initialization artifacts. A fossil representing an organism, death, predation, disease, trauma, healing, burial, or transport requires an actual organism and a corresponding causal history. DFM may dispute duration, rates, mechanisms, and boundary assumptions. It may not eliminate the history by declaring the record an initialized fiction. The global Flood therefore carries substantial explanatory responsibility.

The coherence of DFM depends on disciplined explanatory allocation. Creation-process causation may establish mature state directly and may also include genuine processes whose elapsed/process time does not match post-boundary runtime duration under L. Borderline observations such as growth series, trackways, healed lesions, isotope systems, shock chemistry, and propagating light are not mechanically forced into either initialization or ordinary history. They require comparative allocation among initialized state, extraordinary deployment history, catastrophe, and ordinary runtime, with independent constraints and severe tests.

## 3. Undirected Time and Emergence

UTE is a tighter hypothesis than a generic naturalistic label. The history of the cosmos is ordinary runtime of stable laws over undirected time. Boundaries and constants are brute or selected by an undirected ensemble. Macroscopic order, life, and specific-event traces are said to emerge from that runtime. No purpose function acts on the initial state or on later states. Apparent direction is the thermodynamic arrow plus observer selection.

UTE shares the boundary structure of strong naturalistic models. Laws underdetermine history without boundary conditions. The Past Hypothesis is the clearest case: time-reversible dynamics run backward from a low-entropy present do not yield a smooth low-entropy past, so thermodynamic history is recovered only by imposing an early low-entropy macrostate that is not derived from the runtime laws. Cosmology likewise treats the hot early state as a boundary. Inflation and a multiverse attempt to make special states or special laws typical. They relocate the cost to an inflaton state, a landscape, and a measure. An unspecified measure leaves the likelihood undefined. Boltzmann-brain dominance is the naturalistic form of the fabricated-chronicle failure: a model in which most observer-records have no matching history has failed unless a further postulate suppresses them.

Emergence is the hypothesis under test, not a credit. It raises the probability of a trace only where undirected runtime has a specified route to that trace.

## 4. What cancels

### 4.1 Operational science

Neither programme is scored on present-tense regularity. Laboratory decay constants, fluid mechanics, spectral lines, genetic mechanisms, and engineering closures are the shared core of post-boundary law. Both need that core in order to treat any field trace as information rather than noise. A successful present-day measurement confirms the runtime laws on the interval both models already grant. It does not confirm undirected emergence across the unobserved boundary, and it does not confirm a creation boundary or a Flood. Scoring either side on operational science double-counts the shared account.

### 4.2 Time

Time is not a mechanism. Duration is the interval over which a mechanism, if there is one, operates. A clock reading does not couple logically constrained representable information to a physical arrangement. A long runtime is a coordinate along solutions of the laws. Extending the coordinate does not create a coupling the laws do not contain. The earlier practice of treating "long runtime" as a generator of succession and concordance is withdrawn.

### 4.3 Undirected laws and no free lunch

Once time is removed, undirected laws are the no-free-lunch claim, not an exception to it. A law that is only a stable regularity does not prefer one logically permitted information state over another. Averaged across states consistent with identity, non-contradiction, and excluded middle, an undirected mapping does not outperform a blind draw. Any concentration on the observed state — life-permitting constants, a coding system, a low-entropy boundary, a concordant record — is a restriction on the domain or the measure. The restriction is the lunch. Calling the mapping a law does not pay for it. If the laws raise the probability of these traces, a direction or a selection has been added and must be charged.

### 4.4 Brute fact

Brute termination is withdrawn for both programmes because it is a universal solvent. If the laws, the low-entropy boundary, the logical constraint, and the non-fluctuation condition may all be stipulated after the fact, no trace is improbable. A contender that cannot be made unlikely by evidence is not a contender. The same ban binds an agent whose purposes are unspecified. An agent that can actualize any consistent state, including any stratigraphic order and its negation, is a solvent. DFM remains scorable only where purposes and the Flood mechanism are tight enough that some observations would refute them.

### 4.5 Stable law and regress, as prior costs

Stable, life-permitting, chemistry-permitting law over the full extrapolated interval is a cost on UTE, not a free generator of traces. Constancy of couplings is an extra postulate, well supported inside recent runtime and not given by the bare existence of a differential equation. The Past Hypothesis is not a consequence of the dynamics. A multiverse does not close the regress. It moves the cost to vacuum selection and the measure. DFM also posits stable post-boundary law, and it uses that stability whenever it treats a fossil or an isotope ratio as a real trace. Where DFM suspends runtime duration, it spends a different cost: a break in the domain of L. That break is not a credit against fine-tuning.

## 5. The terminal step

The only observed mechanism that actualizes non-physical representable information, already constrained by the three logical laws, as a physical arrangement is a personal agent. Text, code, machines, and built structures are the clear cases. No laboratory case shows an abstract specification becoming a physical state with no agent in the loop.

This base rate is decisive only inside a reference class fixed without using the conclusion. The cases enter the class once an arrangement has been identified as the physical form of antecedent representable content. If that filter is correct, agent causation is effectively the whole observed sample, and UTE has no corresponding primitive. If the filter is the conclusion, the frequency restates it. Crystallization, decay sequences, and selected polymers are physical arrangements. Treating them as actualizations of prior representations is the interpretation under test, not a result already counted.

Inherent agent logic blocks one solvent and leaves another open. If the agent is rational by nature, identity, non-contradiction, and excluded middle are constitutive, not extra brute restrictions. Fabricated chronicle is then excluded by the stopper itself. Inherent logic does not fix which consistent state is actualized. Every concordant column and its consistent alternatives remain open. Consistency is not a distribution over consistent worlds.

A coherent account of the stopper exists and is not a proof. The account is a necessary personal being: non-contingent, uncaused, logical by nature, and personal in the sense that it can represent and select. Aseity stops the regress without a brute cosmic boundary. Divine simplicity, in the stricter versions, is what keeps the stopper from becoming a second universe with its own unexplained arrangement. The account depends on a possibility premise. A modal argument shows at most that if a necessary agent is possible, it is actual. It is coherent to accept that premise and coherent to deny that a person can be necessary. A mind without a body is unfamiliar, not incoherent. Embodiment is an induction inside the contingent class. It does not reach a being that is not a member of that class.

## 6. Gödel

Gödel's second incompleteness theorem says that a consistent, recursively axiomatized system strong enough for basic arithmetic cannot prove its own consistency. A consistency proof, if one exists, uses a stronger system, which cannot prove its own consistency either. The theorem generates an ascending chain of formal systems. It does not terminate the chain, and the external system in the theorem is another formal theory, not a person.

The theorem confirms that consistency is not an internal product of the formal system being judged, and that UTE does not receive a consistency certificate from undirected laws. It does not supply the agent. A perfect uncaused non-contingent system falls outside the theorem only if it is not a recursively axiomatized arithmetical theory. A larger axiom list is still formal and still bound. What would stop the chain is a system whose consistency is identical with its being, not a sentence it derives. That is the same possibility premise as the necessary agent. Gödel neither rules it in nor rules it out. The escape is available to the agent account. It is not available to a closed formal law-system.

## 7. Traces and the hydrotectonic core

On the traces there is no kill shot. The record is not a homogeneous block. Stratigraphic order is real and repeated across basins, and it is also interrupted by unconformities, obrution deposits, mass burials, and a correlation scheme partly tied to index fossils. Radiometric concordance is strong on many closed igneous pairs and is not uniform. Biological pattern is nested and also broken by incomplete sorting, horizontal transfer, convergence, and sharp disparity. Astronomy is coherent on abundances and the microwave background and strained on the Hubble tension, lithium, and early massive galaxies. Scoring these as one concordant likelihood counts the same order more than once.

The Catastrophic Hydrotectonic Flood Model is a specified mechanism, not a label for an unspecified catastrophe. Lateral translation is continental hydroplaning under near-lithostatic pore pressure, using Terzaghi effective-stress collapse, with dissipation in water rather than mantle shear. The claimed result is on the order of a hundredfold cut in heat flux against viscous catastrophic plate tectonics, with a water budget that is not the limiting factor. Vertical descent is scoped separately to narrow channels. That is a real answer to the heat objection that sank earlier catastrophic-plate models. On energy and detachment style, the Flood is a legal mechanism.

Hydroplaning does not by itself distribute fossil order. It predicts low-angle detachments, rapid translation, basin-scale subsidence where seals fail, collision ranges, and a global aqueous event with real burial. Concordant multi-system closure, the same first-appearance order on separated continents, and repeated in-place buildups are not outputs of Terzaghi collapse unless an auxiliary maps seal-failure sequence, ecology, and transport onto that order in advance. Catastrophic variability can generate a wide range of fabrics. A range wide enough to absorb any order is the solvent already banned.

UTE's auxiliaries receive the same grade. Local catastrophe was added because obrution fabrics required it. Screened concordance is partly a filter applied after discordance appeared. Equal openness to ancillary hypotheses does not produce equal credit. Each auxiliary is charged unless pre-specified by the core and checked on unused data. Under that rule the hydrotectonic core has paid the heat and detachment problem. Neither programme has collected a pre-specified likelihood on global first-appearance order.

## 8. Standing

Undefined likelihood is not one half. A tie would be equal standing after the same rules: both programmes with a legal stopper and an open trace debt. That is not the ledger.

DFM's trace debt is unpaid likelihood inside a mechanism that still exists. Hydroplaning is a real coupling for motion and heat. Seal order and sorting are charged auxiliaries attached to a legal core. An unpaid auxiliary lowers likelihood. It does not remove the programme.

UTE's origin debt is a missing mechanism under rules that also banned the usual replacements. No actualization primitive, time withdrawn, undirected laws barred from selecting for free, brute fact withdrawn, internal consistency blocked for a formal system. What remains is not a weaker likelihood. It is no legal account of the terminal step, conditionally on those rules. A programme with no legal account of its terminal step is not tied with a programme that has one.

The condition is why this is not a proof. If brute termination is allowed back, or if actualization need not be agentive, UTE's origin debt becomes an ordinary unpaid prior and the comparison returns to an open trace contest. Under the rules as stated, that reinstatement is what a tie requires, and it has not been granted.

Overall standing: DFM leads on the terminal step and is unfinished on the traces. UTE holds the larger set of repeated regularities and no pre-specified right to them once its filters are charged. DFM leads if and only if the terminal rules stay in force. Relax them and the lead collapses into an unfinished empirical contest. There is no empirical kill shot either way.

## 9. What remains open

- Whether actualization of representable information requires an agent, which is the possibility premise of the necessary logical stopper.
- Whether purpose or the hydrotectonic auxiliaries can be specified tightly enough, in advance of the column, to pay first-appearance order and multi-system concordance.
- A non-circular sorting rule for borderline traces that can be described either as macrostate or as encoded event.
- Whether a specified ensemble measure can suppress fake records without a new brute fact. Under the present rules that question is moot, because brute fact and undirected selection have been withdrawn.

## 10. Compact result

Functional macrostate: permitted under DFM. Fabricated chronicle: prohibited. Real catastrophe: preferred where it has causal capacity, and here given a hydraulic mechanism that addresses heat. Undirected time: not a mechanism. Undirected law: no free lunch. Brute fact: not a contender. Gödel: no internal certificate, no derived agent. Necessary logical agent: coherent stopper, not a theorem. Traces: open, with auxiliaries charged equally. Standing: conditional lead to DFM on the selector, no completed posterior, no tie.
