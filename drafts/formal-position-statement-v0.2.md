# Formal Position Statement v0.2: Computational and Catastrophic Framework for Biblical Creation

**Status:** Revised working draft, 2026-10-25. **Author:** James D. Longmire.

## I. Authority and method
Scripture is the governing historical authority for the creation account. Physical observations constrain auxiliary models. Explicit textual commitments, interpretive inferences, and provisional physical conjectures must remain distinguishable.

## II. Creation chronology
The six literal terrestrial creation days constitute miraculous phased deployments. Day 1 establishes terrestrial light-dark chronology, while Day 4 commissions the luminaries. A creation-to-Fall interval of unspecified duration may permit biological diversification, whose scope must be demonstrated rather than assumed.

## III. Day 4 cosmology
The Day 4 miraculous deployment initiates the expansion that continues today. Cosmic processes may operate asynchronously relative to terrestrial chronology. By the end of Day 4 the cosmos achieves **normative stabilization**, entering a physically coherent regime of ordinary lawful evolution.

The **Terrestrial Centrality Hypothesis** proposes that Earth lies approximately at coincident centers of global spatial symmetry, matter distribution, and expansion structure. This is an inference from terrestrial observations and the creation deployment narrative, not an explicit scriptural statement or a result established by isotropic expansion. Homogeneous FLRW cosmology has no unique physical expansion center. A distinct center-bearing metric, allowable offset, and observational tests are required.

Initialized macrostates and genuinely executed deployment processes must be distinguished from fabricated chronicles. Event-specific traces require actual event provenance under the DFM Historical Integrity Constraint. Retrodictive age does not logically entail identical elapsed terrestrial history.

## IV. Hybrid cosmological residuals
The **Post-Deployment Cosmological Residuals** hypothesis explores (1) persistent structural properties, potentially relevant to gravitational and vacuum-energy phenomena; (2) transient contributions to expansion dynamics, potentially relevant to evolving dark energy and Hubble-parameter discrepancies; and (3) inherited mature structures, potentially relevant to early galaxies observed by JWST. These are unconfirmed assignments. Physical carriers, conservation laws, quantitative predictions, and independent tests are required.

## V. Global hydrotectonic history
The framework affirms a historical global Flood and investigates large-scale hydraulic and tectonic catastrophism as a possible explanation of major stratigraphic and fossil patterns. Required tests include energy and heat budgets, sediment provenance, tectonic mechanics, ecological sorting, fossil succession, and comparison with competing geological models. Unique-event assumptions alone do not establish explanatory success.

## VI. Evidence and objections
Cosmic redshifts, CMB structure, distant starlight, and early-galaxy observations must be quantitatively reproduced, including contingent event traces. Multi-system radiometric concordance requires a common independently constrained initialization explanation rather than target-age fitting. Fossil succession must be derived from specified diversification and depositional mechanisms. Soft tissue, trace radiocarbon, and helium findings require sample-specific provenance and alternative-cause evaluation. Cross-validation among relative and absolute methods is genuine evidence and cannot be dismissed merely because models use assumptions.

## VII. Falsification and programme status
TCH must be tested against CMB, kSZ, BAO, supernova distances, lensing, structure growth, and redshift drift. PDCR must specify a coherent Day 4 boundary state and forward equations that yield discriminatory predictions. Hydrotectonic models must satisfy conservation, mechanical, and stratigraphic constraints. Failed auxiliaries must be revised or rejected, not protected by unspecified miraculous exceptions.

## Conclusion
The framework proposes six miraculous terrestrial-day deployments, Day 4 initiation of continuing expansion, asynchronous cosmic development, normative stabilization, provisional terrestrial centrality, hybrid cosmological residuals, and global hydrotectonic catastrophism. It remains a developing research programme rather than a quantitatively validated cosmological or geological theory. A verified primary-source reference ledger is required before publication.
