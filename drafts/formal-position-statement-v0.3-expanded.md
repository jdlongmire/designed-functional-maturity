**Version:** 0.3, expanded working edition | **Date:** 25 October 2026

**Programme:** Designed Functional Maturity (DFM), within the Biblical World Model (BWM)

## Abstract
This statement articulates a Christian methodological-designist framework for historical creation and the interpretation of physical evidence. The canonical Scriptures supply its primary historical authority. Genesis 1 is understood to describe six literal terrestrial days of miraculous phased deployment. Day 1 establishes terrestrial illumination and the evening–morning cycle before the Day 4 commissioning of the Sun, Moon, and stars. The wider cosmos is proposed to have undergone asynchronous generative deployment on Day 4, initiating expansion that continues today and reaching normative physical stabilization by the end of that day. Terrestrial geometric centrality is advanced as an auxiliary inference from the Earth-referenced deployment and terrestrial observations, not as an explicit biblical assertion. A hybrid residual hypothesis investigates whether persistent, transient, and inherited properties of the stabilized state might contribute to phenomena modeled as dark matter, dark energy, expansion-rate discrepancies, and unexpectedly mature early galaxies. The framework also investigates biological diversification after creation and a global Flood with associated hydrotectonic catastrophism. Throughout, actual event provenance, physical coherence, independent constraints, and severe empirical testing govern explanatory claims.

## I. Epistemological and Material Authority

### I.1 Scriptural authority
The canonical Old and New Testaments constitute the primary material authority and infallible rule for historical origins within this framework. Scripture provides historical and theological commitments concerning creation, the Fall, and the Flood. The framework does not assume that every proposed physical implementation is itself revealed. An interpretation of a passage, an inference from observations, and a mathematical hypothesis are distinct claims with different evidentiary burdens.

### I.2 Observation, reconstruction, and provenance
Empirical measurements report physical states and relationships. Historical reconstructions combine those measurements with dynamical laws, initial conditions, and assumptions about earlier states. Such reconstructions can be highly corroborated and precise without becoming logically identical to a unique actual history under every conceivable boundary condition. DFM distinguishes historical elapsed duration from retrodictive duration. This distinction does not diminish the evidentiary force of consilient dating or cosmological measurements; it identifies the additional burden on an alternative initialization hypothesis.

### I.3 Special action and ordinary operation
Divine creation is affirmed as a real special causal act. After commissioning, created systems ordinarily operate through stable physical laws. A miraculous initial state must be coherent with subsequent operation; the framework does not allow arbitrary changes to established laws or measurements whenever a difficulty appears.

### I.4 Historical integrity
A foundational constraint prohibits fabricated chronicles of specific events that never occurred. A mature initial macrostate may be instantiated coherently, and genuine extraordinary deployment processes may produce real consequences. But contingent event traces, such as identifiable explosions, collisions, growth episodes, injuries, or sedimentary events, require genuine causal provenance. This distinction applies equally to astronomy, geology, and biology.

## II. Chronology and the Six Phased Deployments

### II.1 Literal terrestrial days
Genesis 1 is read as a sequential historical account of six literal terrestrial days. The evening–morning cadence defines Earth-referenced chronology. The framework's assertion of terrestrial days does not entail that every domain of creation accumulated identical process time during miraculous generative acts. It does entail a fixed terrestrial sequence with ordinary normative operation after each commissioned phase.

### II.2 Day 1 illumination and terrestrial time
Genesis 1:3–5 records the divine provision of light, its distinction from darkness, and the first evening–morning cycle. The framework interprets this as a real terrestrial illumination regime established before the ordinary luminaries were commissioned. Its source is understood as divine provision. The term *proto-eschatological* expresses an analogy with Revelation 21:23 and 22:5, where divine illumination is independent of the Sun; those passages do not specify the physical mechanism of Day 1 light.

### II.3 Functional provisioning
Earth is created with coherent life-supporting physical conditions. Its atmospheric, hydrological, chemical, thermal, and radiogenic inventories must be mutually compatible. A calibrated radiogenic heat budget is an investigable candidate constraint, but it does not independently explain the detailed structure of every isotopic system.

### II.4 Creation-to-Fall interval
Scripture does not supply a precise duration between the completion of creation and the Fall. The framework therefore leaves this interval temporally open and investigates the possibility of ordinary biological diversification and speciation. No numerical duration or morphological radiation rate is asserted in advance. Fossils bearing death, injury, predation, or other event-specific traces must be located in an actual historical interval compatible with the programme's biblical commitments; they cannot simply be assigned to an unspecified pre-Fall process. The relationship among the Fall, the fossil record, and the Flood remains an explicit research obligation.

## III. Day 4 Cosmological Deployment and Expansion

### IV.1 Initiation of continuing expansion
The Day 4 deployment is hypothesized to initiate the physical expansion observed today. It is not merely the placement of already-expanding astronomical bodies into a preexisting ordinary cosmic history. The miraculous act establishes the spacetime geometry, matter-energy fields, expansion dynamics, and functional astronomical architecture.

### IV.2 Asynchronous generative runtime
During the terrestrial Day 4 interval, genuine cosmic generative processes may unfold asynchronously relative to Earth's clock. This may include directly instantiated coherent macrostates and actually executed processes whose physical outcomes are integrated at commissioning. The mechanism relating cosmic process duration to terrestrial elapsed duration remains unspecified and must not be confused with an arbitrary coordinate transformation.

### IV.3 Normative stabilization
At the end of Day 4, extraordinary generative deployment terminates and the cosmic state enters a self-consistent normative dynamical regime. Expansion continues; galaxies, stars, radiation, and fields may evolve further. Stabilization requires appropriate gravitational constraint equations, conservation laws, and physically admissible initial data. It does not require the universe to become static.

### IV.4 The Big Bang and expansion
The observational basis for cosmic expansion is accepted. Standard hot Big Bang cosmology is a broader model encompassing expansion, thermal evolution, primordial nucleosynthesis, the cosmic microwave background, and structure formation. The colloquial phrase “Big Bang” can misleadingly suggest an explosion from a central point into surrounding empty space. Standard FLRW expansion instead concerns the evolving geometry of space without a unique physical center. DFM distinguishes the observed expansion and its lawful continuation from claims about the actual provenance of every extrapolated antecedent state.

### IV.5 Terminal-state formalism
The conceptual deployment map is:

`S_4 = G_4(Phi_4, L_R, B_4, S_3)`

where `G_4` represents extraordinary deployment, `Phi_4` commissioned functions, `L_R` normative laws, `B_4` coherent boundary conditions, and `S_3` the already-established state. For subsequent ordinary evolution:

`S(t) = F_{L_R}(t-t_4,S_4), t >= t_4`.

These expressions define an architecture, not a solved relativistic cosmological model. The relationship between asynchronous generative process time and terrestrial time remains to be derived.

## IV. Terrestrial Centrality Hypothesis

### V.1 Observational and deployment inference
The universe is observed from Earth to exhibit approximate large-scale isotropy and a systematic redshift–distance relation. Genesis supplies an Earth-referenced sequence for the deployment of the wider cosmos. Together, these motivate an auxiliary **Terrestrial Centrality Hypothesis (TCH)**: Earth occupies an approximately distinguished position in the global cosmic geometry established on Day 4 and persisting afterward.

### V.2 Coincident centers
The proposed centrality comprises approximately coincident (a) center of global spatial symmetry, (b) center of the large-scale matter distribution, and (c) physically distinguished expansion structure. These concepts must be defined in a particular spacetime model. Earth need not be exactly stationary: local rotation, orbit, and peculiar motion can coexist with approximate cosmological centrality, subject to specified displacement tolerances.

### V.3 Limits of the inference
Observed isotropy from one location does not uniquely identify that location as the physical center. Homogeneous FLRW cosmology contains no unique physical center of expansion; choosing Earth as coordinate origin does not establish privileged geometry. Some inhomogeneous relativistic models admit central observers but face stringent constraints from CMB anisotropies, kinematic Sunyaev–Zel'dovich effects, BAO, supernova distances, lensing, and structure growth. TCH is therefore **compatible with some conceivable geometries**, not observationally established and not insulated from refutation.

### V.4 Research requirement
A serious TCH model must specify its metric, topology, radial matter profile, expansion congruence, terrestrial offset, light propagation, and perturbation evolution. Its ability to reproduce independent datasets must be tested without fitting a separate free function to every observational class.

## V. Hybrid Post-Deployment Cosmological Residuals

### VI.1 Definition
The **Post-Deployment Cosmological Residuals (PDCR)** hypothesis proposes that the stabilized Day 4 state could preserve signatures of its generative origin in three distinct classes: persistent structural properties, transient dynamical contributions, and inherited mature configurations. This is a research hypothesis, not an established interpretation of current anomalies.

### VI.2 Persistent residuals
These would be enduring matter, field, or geometric properties established during deployment. Potential research targets include gravitational effects conventionally modeled using dark matter and persistent vacuum-energy-like contributions. Any alternative must account for rotation curves, cluster dynamics, lensing, CMB acoustic structure, and the growth of large-scale structure. Calling a gravitational discrepancy a deployment residual does not itself provide an explanation.

### VI.3 Transient residuals
These would be physically identifiable contributions that relax, decay, or evolve under normative post-deployment laws. Candidate applications include time-dependent contributions to cosmic acceleration or expansion-history inference. The Hubble tension is a disagreement among measurement and inference pipelines; it cannot be assumed to represent a physical transient. The model must first predict a quantitative effect and show that it improves agreement across independent probes.

### VI.4 Inherited structural maturity
The Day 4 terminal state could contain coherent mature astronomical structures and their genuine process products. Such inheritance may be investigated in connection with early galaxies observed by JWST, including stellar populations, morphology, luminosity, and chemical enrichment. Particular contingent event records still require actual events. Observational estimates of early galaxy masses and ages are model dependent and subject to revision.

### VI.5 Unified mechanism and constraints
A single coherent terminal-state model should be explored before assigning unrelated auxiliary mechanisms to each phenomenon. A toy transient law such as `R(t)=R_0 exp[-(t-t_4)/tau]` illustrates a decaying degree of freedom but has no explanatory force until its physical carrier, units, couplings, conservation behavior, and observational consequences are specified. PDCR must predict measurable departures from alternative cosmologies, with parameter economy and explicit failure criteria.

## VI. Radiometric Concordance and Historical Age

### Objection
U–Pb, Rb–Sr, Sm–Nd, Ar–Ar, and other methods often yield mutually reinforcing chronologies across terrestrial, lunar, and meteoritic materials. Their agreement, combined with stratigraphic order, provides strong evidence of extended elapsed history.

### Response
DFM accepts the measured isotope ratios, experimentally established decay constants, and the significance of multi-system concordance. It investigates whether a common, physically constrained initialized state, followed by ordinary decay and actual geological events, could reproduce the observed relationships. The distinction between reconstructed and historical age is logically valid, but it is **not** itself a derivation of concordance. The burden includes U–Pb dual decay chains, concordia/discordia, isochron geometry, closure behavior, daughter inventories, and non-heat-centric systems. Independent constraints must determine the initial state; conventional target ages cannot be inserted and then recovered as supposed predictions.

### Further objection: independent cross-validation
Relative stratigraphic relationships and absolute isotopic ages are not identical methods. Their independent agreement strengthens conventional historical inference. DFM must explain the detailed covariance and known-age calibration cases; merely identifying assumptions does not nullify cross-validation.

## VII. Global Flood and Hydrotectonic Catastrophism

### VIII.1 Historical commitment and physical hypothesis
The framework affirms the biblical global Flood as a historical event. It investigates a global hydrotectonic catastrophe involving antecedent and concurrent tectonic failure, major hydraulic transport, sedimentation, ecological sorting, and post-event recovery. The Flood's historical status within the framework is distinct from the physical adequacy of any particular hydrotectonic mechanism.

### VIII.2 Proposed explanatory scope
Rapid sediment deposition, hydrodynamic sorting, ecological zonation, differential mobility, and tectonic reconfiguration are candidate contributors to the fossil and stratigraphic record. A singular event may have effects outside ordinary local observations, but that fact does not establish that all stratigraphy arose from one event or that gradual and episodic conventional models are incapable of explaining the evidence.

### VIII.3 Severe constraints
The proposed model must satisfy energy and heat dissipation budgets, tectonic force and velocity limits, sediment supply and transport, erosion and deposition sequences, basin connectivity, paleontological succession, trace fossils, geochemical patterns, and post-Flood recovery. Evidence indicating genuine repeated events, intervening ecological communities, or long-lived processes must be addressed causally rather than relabeled as sorting by default.

### VIII.4 Fossil succession objection
The ordered succession and nested distribution of fossils present a demanding challenge. The framework proposes created biological organization, actual diversification, ecological and hydrodynamic sorting, and tectonic processes as possible components. These must be modeled jointly and compared with alternatives. The mere existence of catastrophic depositional processes does not prove that they can reproduce the entire observed succession.

## VIII. Biological Diversification and the Creation-to-Fall Interval

The creation-to-Fall interval is temporally unspecified by the biblical narrative. DFM provisionally investigates ordinary diversification and speciation, including whether substantial morphological differences can arise from initially created populations under admissible reproductive and ecological constraints. Quantitative population genetics, generation times, dispersal, and observed diversification rates are required. The placement of fossil evidence relative to the Fall and Flood must respect the programme's commitments about death and event provenance. This auxiliary remains open and may need substantial restriction or revision.

## IX. Comparative Explanatory Burdens: DFM and Naturalistic Reconstructions

### IX.1 The burden is bilateral
DFM does not claim that unresolved questions in conventional science automatically validate biblical creation. Equally, conventional models cannot be treated as explanatorily complete simply because they presently enjoy greater quantitative success in many domains. The appropriate comparison is between **actual, specified research programmes**, with their respective observational achievements, background assumptions, auxiliary hypotheses, unresolved mechanisms, and testable consequences. Each side must distinguish observations from inferred antecedent history and from metaphysical interpretations of that history.

### IX.2 Methodological versus metaphysical naturalism
Methodological naturalism ordinarily limits scientific modeling to regular physical causes. As a practical discipline for studying ongoing ordinary processes, this can be productive and is substantially compatible with DFM's expectation of normative post-deployment physics. **Metaphysical naturalism**, by contrast, asserts that reality contains no supernatural cause. That metaphysical assertion is not a measured result of physics. When methodological restrictions are elevated into a rule that excludes divine special action *as a possible historical cause*, the restriction functions as an antecedent philosophical boundary, not an empirical refutation of the biblical account. Conversely, the possibility of divine action does not relieve DFM of demonstrating the physical consequences of its proposed history.

### IX.3 Initial conditions, physical laws, and cosmic provenance
Standard cosmology successfully models many features of expansion, CMB anisotropies, nucleosynthesis, and structure formation from specified early conditions. Yet the model's empirical success does not by itself establish the ultimate provenance of its initial conditions, why its laws hold, or why the relevant parameters possess their values. Inflationary scenarios, where adopted, introduce their own dynamical and initial-state questions. A naturalistic historical account that purports to explain *ultimate origins* must address these questions or explicitly limit its explanatory claim. DFM has a corresponding obligation to specify a coherent Day 4 terminal state and lawful forward evolution. **Neither programme may treat an unspecified initial boundary as a complete mechanism.**

### IX.4 Dark components, expansion discrepancies, and early structure
Dark matter and dark energy are empirically motivated components of successful models, not mere inventions; nevertheless, their fundamental physical nature remains unresolved. The Hubble tension is a live discrepancy between inference methods, subject to ongoing systematic and theoretical assessment. Some early-galaxy findings have prompted revisions of astrophysical assumptions, although they do not establish a cosmological crisis. These are **real explanatory and model-selection burdens**, not proofs that the conventional framework is false. PDCR must face equivalent or greater scrutiny: it must predict these observations with defined physical carriers, coupled dynamics, and independent constraints rather than simply reclassify them as deployment signatures.

### IX.5 Geological and chronological reconstruction
Conventional geology and radiometric geochronology exhibit substantial empirical consilience, including independent relative ordering and multiple isotopic systems. Their historical application nonetheless depends on reconstructing past conditions, event sequences, and system-specific closure or disturbance histories. These assumptions are often independently testable and must not be caricatured as arbitrary. DFM must explain the same detailed patterns under a constrained initialization and Flood history. The comparative question is not whether *either* model uses assumptions, but which assumptions are independently warranted and which framework better predicts the full evidence.

### IX.6 Historical uniqueness and causal inference
The inability to repeat the origin of the cosmos or an ancient geological catastrophe does not render historical inquiry invalid. Both programmes infer unique events from surviving effects using present observations, physical laws, and boundary assumptions. Naturalistic historical explanations bear the burden of showing that their proposed causal histories adequately explain the evidence, just as DFM bears the burden of showing that its extraordinary deployment and Flood hypotheses generate the claimed effects. A prohibition on supernatural historical causation cannot substitute for an evidentiary comparison.

### IX.7 Symmetric appraisal rule
The paper adopts the following comparative rule: **No explanatory credit without a specified causal account; no automatic dismissal for an unresolved mechanism; no exemption from independent evidence; and no transfer from methodological convention to metaphysical conclusion without argument.** Record separately (a) established observations, (b) successful quantitative predictions, (c) model-dependent historical inferences, (d) unresolved physical mechanisms, and (e) metaphysical commitments. A serious DFM programme must acknowledge where conventional models currently outperform it, while a fair comparison must also acknowledge conventional models' genuine open problems and explanatory limits.

## X. Objections and Responses

The following replies state the strongest available **positive DFM argument first**, followed by a separate **research obligation**. This is a defense of the proposed framework, not a claim that its auxiliary physical models have already been validated. Empirical observations and reconstructions are not interchangeable, and neither DFM nor a conventional historical model receives exemption from specifying initial conditions, assumptions, causal mechanisms, and independent tests.

### Objection 1: How could there be light and literal days before the Sun?
**Objection.** Genesis 1:3–5 places light and an evening–morning cycle on Day 1, but 1:14–19 commissions the luminaries on Day 4. Since ordinary daylight is solar, the first three days cannot be literal.

**DFM response.** This objection assumes the **ordinary governor of a process must also be its original cause**. The text makes precisely the opposite distinction. God creates light and establishes the first terrestrial light–dark sequence before appointing the luminaries to govern and mark that sequence. The days are defined by the recurring terrestrial evening–morning pattern, not by the claim that sunlight was its only possible source. The narrative's chronology is therefore internally coherent under its own explicit premise of supernatural creation. Revelation 21:23 and 22:5 further illustrate the biblical conception of illumination independent of the Sun, although they do not specify the Day 1 mechanism. The transition from extraordinary initial illumination to ordinary solar governance is consistent with DFM's phased deployment architecture: **commissioning a normative mechanism need not coincide with the first existence of its function**.

**Research obligation.** Specify the physical interpretation of a terrestrial 24-hour cycle, the relation of illumination to Earth's rotation, and the transition to Day 4 solar governance. These details are not needed to establish logical coherence but matter to a complete physical model.

### Objection 2: Distant starlight requires billions of years of terrestrial history
**Objection.** Light from distant galaxies and detailed supernova signals imply that the universe has undergone a long ordinary history.

**DFM response.** Light-travel distance constrains the propagation and causal structure of observed radiation; equating that with elapsed **terrestrial creation chronology** assumes a particular globally applicable historical time mapping across the creation boundary. DFM explicitly distinguishes the terrestrial Day 4 interval from asynchronous cosmic generative development. Genuine stellar processes, radiation propagation, and their observable consequences could occur within the extraordinary deployment regime and then enter normative runtime. Thus DFM does not need to deny distant photons, their spectra, or actual supernova events, nor claim that photons contain fictional event histories. Its contention is that **the laws governing the commissioned cosmos need not describe the temporal mechanics of its commissioning**.

**Research obligation.** Construct a coherent spacetime and clock relation that reproduces photon trajectories, redshifts, time dilation, supernova event correlations, and CMB observables without fabricated chronicles. The proposed asynchronous relation is not yet a solved metric.

### Objection 3: The CMB and primordial abundances prove conventional hot Big Bang history
**Objection.** The CMB's blackbody spectrum and anisotropies, together with primordial elemental abundances, corroborate the standard hot early-universe reconstruction.

**DFM response.** These are significant observations, but a reconstruction of antecedent physical conditions is not identical to direct observation of every historical stage. DFM allows the Day 4 generative process to **actually produce** thermal, radiative, and nucleosynthetic conditions, or to establish an integrated mature macrostate where historical integrity permits. The observed consistency of multiple fields is exactly the kind of coherence expected of an integrated deployment; it does not by itself establish that all corresponding conventional elapsed time was traversed on the terrestrial clock. This is a claim about the underdetermination of provenance, not a dismissal of the measurements.

**Research obligation.** Derive, rather than merely assume, the CMB spectrum and anisotropy structure, acoustic features, polarization, light-element abundances, and their covariance. Presently standard cosmology has substantial quantitative success that DFM must match or improve upon.

### Objection 4: Radiometric concordance excludes mature initialization
**Objection.** Multiple isotope systems yield mutually reinforcing ages, so a younger historical boundary is inconsistent with the data.

**DFM response.** Radiometric instruments measure isotope states; inferred ages depend on models of initial composition, closure, disturbance, and subsequent decay. DFM accepts ordinary decay physics and the reality of concordance, but disputes the **logical necessity** of treating a backward reconstructed trajectory as the only possible provenance across a supernatural initialization boundary. A coherently initialized world would be expected to possess correlated physical and chemical state variables, not arbitrary isotope ratios. In particular, terrestrial heat production provides a functional constraint on U, Th, and K inventories. Concordance therefore motivates a stringent **coherent-initialization hypothesis**, not a categorical logical contradiction of creation.

**Research obligation.** The heat budget does not derive U–Pb concordia, isochron slopes, extinct radionuclide patterns, or cross-system correlations. These must emerge from independent constraints without inserting conventional target ages. Existing concordance remains strong evidence favoring conventional chronologies until an alternative earns comparable quantitative support.

### Objection 5: Fossil succession cannot result from a global Flood
**Objection.** Fossil ordering, trace fossils, ecological assemblages, and stratigraphic structures cannot all be explained by rapid deposition.

**DFM response.** The objection risks substituting a single simplified mechanism, hydrodynamic sorting, for the proposed **compound historical-causal model**. DFM's BWM framework considers created biological organization, genuine post-creation diversification, ecological zonation, organism mobility, hydraulic transport, rapid sedimentation, tectonic reconfiguration, and subsequent recovery. A global catastrophe would interact with geographically and ecologically structured populations rather than randomly mix a uniform biota. Nor does the proposal assert that every geological layer must be produced by one undifferentiated surge. The biblical Flood supplies a historical candidate for broad correlated catastrophe that deserves mechanistic investigation rather than exclusion solely because its scale is exceptional.

**Research obligation.** Derive specific fossil-ordering, sediment-provenance, trace-fossil, and basin-scale predictions while satisfying thermal and tectonic budgets. An explanatory inventory is not yet a successful quantitative geological reconstruction.

### Objection 6: The Copernican Principle rules out terrestrial centrality
**Objection.** Earth's observational isotropy cannot make it special because standard cosmology is homogeneous and has no center.

**DFM response.** Observational isotropy from Earth does not logically establish homogeneity everywhere; the inference to a nonprivileged location uses additional assumptions and evidence. Likewise, **a coordinate origin is not a physical center**, so DFM's TCH does not rest on simply drawing coordinates around Earth. It instead proposes a testable, approximately distinguished geometry and matter distribution motivated by the terrestrial deployment sequence. The Copernican Principle is a productive scientific heuristic, not a theorem that prohibits investigation of a physically distinguished observer position. A non-FLRW centrality model is a legitimate mathematical research question.

**Research obligation.** The broad success of homogeneous cosmology and independent tests of homogeneity place serious constraints on central models. TCH needs an explicit metric, allowed terrestrial offset, and successful predictions for CMB, kSZ, BAO, supernovae, lensing, and growth.

### Objection 7: Expansion cannot begin on Day 4 and still continue today
**Objection.** Expansion is understood as a long-running cosmological process, not an event centered on Earth or initiated within a terrestrial day.

**DFM response.** DFM distinguishes the **initiation of a dynamical state** from its subsequent lawful evolution. A miraculous Day 4 act could establish an expanding spacetime whose terminal state satisfies the equations of normative physical evolution. The same expansion can then continue without further miraculous intervention. The creation-to-runtime boundary is not equivalent to an abrupt stop in motion. Earth-centered expansion, if asserted, requires a distinguished geometry; it is not the conventional picture of galaxies flying outward from Earth through static space.

**Research obligation.** Specify the generative-to-normative matching conditions, stress-energy constraints, metric, and expansion history. Standard FLRW models have no unique expansion center; TCH cannot borrow their successes without showing how its distinct geometry reproduces them.

### Objection 8: Dark matter, dark energy, the Hubble tension, and JWST cannot be deployment residuals
**Objection.** These phenomena have naturalistic interpretations, and anomalies do not establish creation.

**DFM response.** DFM does not argue that an anomaly automatically proves deployment. It advances a **unified boundary-condition research alternative**: Day 4 may establish enduring structural properties, transient dynamical contributions, and inherited mature astronomical states. These classes give principled, distinct explanatory assignments rather than an unrestricted appeal to miracle for every datum. If a common terminal state generates correlated consequences across expansion, gravitation, and early structure, that would be more informative than separately tuning unrelated mechanisms. Competing models also invoke auxiliary parameters, dark components, and reconstructed initial conditions; their evidential standing rests on predictive performance, not on a privileged metaphysical label.

**Research obligation.** Identify carriers and governing equations; reproduce dark-matter gravitational evidence and acceleration probes; resolve rather than rename any Hubble discrepancy; and predict early-galaxy observables independently. The residual account is presently conjectural, not a confirmed explanation.

### Objection 9: A global hydrotectonic catastrophe is untestable because it was unique
**Objection.** A singular global Flood cannot be experimentally repeated and therefore lies outside scientific analysis.

**DFM response.** Historical sciences routinely investigate unrepeatable events by studying **repeatable causal principles and enduring traces**. Impact events, volcanic episodes, and ancient tectonic transitions are evaluated through mechanics, chemistry, spatial patterns, and surviving evidence. A proposed global Flood is no different in principle: the event itself need not recur for its hypothesized physical mechanisms and predicted consequences to be tested. Excluding it a priori because it is biblically motivated would conflate metaphysical preference with methodological assessment.

**Research obligation.** Demonstrate the actual feasibility of the proposed global event, including tectonic stresses, thermal dissipation, sediment transport, fossil ordering, and post-event conditions. Testability does not guarantee success.

### Objection 10: Functional maturity is deceptive and makes DFM unfalsifiable
**Objection.** A creator could initialize any apparent age or trace, making every possible observation compatible with the framework.

**DFM response.** That describes **unconstrained appearance-of-age reasoning**, which DFM expressly rejects. Its Historical Integrity Constraint forbids invented records of contingent events. Its no-free-lunch rule demands integrated, physically coherent initial states constrained by commissioned function, chemistry, conservation, and independently testable covariance. Its distinction between initialization and genuine deployment history preserves real causal provenance. DFM therefore proposes a restricted explanatory architecture, not the claim that any conceivable observation can be declared created in place.

**Research obligation.** Translate those restrictions into quantitative initial-state families and predictions. Where a proposed auxiliary fails independent severe tests or requires arbitrary rescue parameters, reject or revise that auxiliary rather than treating the observation as unconstrained initialized maturity.

### Objection 11: Soft tissues, trace radiocarbon, and helium retention settle the chronology
**Objection.** Such findings are either decisive evidence against conventional ages or fully explained by conventional preservation and contamination processes.

**DFM response.** Neither blanket conclusion follows without specimen-specific analysis. DFM treats these findings as **potential discriminators**, not automatic chronological proofs. Their value depends on verified sampling, contamination control, preservation chemistry, diffusion and closure conditions, and competing predictions. Where ordinary models fail a well-controlled test, the failure should be acknowledged rather than dismissed because its implications are inconvenient. Equally, a surprising result must not be promoted into a universal chronology without a validated causal account.

**Research obligation.** Maintain a provenance-controlled evidence ledger, distinguish genuine anomalies from artifacts, and test whether DFM predicts the results better than alternative mechanisms.

### Objection 12: Cross-validation of independent dating methods establishes unique historical provenance
**Objection.** Agreement among stratigraphy, isotopic dating, astronomy, and other chronometers makes alternative histories untenable.

**DFM response.** Cross-method agreement is powerful evidence of a coherent reconstruction, but its historical interpretation depends on the independence of relevant boundary and provenance assumptions. DFM's claim is not that consilience is meaningless; it is that **consilience establishes coherence, while provenance warrants history**. A coherent initialized state could yield coordinated observables across coupled systems. Conversely, genuine post-deployment events can produce valid chronometers that DFM must retain. The correct question is whether the detailed agreements can be derived from independent functional and physical constraints under the proposed deployment boundary.

**Research obligation.** Identify which dating systems truly provide independent provenance constraints, and derive their covariance without case-by-case tuning. Where DFM cannot do so, the conventional reconstruction retains its evidentiary advantage.

## XI. Research Programme Commitments and Falsification

The programme shall preserve the following separation: **scriptural historical commitments**, **exegetical or architectural inferences**, **auxiliary physical mechanisms**, **empirical predictions**, and **unresolved obligations**. It shall prefer independently motivated models, record contrary evidence, and reject failed auxiliaries rather than migrate their unexplained observations into unspecified miraculous initialization.

Near-term priorities are: (1) specify the Day 4 terminal state and ordinary forward evolution; (2) formulate and test a distinguished-center metric with terrestrial offset bounds; (3) derive persistent and transient residual dynamics; (4) compare cosmological predictions against CMB, BAO, supernova, lensing, kSZ, and structure growth; (5) evaluate early-galaxy properties against evolving JWST datasets; (6) derive cross-system isotope predictions without fitting target ages; and (7) quantify global hydrotectonic energy, sediment, and fossil-ordering predictions.

Failures in TCH, PDCR, or hydrotectonic auxiliaries would warrant their modification or rejection. They do not by themselves logically refute the biblical claims, but repeated auxiliary failure without predictive progress weakens the physical research programme.

## Conclusion
The framework affirms six literal Earth-referenced days of miraculous phased creation, initial divine illumination before the Sun's Day 4 commissioning, asynchronous Day 4 cosmic deployment that initiates continuing expansion, and normative stabilization by that day's conclusion. It advances terrestrial geometric centrality and hybrid cosmological residuals as provisional auxiliary hypotheses. It also investigates ordinary biological diversification and a global hydrotectonic Flood as historical and physical components of the Biblical World Model. The programme's governing requirement is that proposed explanations preserve historical integrity, satisfy physical constraints, and produce independently testable consequences.
