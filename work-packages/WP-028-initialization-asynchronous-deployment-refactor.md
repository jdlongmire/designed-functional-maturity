# WP-028: Initialization + Asynchronous Deployment Refactor

**Status:** Proposed programme refactor  
**Scope:** DFM hard-core clarification, LPI temporal-architecture recovery, Day 4 cosmology, historical-integrity rules, and corpus propagation

## Governing correction

> **DFM is a both-and model: necessary initialization and genuine asynchronous deployment are complementary components of creation, not mutually exclusive alternatives.**

Recent DFM development increasingly asked coherent initialization to carry work that the earlier Literal Programmatic Intervention (LPI) model assigned to genuine cosmic process history under extraordinary temporal deployment. This WP recovers the strongest LPI temporal insight without restoring superseded LPI claims wholesale.

## Governing architecture

\`\`\`text
necessary initialization
        +
phased creative/deployment acts
        +
asynchronous process execution where warranted
        ->
synchronization / commissioning
        ->
ordinary runtime
\`\`\`

Formal scaffold:

\`\`\`text
S0 = G(Phi, L_R, B)
S0 -- A(tau_E, tau_C1 ... tau_Cn) --> S*
Sync(S*) -> R(L_R)
\`\`\`

Here S0 is the coherently initialized boundary state; G the divine initialization act; Phi commissioned functionality and creational objectives; L_R ordinary post-deployment runtime law; B coherent boundary conditions; A the extraordinary deployment regime; tau_E Earth-local elapsed/process time; tau_Cn elapsed/process time associated with other domains/processes; S* completed deployment state; and Sync the transition into ordinary coordinated operation.

The model does not require tau_E = tau_Cn during extraordinary deployment.

## Initialization-Deployment Complementarity Principle

> **DFM does not treat initialization and extraordinary process history as mutually exclusive explanations. Creation may include both directly instantiated mature states and genuinely executed causal processes operating under extraordinary deployment conditions. The explanatory task is to determine their warranted allocation within a unified creation architecture.**

Initialization is not a fallback for failed history. Asynchronous deployment is not a universal requirement that every initialized feature acquire an executed developmental biography.

## Day 1 initialization boundary

Genesis 1:1-3 supplies the theological boundary for the initial creation state. DFM retains the claim that creation begins with whatever matter-energy, spacetime, law-governed capacities, information, order, illumination, and coupled boundary conditions are necessary for the subsequent creation sequence.

The Day 1 boundary is substantive rather than a null starting point. Every origins model requires an initial or boundary state. DFM's distinctive claim concerns the content, agency, purpose, and revealed chronology of that state.

## Day 4 asynchronous cosmic deployment

The recovered LPI hypothesis is:

> **During the terrestrial Day 4 interval, cosmic domains and processes may execute under temporally asynchronous conditions such that genuine physical histories accumulate without requiring identical elapsed process-time on the Earth-local creation-day clock.**

The Genesis day remains an ordinary terrestrial evening-morning day. The auxiliary does not redefine day as billions of years.

\`\`\`text
Delta tau_E ~ one terrestrial day
Delta tau_C may be much greater than Delta tau_E
\`\`\`

Candidate Day 4 processes include stellar development, galactic organization, nucleosynthesis, supernova events and remnants, radiation propagation, and other causally connected cosmic processes whose observed state carries genuine process depth.

The hypothesis does not require every cosmic feature to be procedurally generated. Initialized macrostructure and genuine asynchronous process history may coexist.

## Scriptural possibility warrant: Temporal Coordination Principle

Joshua 10:12-14 and 2 Kings 20:8-11 / Isaiah 38:7-8 record divine interventions affecting the ordinary relationship among astronomical phenomena, terrestrial observation, and temporal progression.

These texts do not disclose the Day 4 mechanism and must not be represented as technical descriptions of relativity or multiple clock domains.

> **Temporal Coordination Principle:** Scripture records divine interventions in which the ordinary temporal coordination among created physical processes is altered. Uniform synchronous progression across all created physical processes therefore cannot be imposed as a theological constraint upon the creation act.

This is a possibility warrant, not a mechanism proof. 2 Peter 3:8 may illustrate divine transcendence of creaturely temporal scale but should not carry the technical argument.

## Physical possibility and model-development boundary

Modern relativity rejects an absolute universal Newtonian clock. Proper time is worldline-dependent, so differential elapsed time is physically intelligible. DFM may use this as a conceptual physical-possibility result. It may not infer that GR therefore already supplies the Day 4 mechanism.

Candidate relativistic, gravitational/cosmological time-dilation, anisotropic-synchronization, or other models remain auxiliaries to compare and severely test. The computer analogy of multiple threads at different clock speeds remains pedagogically useful but is not itself the physical model.

## Cana and miraculous state/process warrant

John 2:1-11 establishes that a genuine completed physical state need not have traversed the ordinary-duration pathway an observer would reasonably retrodict from it:

\`\`\`text
Observed state + ordinary process model -> retrodicted history
Retrodicted history -/-> necessarily actual historical pathway
\`\`\`

Cana supports initialization and extraordinary transformation. It does not require all causally rich cosmic observations to be initialized states. The recovered LPI architecture permits the stronger explanation where warranted: a process may genuinely occur while its temporal execution differs radically from ordinary runtime.

## Historical integrity under the both-and architecture

The obsolete strong criterion is:

\`\`\`text
T(e) -> ordinary antecedent history e must have been traversed
\`\`\`

Replace it with:

\`\`\`text
Properties(S) compatible with or ordinarily generated by H -/-> Actual(H)
\`\`\`

Distinguish four categories:

1. **Initialized actuality:** genuine state directly instantiated at a creation boundary or creative phase.
2. **Asynchronously executed actuality:** genuine causal process/event executed under extraordinary deployment conditions.
3. **Ordinary runtime history:** genuine process/event under post-deployment regular operation.
4. **Arbitrary fabricated testimony:** semantically determinate pseudo-biography inserted solely to assert a nonexistent history without independent creational warrant.

DFM permits 1-3 and rejects 4.

## Allocation discipline

For each explanandum record the observed state, ordinary retrodictive interpretation, candidate initialized components, candidate genuinely executed processes, proposed temporal regime, independent creational/physical rationale, coupling requirements, free parameters and auxiliaries, discriminators, and failure conditions.

The both-and architecture fails methodologically if assignments are changed observation-by-observation solely to protect chronology or if no coherent common deployment architecture can reproduce required cross-domain relationships.

## Supernova and starlight application

The preferred question is no longer: Was the supernova initialized OR did it really occur?

It is:

> **What combination of initialized cosmic state and genuine asynchronously executed causal history best accounts for the observed phenomenon within one coherent Day 4 deployment architecture?**

A supernova may involve initialized progenitor/cosmic macrostate plus genuine stellar development, explosion, nucleosynthesis, remnant formation, radiation propagation, extraordinary differential elapsed/process time during Day 4, and synchronization into ordinary runtime. Not every element must receive the same allocation.

## Hard core versus auxiliary

Candidate hard-core clarification:

- Genesis 1 records real phased supernatural creation/deployment.
- Creation includes a purposefully specified coherent initial/boundary state.
- Created systems may possess designed functional maturity.
- Creation-process causation is not required to be identical to ordinary post-deployment runtime trajectories.
- The Genesis creation days are real terrestrial days.

Recovered auxiliary hypothesis:

- Day 4 included asynchronous cosmic deployment in which non-terrestrial domains/processes could accumulate substantially different elapsed/process time before synchronization into ordinary runtime.

The specific physical implementation is not hard core.

## Layered Hypothesis Architecture

WP-028 governs a **layered hypothesis stack**. Claims at different layers must not be assigned equal epistemic status. Failure of an upper layer does not automatically defeat a lower layer, and success of a lower layer does not establish an upper layer.

### Layer 0: Canonical constraints

Claims treated as governing theological/exegetical constraints for this programme:

- God is the intentional Creator and creation is not an unguided self-originating event.
- Genesis 1 presents a real ordered creation sequence.
- The creation days establish a terrestrial evening-morning chronology.
- Day 1 establishes a substantive created boundary rather than a null state.
- Day 4 has a real role in the deployment and governance of the luminaries.
- Scripture does not require a universal synchronous clock across every physical process during supernatural creation.

Layer 0 constrains model construction but does not itself specify a physical mechanism.

### Layer 1: DFM architectural hypotheses

These claims define the present DFM systems architecture:

- creation begins from a coherently initialized state;
- initialized states may possess Designed Functional Maturity;
- initialization and genuine process execution are complementary;
- creation-process causation need not reproduce ordinary post-commissioning runtime trajectories;
- extraordinary deployment transitions into ordinary runtime;
- present state plus an ordinary process model does not uniquely entail the actual pathway by which that state obtained.

This layer is the principal subject of the DFM programme and should change only when the programme-level argument requires revision.

### Layer 2: LPI asynchronous-deployment auxiliary

The recovered LPI hypothesis proposes that Day 4 includes genuine cosmic process execution under differential temporal conditions:

```text
Delta tau_E ~ one terrestrial day
Delta tau_C,i need not equal Delta tau_E
```

A stronger candidate form is:

```text
Delta tau_C,i >> Delta tau_E
```

The weaker non-equality claim and the stronger large-differential claim must be tested separately. Day 4 asynchronous deployment is an auxiliary hypothesis, not revealed doctrine and not yet an established physical model.

### Layer 3: Candidate physical implementations

Possible mechanisms capable of realizing Layer 2 belong here. Examples include:

- relativistic differential proper-time architectures;
- gravitational or cosmological time-dilation models;
- preferred-foliation or creation-boundary metric models;
- anisotropic synchronization proposals;
- multiple coupled temporal domains;
- temporal-domain translation or frequency-scaling mechanisms.

These are competing candidate implementations. No item in this layer is presently canonical DFM. The computer metaphor of asynchronous threads is an analogy, not a mechanism.

### Layer 4: Observation-specific research hypotheses

Claims assigning particular observations to initialization, asynchronous deployment, or their interaction belong at the highest and most defeasible layer. Current examples for investigation include:

- dark-matter phenomenology as partly related to initialized structural conditions;
- dark-energy phenomenology as partly related to initialized metric/boundary conditions;
- the CMB as potentially containing initialization or deployment-regime signatures;
- stellar populations as mixtures of initialized macrostate and genuine deployment history;
- supernovae, remnants, nucleosynthetic products, photons, neutrinos, and gravitational signals as causally coupled allocation problems;
- redshift or spectral effects as possible constraints on any temporal-domain transition.

These are research hypotheses, not DFM conclusions.

### Layered Dependency Rule

```text
canonical constraint
    -> architectural hypothesis
        -> deployment auxiliary
            -> candidate physical mechanism
                -> observation-specific hypothesis
```

Evidence may propagate upward only when the relevant inferential bridge is independently warranted. Falsification propagates downward only when the failed claim is logically required by the lower layer.

Thus:

- failure of a CMB deployment-residual hypothesis does not defeat asynchronous Day 4;
- failure of one time-dilation mechanism does not defeat the general asynchronous auxiliary;
- failure of asynchronous Day 4 does not by itself defeat Designed Functional Maturity or coherent initialization;
- failure of a DFM initialization auxiliary does not by itself overturn the canonical Genesis constraints.

Conversely, the truth of Genesis does not establish a particular CMB, dark-matter, or relativistic mechanism.

## Ancillary-Hypothesis Symmetry Principle

> **DFM retains the same legitimate freedom as competing research programmes to introduce, test, revise, combine, or reject ancillary hypotheses in response to unresolved explanatory burdens and new evidence. An ancillary hypothesis is not rendered ad hoc merely because it preserves the programme's hard core. Its epistemic cost depends on independent motivation, constraint, explanatory integration, predictive or discriminating consequences, and performance under severe testing. The same standards must be applied symmetrically to competing naturalistic auxiliaries.**

Ancillary hypotheses are therefore genuine research opportunities. DFM should actively permit multiple competing implementations beneath the same architectural layer rather than prematurely forcing one mechanism into the hard core.

A healthy programme may contain, for example:

```text
DFM hard core
    -> architectural hypotheses
        -> H1 differential proper-time architecture
        -> H2 creation-specific metric/foliation architecture
        -> H3 anisotropic propagation/synchronization
        -> H4 mixed initialization + temporal deployment
        -> Hn presently unformulated mechanism
```

These hypotheses may compete, overlap, combine, or fail independently.

### Ad hoc versus progressive development

Introduction after an anomaly is discovered does not by itself make an auxiliary degenerative. The relevant questions are what constrains the hypothesis and what it does after introduction.

A proposed ancillary is strengthened when it:

- has motivation independent of the datum it was introduced to address;
- applies across multiple observations rather than one isolated anomaly;
- reduces rather than multiplies free parameters;
- integrates previously separate explanatory domains;
- yields discriminators, novel expectations, or severe tests;
- survives tests on evidence not used to formulate it;
- exposes itself to identifiable failure conditions.

It becomes methodologically costly when it:

- is tuned independently for each observation;
- has no independent rationale;
- merely restates the observation in model language;
- generates no consequences beyond the anomaly it protects;
- migrates whenever a test threatens it;
- is indefinitely insulated from falsification.

### Symmetry rule

The same accounting applies to DFM, uniformitarian/deep-time models, standard cosmological models, and other competing historical programmes. Terms such as *auxiliary*, *ancillary*, *adjustment*, *new mechanism*, or *model extension* must not carry different evidential standards merely because one programme is naturalistic and another is design-based.

The comparison is not:

```text
new DFM auxiliary = rescue
new naturalistic auxiliary = science
```

The comparison is:

```text
independent motivation
+ constraint
+ explanatory integration
+ empirical consequence
+ severe-test performance
= epistemic appraisal
```

### Opportunity rule

> **Novel ancillary hypotheses are research opportunities, not methodological liabilities merely because they are novel. They become liabilities when they remain unconstrained, observation-specific, non-discriminating, or indefinitely protected from failure.**

Accordingly, WP-028 should maintain an explicit hypothesis registry as Layers 2-4 develop. Candidate mechanisms should be allowed to branch rather than being silently promoted, and rejected branches should remain auditable.


## Test Everything, Keep the Good

WP-028 adopts the following research rule:

> **Retain what the architecture requires. Explore what the architecture permits. Assert only what Scripture, observation, or a developed physical model warrants.**

Each hypothesis is to be independently registered, constrained, tested, revised, retained, demoted, or rejected. Failed upper-layer hypotheses remain part of the audit trail rather than being silently absorbed into a revised narrative.

The programme should preferentially attempt to break the stronger auxiliary claims. In particular, the Day 4 asynchronous hypothesis gains explanatory value only if a common constrained architecture can preserve multiple independent causal relationships without observation-specific clock tuning.

## Canonical provisional statement

> **Designed Functional Maturity models creation as both initialized and deployed. God establishes a coherent, purposefully specified creation boundary and may directly instantiate mature functional states. The same creation process may also include genuine causal histories executed under extraordinary deployment conditions.
