# WP-003 — Supernova Functional-State Hypothesis

## Status

Proposed / active investigation

## Purpose

Develop and test a Designed Functional Maturity (DFM) hypothesis in which supernova-related states and residual products are not treated merely as problematic "light in transit," but as potentially functional components of the coherently initialized, life-permitting cosmic system.

SN 1987A is the motivating test case because its arriving radiation carries temporally structured information corresponding to a stellar explosion. The work package asks whether DFM can account for such information-rich astronomical observations through a system-level initialization model without resorting to an unconstrained auxiliary.

## Working hypothesis

**DFM Supernova Functional-State Hypothesis (SFSH):** Supernova events and/or their residual products constitute functionally relevant components of the designed mature cosmic state, contributing materially to the chemical, energetic, and environmental conditions required for a life-permitting universe and Earth.

The hypothesis does not presently assert that any single supernova is necessary for life. The initial research claim is narrower: stellar nucleosynthesis and dispersal processes, including supernovae, contribute to elemental and environmental conditions relevant to terrestrial habitability.

## Systems framing

DFM should not treat distant-light initialization as an isolated photon problem. The candidate initialization unit is larger:

**source + event-state + products + causal relationships + propagating information + downstream functional consequences**

The research question is whether those elements form a coherent functional state whose retrodictable antecedents may extend behind the creation initialization boundary without requiring that every reconstructed antecedent state was instantiated during post-initialization runtime.

## Epistemic constraint

Revelation supplies the claimed creation boundary, agency, and purpose. Nature supplies the observable resulting state. DFM's task is to contextualize the observations within the revealed history.

This work package may not use "God could have done it" as sufficient explanation, arbitrary initialized photons detached from a coherent source/event state, unrestricted mature-state auxiliaries introduced solely to rescue the model, or dismissal of conventional astronomical measurements because their retrodictive interpretation conflicts with DFM.

Information-rich observations such as SN 1987A are research obligations for DFM.

## Research questions

1. Which life-permitting elemental abundances are materially dependent on stellar nucleosynthesis and dispersal?
2. Which relevant elements are predominantly sourced by core-collapse supernovae, Type Ia supernovae, AGB stars, neutron-star mergers, or other processes?
3. What evidence indicates supernova contributions to solar-system formation, including short-lived radionuclides and isotopic anomalies?
4. What functional roles do supernova remnants, shock waves, and enriched interstellar material play in subsequent star and planet formation?
5. What information is encoded in observed supernova light curves and spectra, particularly SN 1987A?
6. Under DFM, what would have to be initialized coherently for an information-rich radiation field to correspond to a real source and functional cosmic state?
7. Can DFM distinguish an initialized functional state from a fabricated event history in a principled, non-ad-hoc way?
8. What observations would count against the proposed functional-state hypothesis?
9. Does the hypothesis generate any novel or discriminating expectation concerning elemental abundance, source/event relationships, or cosmic habitability?
10. How does the hypothesis interface with Day 4 deployment without asserting more than the biblical text warrants?

## Initial falsification / failure conditions

The hypothesis should be weakened or rejected if research shows that the proposed supernova-related states have no material functional relationship to life-permitting conditions; the required initialized state cannot preserve coherent source, event, product, and radiation relationships without arbitrary auxiliaries; the model requires observations to represent events having no functional or causal relationship to the initialized system; a simpler DFM mechanism explains the observations with fewer unsupported assumptions; or the hypothesis conflicts with established observations that cannot be reconciled without relaxing its stated constraints.

## Work plan

### Tranche A — Conventional astrophysical baseline

Establish from primary and high-quality technical sources supernova nucleosynthesis and elemental yields, heavy-element dispersal and interstellar enrichment, supernova-remnant effects on molecular clouds and star formation, solar-system evidence for nearby stellar/supernova contributions, and SN 1987A observations including neutrinos, light curve, spectra, remnant, and ongoing evolution.

### Tranche B — Functional dependency analysis

Map which observed supernova products or effects contribute to rocky-planet composition, radiogenic inventory, atmospheric/ocean chemistry, magnetic/geodynamic conditions where warranted, biological elemental requirements, and broader galactic habitability. Distinguish necessary, contributing, contingent, and merely correlated roles.

### Tranche C — DFM initialization model

Develop candidate system-state models that preserve coherent relationships among source, event-state, ejecta/products, propagating radiation/information, and downstream effects. Compare ordinary elapsed-history interpretation, naive light-in-transit initialization, coherent functional-state initialization, and any other constrained DFM alternative emerging from the evidence.

### Tranche D — SN 1987A test case

Use SN 1987A as a high-information stress test. Determine what each candidate model must explain and identify residual explanatory costs.

### Tranche E — Predictions and discriminators

Identify observations that could discriminate among candidate models or constrain DFM. Record unresolved items rather than purchasing agreement through unconstrained auxiliaries.

## Deliverables

- conventional-science evidence ledger with primary-source preference;
- supernova functional-dependency map;
- SN 1987A DFM test-case analysis;
- constrained initialization model options;
- explicit falsifiers/failure conditions;
- candidate novel predictions or discriminators;
- recommendation for integration into the DFM formal framework and BWM cosmology interface.

## Relationship to WP-002

WP-002 owns the general distinction among observation, model-conditioned retrodiction, initialization, and actual history. WP-003 applies those constraints to information-rich astronomical states and must not weaken WP-002's no-smuggling/no-free-lunch discipline.

## Acceptance criterion

WP-003 succeeds only if it produces a constrained, evidence-responsive account of supernova-related functional state that explains more than the bare assertion that God could initialize light in transit. An unresolved result is acceptable and must be recorded as such.
