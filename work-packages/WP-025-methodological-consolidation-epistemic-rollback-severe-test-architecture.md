# WP-025 — Methodological Consolidation, Epistemic Rollback, and Severe-Test Architecture

**Status:** Active / consolidation baseline  
**Programme:** Designed Functional Maturity (DFM)  
**Purpose:** Codify methodological gains from the retrodiction/chronology exchange while correcting concessions that improperly collapsed measurement, model-conditioned reconstruction, and historical actuality.

## 1. Problem statement

Recent adversarial review materially strengthened DFM's evidential discipline, but also exposed a risk: in conceding the maturity and cross-validation of conventional deep-time reconstruction, programme language can inadvertently grant the historical interpretation itself rather than the strength of the measurements and retrodictive framework.

DFM must preserve the distinction:

```
measurement strength != reconstruction strength != historical certainty
```

and, formally:

```
O_t -> measurement -> (M,A,B) -> T_R -> historical interpretation
```

where:

- `O_t` = present observed state;
- `M` = model connecting observations to a reconstructed trajectory;
- `A` = auxiliary assumptions/hypotheses;
- `B` = boundary, calibration, and initialization conditions;
- `T_R` = model-conditioned retrodictive age;
- `T_H` = actual historical elapsed time.

Strong convergence among independently constrained `T_R` estimates can greatly strengthen the historical inference. It does not change the epistemic category of that inference or establish `T_R = T_H` by logical identity.

## 2. Canonical posture

Replace coarse formulations such as "conventional chronology is established" or unqualified statements that it "leads" with:

> Conventional deep-time reconstruction presently possesses the more developed and extensively cross-validated retrodictive framework. DFM has not yet produced a comparably developed alternative reconstruction. That asymmetry in model development and empirical testing does not, by itself, establish the identity of model-conditioned retrodictive time T_R with actual historical elapsed time T_H.

This is not evidential parity. It is epistemic precision.

## 3. Canonical methodological principles

### 3.1 Independent Burden Principle

An unresolved problem in one research programme does not discharge an explanatory burden in a competing programme.

No programme receives explanatory credit merely because its competitor has an unanswered question.

### 3.2 No Unexplained Arrow

Whenever a programme asserts `A -> B`, the arrow carries a burden of mechanism, constraints, reachable state space, rates/probabilities where applicable, viable intermediates, causal adequacy, and empirical warrant.

Time is duration, not a mechanism. "Emergence" is not a mechanism by itself. "Initialization," "Flood," "evolution," "migration," and "design" likewise do not discharge the arrow merely by naming it.

### 3.3 Compatibility Is Not Replacement

Compatibility and accommodation are weaker than independent derivation, novel prediction, and successful severe testing.

### 3.4 Causal Adequacy Is Not Causal Attribution

Showing that a cause can produce effects of class E does not establish that it produced a particular historical E1. Historical attribution requires discriminating evidence.

Intelligent agency is an empirically observed causal category capable of producing integrated information-rich artifacts and derived information-processing systems. That causal precedent does not by itself attribute biological systems, geological history, or DFM chronology to intelligent action.

### 3.5 No Free Lunch

No programme may hide explanatory cost inside initialization conditions, fitted parameters, auxiliary hypotheses, unspecified emergence, elapsed time, or unconstrained historical reconstruction and then count the resulting fit as independent explanatory success.

### 3.6 Dependency-Aware Consilience

Consilience is not homogeneity. Convergence is not perfect concordance.

Evidential weight must account for:

- actual independence of streams;
- shared assumptions;
- distinct physical mechanisms;
- calibration chains;
- initialization and boundary conditions;
- model mediation;
- known failure modes;
- discordances and their explanations;
- predictive history;
- robustness to changed assumptions.

### 3.7 Specify -> Run -> Predict -> Compare

For DFM initialization:

```
S_0 = G(Phi,L,B)
```

`Phi`, `L`, and `B` must be independently constrained enough that relevant observational structure follows from the generator rather than being encoded from the target.

Forbidden workflow:

```
OBSERVE -> FIT -> RELABEL -> CLAIM DERIVATION
```

Required workflow:

```
SPECIFY -> RUN -> PREDICT -> COMPARE
```

### 3.8 Test Both Directions

Two distinct tracks shall be maintained without evidential conversion between them.

**Track A — DFM Generative Testing**

Can independently specified DFM constraints generate observations presently interpreted as evidence of deep history?

**Track B — Conventional Trajectory Testing**

Can a specified conventional historical mechanism quantitatively generate the observed state under independently constrained physical conditions?

A Track B failure debits the tested conventional causal story. It does not automatically credit DFM. A Track A failure debits DFM. It does not automatically solve an independent conventional provenance burden.

### 3.9 Interpretive Sensitivity Principle

Radiometric chronology is a model-mediated inverse problem. A measured isotope state and a measured decay law do not, by themselves, specify historical elapsed time.

For a radiometric reconstruction, use the canonical abstraction:

```
T_R = f(O_t, lambda, S_0, H, M, A)
```

where:

- `O_t` = present measured isotope state;
- `lambda` = operationally measured decay physics;
- `S_0` = initialization conditions at the relevant reconstruction boundary;
- `H` = subsequent system history, including closure, retention, loss, migration, alteration, and thermal events;
- `M` = interpretive/reconstruction model;
- `A` = remaining auxiliaries;
- `T_R` = model-conditioned retrodictive age.

Therefore:

```
analytical precision of O_t and lambda != historical certainty of T_H
```

Sensitivity is not uniform across radiometric systems. Systems with independently constrained initialization conditions, robust closure evidence, multiple decay chains, concordance relations, and genuinely independent geological cross-checks can sharply restrict admissible reconstructions. Systems with poorly constrained initialization conditions or disturbed histories may be highly sensitive to interpretive choices.

Accordingly, DFM shall not argue merely that "radiometric dating depends on assumptions." It shall identify which initialization condition or historical auxiliary materially controls a result, quantify sensitivity where possible, and test whether independent evidence constrains that degree of freedom.

The preferred terminology is:

- **initial state** for the actual physical state `S_0`;
- **initialization conditions** for the conditions specified, inferred, or assumed when constructing the retrodictive model.

This distinction applies symmetrically to DFM and conventional reconstructions.

## 4. Epistemic rollback audit

The programme shall review existing DFM artifacts for language that inadvertently grants more than the evidence directly establishes.

Each substantive chronological statement should be classified as one of:

1. **Observation (O):** measured present-state fact.
2. **Operational physics (L):** experimentally constrained regularity.
3. **Model-conditioned reconstruction (R):** inferred prior state/trajectory conditional on model, assumptions, and boundaries.
4. **Historical/provenance claim (H):** assertion that the reconstructed trajectory was actually traversed.

Flag and revise passages that move from O/L/R to H without identifying the inferential step.

The audit must not weaken strong evidence merely because it is inferential. Its purpose is correct epistemic labeling.

## 5. Weighted dependency graph

For each major chronological claim, record:

| Field | Question |
|---|---|
| Observation | What is directly measured? |
| Measurement method | How is it measured? |
| Physical mechanism | What operational physics is invoked? |
| Model | What maps the measurement to prior state/trajectory? |
| Shared assumptions | Which assumptions recur across streams? |
| Calibration | What calibration chain is required? |
| Initialization conditions | What initial isotope/mineral/physical conditions are specified or inferred? |
| Subsequent history | What closure/retention/environmental history matters? |
| Auxiliaries | What additional hypotheses are used? |
| Failure modes | How can the system depart from ideal behavior? |
| Independent cross-checks | Which checks use genuinely distinct physics/data? |
| Predictive history | What was predicted before the target observation? |
| Discordance | What residual disagreement remains? |
| Conventional interpretation | What history is inferred? |
| DFM treatment | What does DFM independently derive or fail to derive? |
| Discriminator | What observation would separate accounts? |
| Status | Current evidential appraisal |

Shared assumptions are counted once, not once per dependent measurement.

## 6. Clocked-skeleton severe-test matrix

The phrase "clocked skeleton" is not canonical DFM terminology because "clocked" can import the historical interpretation being evaluated. Use **Chronological Constraint Network (CCN)**.

Initial high-priority CCN families:

1. multi-chain and multi-system radiometric relationships, beginning with U-Pb concordance;
2. independently warranted annual or near-annual layering and cross-calibration;
3. superposition/cross-cutting relationships tied to quantitative chronology;
4. fossil succession, genomic branching, ERV distributions, and biogeographic correspondence;
5. astronomical lookback relationships and cosmological chronological inference.

For each family, construct the dependency graph before making programme-level claims.

## 7. Severe-test rule for radiometric systems

DFM shall not initialize isotope systems using the conventional age as a hidden input and then count recovery of that age as a prediction.

For U-Pb, if initialized ratios are selected from:

```
206Pb/238U = exp(lambda_238 T_R) - 1
207Pb/235U = exp(lambda_235 T_R) - 1
```

solely because a selected `T_R` places the system on conventional concordia, the result is accommodation, not derivation.

The open severe-test question remains:

> Can independently constrained initialization conditions produce decay-constant-indexed concordance without elapsed retrodictive age functioning as an initialization surrogate?

Failure is to be recorded as failure.

## 8. Auxiliary-hypothesis grading

Retain the symmetric taxonomy:

- **A1:** independently observed mechanism;
- **A2:** independently constrained mechanism;
- **A3:** model-derived auxiliary producing novel predictions;
- **A4:** anomaly-motivated auxiliary subsequently corroborated;
- **A5:** anomaly-motivated accommodation without independent corroboration;
- **A6:** unconstrained rescue hypothesis.

Classification follows evidential status, not programme ownership.

## 9. Track B geological causal audits

Conventional formation histories may be decomposed into constituent arrows and tested quantitatively without treating a failure as automatic evidence for DFM.

Petroleum systems provide a useful exemplar:

```
SOURCE
-> GENERATION
-> EXPULSION
-> MIGRATION
-> ACCUMULATION
-> TRAPPING
-> PRESERVATION
-> PRESENT OBSERVATION
```

Candidate constraints include:

- kerogen/source characterization;
- laboratory kinetics and thermal history;
- source-reservoir correlation and biomarkers;
- permeability and pressure gradients;
- expulsion efficiency;
- carrier pathways;
- migration distance and timing;
- trap formation relative to charge;
- fluid inclusions and diagenetic cements;
- seal integrity and lifetime;
- diffusion and seepage;
- preservation;
- present reservoir architecture.

A failed migration model debits that migration model unless the failure also independently constrains the chronological network. It does not automatically establish a compressed history.

Conversely, the conventional model does not receive a causal arrow merely because its endpoints are compatible with the accepted chronology.

## 10. Generative-sufficiency ledger

Chronological warrant and generative sufficiency remain distinct.

A comprehensive undirected provenance account carries burdens such as:

```
specified early physical state
+ laws
+ undirected dynamics
+ elapsed time
-> life-sustaining and life-producing universe
```

Each material arrow must be audited independently.

Strong chronology does not by itself establish abiogenesis, biological coding, or any other mechanistically unresolved transition.

Likewise, an unresolved generative transition does not establish DFM chronology.

## 11. Progressive evidential ladder

Use:

```
compatibility
< accommodation
< generic coherence
< process-specific sequential consilience
< independent derivation
< novel prediction
< successful severe test
```

Dependency-aware weighting applies at every level.

## 12. Research-programme status

Canonical appraisal:

> DFM has not yet independently derived enough of the process-specific chronological record to replace the conventional deep-time reconstruction. The conventional framework is presently more developed and more extensively cross-validated as a retrodictive framework. That asymmetry does not, by itself, establish T_R = T_H. DFM remains an active alternative provenance research programme whose standing must rise or fall through independently specified derivations, discriminating predictions, and severe tests rather than mere compatibility with observation.

Avoid language that either:

- grants historical actuality merely from retrodictive success; or
- treats inferential status as though it makes strong conventional evidence weak.

## 13. Failure and progression criteria

DFM degenerates if it:

- repeatedly substitutes "initialized that way" for derivation;
- uses conventional ages as hidden initialization parameters;
- multiplies A5/A6 auxiliaries without corroboration;
- forbids no observations;
- cannot generate discriminating predictions;
- encodes apparent history datum by datum;
- repeatedly fails central severe tests without theoretical progress.

DFM progresses if it:

- reduces free initialization parameters;
- independently derives previously accommodated structure;
- predicts novel observations;
- explains known discordances without post hoc fitting;
- survives severe tests;
- identifies observations forbidden by the model.

The same auxiliary and causal standards apply when auditing conventional historical mechanisms.

## 15. Canonical maxims

> Measurement establishes state. Retrodiction reconstructs trajectory. Provenance warrants history.

> Physical maturity is a state property. Historical age is a trajectory property.

> Radiometric age is reconstructed, not directly measured.

> Initialization conditions and subsequent system history are part of the inference, not part of the measurement.

> Analytical precision does not erase interpretive sensitivity.

> Consilience is not homogeneity.

> Convergence is not perfect concordance.

> Build the dependency graph.

> No programme receives explanatory credit merely because its competitor has an unanswered question.

> No unexplained arrow.

> Compatibility is not replacement.

> Causal adequacy is not causal attribution.

> No free lunch.

> Test both directions, but keep the ledgers separate.

> Entering the test is not passing the test. Leading the test is not ending the test.

## 16. Exit criteria

WP-025 is complete when:

- the methodological principles are canonical in programme governance;
- the epistemic rollback audit is complete;
- the CCN template exists and at least one severe-test family is instantiated;
- Track A and Track B are explicitly separated;
- auxiliary grading is uniformly applied;
- programme language consistently distinguishes O, L, R, and H;
- the flagship and public-facing materials no longer over-concede historical interpretation or overclaim DFM evidence;
- subsequent work packages can inherit this methodology without reconstructing the debate.
