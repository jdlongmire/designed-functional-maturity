# WP-037: Creation Time Coordinates and DFM Day Four Integration

**Status:** Proposed, pending PI review. **Date:** 2026-11-01.  
**Parents:** WP-028, WP-029, WP-031, WP-032, WP-034.  
**Epistemic classification:** Layer 3 candidate implementation, not accepted physical mechanism.

## Objective
Evaluate whether Creation Time Coordinates (CTC), as proposed by Tenev, Baumgardner and Horstemeyer (2018), can contribute a mathematically valid creation hypersurface or preferred foliation to Designed Functional Maturity (DFM)'s **Day Four cosmological deployment**. Preserve DFM's requirement for genuine cosmic histories and distinguish coordinate synchrony from invariant accumulated proper time.

DFM places cosmological deployment on terrestrial Day Four (Genesis 1:14–19). WP-028 combines necessary coherent initialization with genuine asynchronous process execution, synchronization/commissioning, and ordinary runtime. WP-029 governs Day Four severe testing. WP-032 prohibits fabricated event-specific records. WP-034 assigns external-cosmos supernova events to genuine Day Four deployment rather than ad hoc initialized pseudo-history.

## Hypothesis separation
- **H1, coordinate description:** CTC is an admissible time-coordinate and hypersurface construction. This does not establish unique physical simultaneity.
- **H2, boundary construction:** A CTC-inspired hypersurface provides coherent Day Four initialization or *completion/commissioning* conditions.
- **H3, physical asynchronous deployment:** A specified metric and dynamics yield real cosmic process histories with substantial proper-time accumulation while Earth experiences one creation day.
- **H4, empirical discrimination:** The integrated model predicts at least one quantitative observation not equally expected from a standard FLRW comparator.

Each hypothesis must be tested separately; H1 does not entail H2–H4.

## Workstream A: Primary-source and mathematical audit
1. Obtain and transcribe the primary CTC paper's actual coordinate map, metric, hypersurface equations and figure definitions; no equation is to be inferred from secondary figure descriptions.
2. Specify Earth and stellar worldlines, creation/emission/reception events, null geodesics, and spacelike/timelike/null intervals. Verify every asserted light-cone placement against the relevant Earth event.
3. Distinguish coordinate time from proper time using Δτ = ∫ sqrt(-g_ab dx^a dx^b)/c along timelike worldlines (signature -+++). Confirm that causal order and physical clock readings are invariant under coordinate changes.
4. Determine whether a smooth, globally consistent foliation exists, whether curved spacetime is required, and whether initial data satisfy relevant physical constraints.
5. Investigate CTC hypersurface as a Day Four *synchronization/commissioning* boundary rather than only a stellar creation boundary.

## Workstream B: Genuine cosmic process and signal integrity
Evaluate real stellar evolution, nucleosynthesis, supernovae, remnants, eclipsing binaries, light curves, redshift and radiation propagation as a coupled history. SN 1987A is a required case: explosion, progenitor, neutrino/light timing, echoes and remnant must refer to genuine events. No preloaded photons encoding events that never occurred. Do not infer a young galaxy from an SNR count without rate, lifetime, environment and completeness analysis.

## Workstream C: Symmetric comparator
Compare (1) CTC as published, (2) CTC plus explicit asynchronous DFM dynamics, (3) other DFM Day Four candidates, and (4) standard FLRW/ΛCDM with disclosed assumptions and boundary conditions. Apply equal standards for logical coherence, fit, predictive discrimination, parameter burden, and falsification. Neither methodological naturalism nor biblical chronology is counted as independent observational confirmation.

## D3 discriminator and failure criteria
Preregister a candidate deployment-specific prediction before tuning against observations. Screen source time dilation, redshift-distance/chronometer consistency, cosmic isotropy residuals and cross-domain process-time correlations. These are candidate domains, **not established predictions**. Record model equations, prior parameter bounds, data provenance, uncertainty, holdouts, comparison likelihood, and failure threshold.

**Fail as a physical implementation** if it only relabels timestamps; cannot produce required proper-time history; violates invariant causality; requires nonexistent event records; lacks a coherent commissioning boundary; or survives only through observation-specific auxiliaries. If no distinguishing observation can be derived, record the D3 null result as an explanatory debit.

## Deliverables and acceptance gates
- A1 primary-source equation and figure ledger.
- A2 invariant-versus-coordinate causal audit.
- A3 metric/initial-value and synchronization feasibility memorandum.
- B1 SN 1987A and coupled-observables matrix.
- C1 symmetric comparator and auxiliary-cost ledger.
- D3 preregistered prediction or documented null result.
- PI decision: accept as component, revise, reject as mechanism, or retain as coordinate interpretation.

Acceptance requires reproducible mathematics, genuine event provenance, quantified proper-time dynamics if claimed, observational testing, and recorded negative results. This WP makes **no claim** that CTC is already validated or that DFM's hard core has changed.
