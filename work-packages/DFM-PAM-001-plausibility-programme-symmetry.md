# DFM-PAM-001: Plausibility and Programme Symmetry Assessment

**Status:** Proposed governance and evaluation framework, 2026-10-27.  
**Scope:** DFM radiogenic research, extensible to the full DFM programme.  
**Parents:** WP-002, WP-004, WP-005, RCIM-001 through RCIM-003.

## Purpose
Develop a scientifically informed *plausibility and explanatory accommodation* model before requiring comparative superiority. DFM may propose a specially initialized physical state; this is not itself a conventional natural mechanism. All subsequent ordinary physics, including decay and geochemical transport, must remain coherent. Plausibility does not imply truth, confirmation, or evidential parity with established geological accounts.

## Two-track maturity architecture

**Track A: Plausibility and explanatory adequacy**
A1. Logical coherence: no internal contradiction among initial-state assumptions, chronology, measured decay, and subsequent dynamics.
A2. Physical coherence: no unaddressed violations of conservation constraints, thermal budget, mineral physics, or observable present-state boundary conditions. A special-creation boundary may stipulate initial inventories, but not evade post-boundary physics.
A3. Explanatory coverage: construct one coupled account of major observations: parent/daughter inventories, dual-chain U-Pb concordance, K-Ar/Ar-Ar patterns, heat, mineral distributions, inheritance and alteration, and independent event constraints.
A4. Auxiliary discipline: initial daughter inventories, source distributions, open-system events, and functional constraints must be explicitly specified, justified, and bounded; unresolved areas remain recorded.
A5. Plausibility decision: **provisional**, **conditional**, **not yet established**, or **contradicted**. A conditional result must name the outstanding critical conditions. A lack of demonstrated impossibility alone is insufficient for a positive plausibility finding.

**Track B: Discriminatory and comparative testing**
B1. Derive predictions from a shared initialization generator.
B2. Test on independently held-out measurements and cross-system observations.
B3. Compare against conventional geological models with their recognized initial-daughter, closure, inheritance and mixing corrections.
B4. Evaluate predictive skill, auxiliary complexity, calibration, and failure recovery.
Track B is a progressive research objective, not a prerequisite for a provisional Track A assessment.

## Programme symmetry rule
Apply the same *kinds* of scrutiny to every programme, without pretending that every auxiliary has identical independent evidential support:
- State core commitments, boundary assumptions, and protective-belt auxiliaries explicitly.
- Permit physically motivated auxiliary explanations in both programmes.
- Require quantitative accounting for relevant observations, including known-age calibration and cross-system concordance.
- Record independent evidence, degrees of freedom, and unresolved anomalies.
- Reject unrestricted post-hoc fitting in both programmes.
- Distinguish accommodation of evidence from independent prediction and corroboration.

An initialized daughter inventory is not automatically inadmissible because it is initialized. Nor does its permissibility establish that a specific distribution is plausible or that the resulting model has equal empirical standing.

|---|---|---|---|
| Initial conditions | Can a coherent, bounded parent/daughter state be specified? | Are initial daughter assumptions and corrections independently supported? | DFM: not yet quantified |
| Decay physics | Does ordinary post-boundary decay hold? | Does ordinary decay hold? | Common physical baseline |
| Radiogenic heat | Does the model satisfy post-boundary heat flow and thermal constraints? | Does the historical thermal reconstruction satisfy them? | DFM: open |

## Track A evidence ledger
Each proposed accommodation must document:
1. **Observation:** measured quantity and uncertainty, separately from interpreted age.
2. **Conventional account:** actual published explanation, not a simplified caricature.
3. **DFM account:** initial-state or post-boundary mechanism with equations and parameters where feasible.
4. **Independent rationale:** why the auxiliary is physically or functionally motivated, beyond matching the target.
5. **Boundaries:** parameter ranges, conservation and thermal checks, geological constraints.
6. **Residual:** unexplained observations and conflicts.
7. **Maturity:** conceptual / analytical / calibrated / empirically checked.
8. **Next gate:** smallest quantitative test that could resolve the uncertainty.

## Guardrails
- Do not require unique isotopic necessity or prove historical provenance from present utility.
- Do not declare Track A success simply because an arbitrary initial condition could fit any observation.
- Do not require Track B superiority before allowing Track A provisional plausibility.
- Do not dismiss independent conventional evidence merely because it is interpreted through a research programme.
- Keep scientific plausibility, theological warrant, and historical truth claims distinct.

## Immediate execution sequence
1. Populate the evidence ledger for U-Pb paired-chain concordance, K-Ar/Ar-Ar, radiogenic heat, and known-age controls.
2. Use RCIM-002 forward equations to demonstrate coupled internally consistent example states, clearly labeled illustrative.
3. Establish explicit bounded parameter families and the conditions under which each observation can be accommodated.
4. Issue a *conditional Track A assessment* identifying supported and unresolved dimensions.
5. Advance selected models into Track B only when sufficient empirical datasets and frozen parameters exist.
