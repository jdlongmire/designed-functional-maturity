# WP-007 Primary-Source Evidence Ledger

**Status:** Baseline source pass complete  
**Scope:** Flagship DFM claims most relevant to radiogenic inference and the immediate U-Pb severe-test gate.

## Evidence standard

Priority is given to primary experimental or analytical literature. Review literature is used only where it synthesizes a field-level quantity not conveniently represented by one experiment.

DFM does not treat conventional interpretation as raw observation. Conversely, it does not dismiss experimental controls merely because they are used inside conventional chronology.

| Claim / issue | Primary source | What the source establishes | DFM use |
|---|---|---|---|
| U-235 and U-238 decay constants are experimentally measurable | Jaffey et al. (1971) | Precision measurements of U-235 and U-238 half-lives and specific activities | Supports ordinary-decay default. DFM dispute is not with existence of decay. |

## Resulting DFM constraints

The primary-source pass supports the following controlled statements:

1. **Decay physics is not the DFM escape hatch.** U decay is directly measured and sufficiently stable for DFM to retain ordinary prospective decay as its default.
2. **Radiogenic heat is a real functional constraint.** U/Th/K inventory is coupled to Earth's thermal architecture, although exact whole-Earth partition remains model-sensitive.
3. **U-Pb is a severe test.** Zircon chemistry, dual U decay chains, concordia structure, Pb retention, and diagnosable disturbance make simplistic initial-daughter objections inadequate.
4. **Concordance is empirical structure.** Meteorite and cross-method examples show that convergence cannot be dismissed as mere repetition of one assumption.
5. **Chronometry is strongest where provenance is independently constrained.** Known-event or stratigraphically constrained cases deserve to be preserved as genuine post-initialization chronometers.
6. **The DFM research question survives but narrows:** whether the oldest concordant structures uniquely warrant a traversed pre-initialization chronology, or whether a quantitatively constrained coherent initialization can generate them without target-age fitting.

## WP-008 handoff

The U-Pb severe-test model must include at minimum:

- both U-238/Pb-206 and U-235/Pb-207 systems;
- measured decay constants;
- zircon U incorporation and Pb exclusion constraints;
- common Pb treatment;
- Pb diffusion/retention and radiation-damage pathways;
- concordia/discordia geometry;
- independently constrained initialization degrees of freedom;
- a prohibition on target-age initialization;
- comparison against actual high-quality zircon datasets;
- an explicit failure criterion.
