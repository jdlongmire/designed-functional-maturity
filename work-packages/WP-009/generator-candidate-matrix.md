## Decision

No candidate presently satisfies all requirements for an age-independent DFM concordia generator.

The most promising research direction is not grain-level initialization but a **reservoir-level generator** whose parameters are independently constrained and whose predictions propagate into multiple isotope systems and mineral populations.
