# WP-029A: SN 1987A Causal Ledger and First Hypothesis Competition

**Parent:** WP-029 Day 4 Causal Architecture and Severe-Test Programme  
**Status:** Initial evidence/competition baseline  
**Purpose:** Use SN 1987A as the first high-coupling exemplar for discriminating initialized state, genuine asynchronous deployment history, ordinary runtime history, and hybrid allocation.

## Why SN 1987A

SN 1987A is not a single travel-time datum. The observational package contains mutually constraining phenomena: a detected stellar transient; neutrinos associated with core collapse; circumstellar rings that predate the observed explosion under the standard reconstruction; expanding ejecta; blast-wave interaction with the ring; multiwavelength evolution; radioactive cobalt signatures; dust formation; light echoes; and evidence consistent with a compact remnant.

A viable DFM allocation should therefore preserve a causal network rather than reproduce an isolated appearance.

## Initial allocation pressure

The coupled observations create a strong methodological presumption against explaining SN 1987A solely as an initialized photon stream or isolated apparent event.

That is not a proof that every antecedent stage occurred under ordinary-duration runtime. It is evidence that a successful DFM model should prefer genuine causal execution wherever the observations form an independently coupled event network.

Working allocation:

```text
initialized cosmic/source boundary
        +
genuine deployment-time antecedent process where required
        ->
genuine collapse/explosion
        ->
genuine nucleosynthesis / remnant / emitted signals
        ->
propagation
        ->
ordinary Earth observation and subsequent remnant evolution
```

The open problem is the temporal mapping between the genuine remote causal chain and Earth-local creation chronology, not whether every observed consequence should be treated as fabricated event history.

## Candidate competition

### H3-001 Relativistic differential proper time

**Potential strength:** differential proper time is physically established in relativity and supplies a non-Newtonian conceptual basis.

**Current weakness:** no DFM model yet derives the required magnitude/profile, spatial architecture, source-to-Earth propagation relation, or Day 4 synchronization.

**Initial score:** physically motivated / not yet explanatory.

### H3-002 Creation-specific metric or foliation

**Potential strength:** could explicitly relate the terrestrial Genesis reference domain to cosmic deployment domains and define a commissioning hypersurface.

**Current weakness:** currently underdetermined; risks becoming a preferred-frame assertion without independent physical specification.

**Initial score:** candidate architecture / high development burden.

### H3-003 Anisotropic propagation or synchronization

**Potential strength:** may address source-to-observer timing without requiring identical temporal accumulation everywhere.

**Current weakness:** propagation alone does not explain the full antecedent stellar, circumstellar, nucleosynthetic, and remnant causal network.

**Initial score:** potentially contributory, unlikely sufficient alone.

### H3-004 Coupled temporal domains

**Potential strength:** maps naturally onto the both-and architecture and can in principle assign genuine process depth to different causally coupled domains.

**Current weakness:** presently formal rather than physical. Coupling laws and synchronization conditions are unspecified.

**Initial score:** strongest architectural fit / mechanism open.

### H3-005 Temporal-domain translation

**Potential strength:** explicitly recognizes that cross-domain signals may impose transformation constraints.

**Current weakness:** easily becomes an unconstrained repair layer. Must derive, not tune, effects on spectra, energy, phase, neutrinos, photons, and gravitational phenomena.

**Initial score:** useful conditional sub-hypothesis / not a standalone solution.

## First comparative result

No Layer 3 candidate presently earns a severe-test pass.

The leading architecture for further development is **H3-004 coupled temporal domains**, not because it is established physics, but because it most directly represents the causal problem without prematurely selecting a mechanism. H3-001 and H3-002 are plausible implementation families beneath it. H3-003 and H3-005 appear more naturally as contributory mechanisms than complete architectures.

This ranking is provisional and must not be promoted to DFM doctrine.

## Severe-test requirements derived from SN 1987A

A viable Day 4 implementation should preserve, with one constrained architecture:

1. causal association between collapse and neutrino emission;
2. causal association between explosion and optical/other electromagnetic emission;
3. circumstellar geometry and subsequent shock interaction;
4. ejecta expansion and remnant evolution;
5. radioactive/nucleosynthetic signatures;
6. light-echo geometry;
7. consistency of signal propagation across relevant messengers;
8. continuity into the decades of ordinary observed evolution after 1987.

## Key discriminator

> **The more independently coupled consequences an observation contains, the less explanatory freedom DFM has to allocate those consequences independently.**

This is a severe-test advantage rather than a liability. High-coupling observations constrain the initialization/deployment allocation and reduce arbitrary degrees of freedom.

## Epistemic status

This ledger supports genuine causal history for substantial portions of the SN 1987A phenomenon more strongly than a pure initialized-signal account. It does not establish ordinary-duration antecedent history, a particular Day 4 temporal mechanism, or the truth of the asynchronous auxiliary.

### Observation versus reconstruction discipline

Several widely quoted quantities are model-derived rather than direct clock readings. For example, the statement that the equatorial ring was expelled roughly 20,000 years before the explosion is an inference from ring geometry, kinematics, and stellar-evolution modeling. WP-029 records such values as **R: reconstructed** unless the measurement itself directly establishes elapsed duration.

The same rule applies to the approximately 160,000-light-year source distance and any conversion of observed geometry into ordinary-runtime travel history. These remain important quantitative constraints, but the mapping from state to elapsed history is precisely what the Day 4 auxiliary places under test.

### Invariants before mechanism

Candidate temporal models are not permitted to tune Q1-Q10 independently. At minimum they must preserve:

```text
collapse <-> neutrino burst
collapse/explosion -> radioactive products
explosion -> ejecta expansion
explosion -> propagating shock -> ring interaction
explosion -> radiation -> light-echo geometry
explosion -> remnant evolution -> compact remnant signatures
```

Here arrows express causal ordering, not yet a commitment to ordinary-duration elapsed time.

### First constraint on temporal mappings

Let a candidate implementation map deployment-domain intervals to Earth-local observational intervals through a relation `F_i`.

A minimally acceptable model must preserve causal order:

```text
A precedes B  =>  F(A) does not reverse B before A
```

and coupled-event identity:

```text
one collapse event
  -> one mutually consistent neutrino / electromagnetic / ejecta / remnant causal family
```

A model that requires a different unconstrained `F_i` for each messenger or each observational node fails the common-architecture criterion.

### Immediate mathematical target

The next formal task is therefore not to choose a numerical acceleration factor. It is to determine the smallest transformation family capable of preserving the observed causal DAG:

```text
F : {tau_C,i, x, signal class, deployment state} -> {tau_E-observable relations}
```

subject to causal-order preservation, cross-messenger consistency, spectral/energy constraints, geometric consistency, and a single synchronization/commissioning boundary.
