# WP-019: Equation and Variable Explanation Sweep

**Status:** Executed
**Type:** Notation governance / documentation quality

## Objective

Ensure that displayed equations and formal expressions are locally intelligible: variables, indices, operators, and specialized symbols are explained at first meaningful use rather than requiring the reader to reconstruct notation from another document.

## Rule

For every substantive displayed equation:

1. Define each non-obvious variable and index at first meaningful use.
2. Define specialized operators or functions when their meaning is not standard in context.
3. State units or physical interpretation when material.
4. Re-define symbols when a document is intended to stand alone.
5. Do not burden trivial logical propositions with unnecessary notation glosses.
6. A central glossary may supplement but never replace local definitions.

## Sweep coverage

Audited the public Pages source, README, PROGRAMME, flagship draft, and formal radiogenic WPs. Corrections were applied to the core deployment/retrodiction equations and to high-density U-Pb, reservoir/mass-balance, common-generator, and decay-constant-indexed concordance material.

## Canonical editorial standard

> A displayed equation should be understandable from its immediate section without requiring the reader to search another file for the meaning of its symbols.

Future WPs introducing notation inherit this requirement.
