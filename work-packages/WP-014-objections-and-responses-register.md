# WP-014: Objections and Responses Register

**Status:** Executed  
**Type:** Programme governance / adversarial testing  
**Depends on:** WP-013

## Objective

Create a canonical objections-and-responses register for DFM so objections, responses, unresolved burdens, and potential defeaters accumulate as controlled programme knowledge rather than being repeatedly reconstructed in papers and discussions.

## Requirements

Each objection receives:

- stable OAR identifier;
- objection class;
- strongest fair formulation;
- target hard-core proposition, auxiliary, or inference;
- DFM response;
- residual explanatory burden;
- evidence or analysis needed;
- disposition;
- links to relevant work packages where applicable.

## Dispositions

- **Answered:** the objection identifies no unresolved logical or evidential burden material to the targeted claim.
- **Partially Answered:** the central objection has a response, but a meaningful explanatory burden remains.
- **Open Burden:** DFM has not yet supplied a sufficient answer.
- **Severe Challenge:** the objection directly pressures a major auxiliary or programme claim and requires targeted testing.
- **Auxiliary Defeater:** evidence defeats or requires abandonment of an auxiliary without necessarily defeating the hard core.
- **Hard-Core Challenge:** objection directly targets a hard-core commitment and cannot be resolved merely by protective-belt revision.

Canonical burden language:

> This objection does not defeat the argument, but it does identify a genuine explanatory burden that remains.

## Governance rule

An OAR entry must state the strongest version of the objection before presenting the DFM response. Entries may not be closed merely because DFM is logically possible. Empirical and explanatory burdens remain open until independently constrained work addresses them.

Papers and work packages should reference OAR IDs rather than silently changing the canonical response.

## Deliverables

1. Root `OBJECTIONS-AND-RESPONSES.md`.
2. Initial seeded objection set.
3. README and PROGRAMME links.
4. WP index entry.
