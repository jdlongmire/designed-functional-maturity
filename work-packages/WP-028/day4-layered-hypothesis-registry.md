# WP-028-HR: Day 4 Layered Hypothesis Registry

**Parent:** WP-028 Initialization + Asynchronous Deployment Refactor  
**Status:** Active registry  
**Purpose:** Controlled inventory of Layer 2-4 hypotheses. Inclusion is not endorsement.

## Status vocabulary

- **Retain:** presently warranted at its assigned layer.
- **Active:** under development/testing.
- **Candidate:** admissible research hypothesis requiring development.
- **Deferred:** legitimate but not current priority.
- **Rejected:** excluded from current DFM.
- **Superseded:** replaced while retained in the audit trail.

## Registry schema

Each hypothesis records: ID, layer, proposition, motivation, dependencies, free parameters, explanatory targets, discriminators, failure conditions, and status.

## Layer 2: deployment auxiliaries

### D4-H2-001: Asynchronous Day 4 deployment

**Proposition:** During the terrestrial Day 4 interval, one or more non-terrestrial physical domains/processes accumulated elapsed/process time different from Earth-local elapsed time before entering ordinary runtime.

**Formal minimum:** `Delta tau_C,i != Delta tau_E`  
**Stronger candidate:** `Delta tau_C,i >> Delta tau_E`

**Motivation:** Preserve an ordinary terrestrial creation day while permitting genuine causally deep cosmic process history.

**Dependencies:** DFM Initialization-Deployment Complementarity; terrestrial-day chronology.

**Free parameters:** number/extent of temporal domains; differential relation; duration/profile; coupling; synchronization boundary.

**Targets:** stellar histories, supernovae, radiation propagation, galactic organization, nucleosynthesis.

**Discriminators:** common architecture across independent targets; preservation of causal correlations; quantitative synchronization.

**Failure conditions:** observation-specific clock tuning; broken causal coupling; no coherent synchronization; contradictions with independent observations.

**Status:** Active auxiliary.

### D4-H2-002: Mixed provenance allocation

**Proposition:** A single observed cosmic system may contain both directly initialized state and genuinely executed deployment history.

**Motivation:** Avoid false initialization-versus-history dichotomy.

**Dependencies:** Initialization-Deployment Complementarity.

**Targets:** supernova systems, stellar populations, radiation fields, large-scale structure.

**Failure conditions:** allocation becomes arbitrary or cannot be independently constrained.

**Status:** Retain.

## Layer 3: candidate physical implementations

### D4-H3-001: Relativistic differential proper-time implementation

**Proposition:** A physically explicit spacetime architecture can produce the required differential elapsed times using relativistic proper-time relations.

**Disposition note:** Single-metric Family A tests established mathematical feasibility but exposed coupled propagation, spectral, spatial-geometry, source, and matching costs. Homogeneous conformal and single-field non-conformal realizations did not provide a parsimonious mechanism under the WP-029 stop rule. Retain as a control/feasibility family and reopen only on new independently motivated structure.

**Status:** Demoted to constrained feasibility/control family.

### D4-H3-002: Creation-specific metric or foliation implementation

**Proposition:** The creation-deployment regime includes a physically meaningful metric structure or preferred creation foliation capable of coordinating Earth-local chronology with non-terrestrial process time.

**Status:** Candidate.

### D4-H3-003: Anisotropic propagation/synchronization implementation

**Proposition:** Non-standard but physically coherent propagation or synchronization relationships contribute to reconciling Day 4 deployment with terrestrial observation.

**Status:** Candidate.

### D4-H3-004: Coupled temporal-domain implementation

**Proposition:** Multiple physical domains/process classes execute with distinct but coupled temporal parameters during deployment and converge at a common commissioning boundary.

**Status:** Candidate.

### D4-H3-005: Temporal-domain translation

**Proposition:** Transfer between asynchronous deployment domains and Earth-local observation involves a constrained transformation preserving observable frequency, energy, phase, or related signal properties.

**Motivation:** Explore whether cross-domain signals require an explicit translation relation.

**Warning:** This is not established and carries substantial spectral, redshift, propagation, conservation, neutrino, and gravitational-wave burdens.

**Status:** Candidate.

## Layer 4: observation-specific hypotheses

### D4-H4-001: Supernova mixed causal history

A supernova observation may combine initialized source/macrostate with genuine deployment-time stellar evolution, explosion, nucleosynthesis, remnant formation, and signal propagation.

**Status:** Active exemplar.

### D4-H4-002: CMB deployment/initiation signature

The CMB may contain information attributable partly to initialization or extraordinary deployment rather than exclusively to conventional antecedent cosmic history.

**Status:** Candidate. No DFM conclusion presently established.

### D4-H4-003: Dark-matter initialization role

Some dark-matter phenomenology may reflect initialized structural conditions required for mature cosmic organization.

**Status:** Candidate. No DFM conclusion presently established.

### D4-H4-004: Dark-energy boundary/metric role

Some dark-energy phenomenology may relate to initialized boundary or metric conditions.

**Status:** Candidate. No DFM conclusion presently established.

### D4-H4-005: Redshift/spectral transition constraint

Observed redshift and spectral structure may constrain or discriminate among temporal-domain implementations and any proposed transition/translation relation.

**Status:** Active constraint, not an explanatory conclusion.

### D4-H4-006: Nucleosynthetic process-depth allocation

Observed elemental/isotopic inventories may discriminate between initialized inventory, genuine deployment-time nucleosynthesis, and hybrid allocation.

**Status:** Candidate severe-test domain.

## Explicit exclusions

The following are not restored by this registry:

- accelerated nuclear decay;
- a mandatory white-hole cosmology;
- 2 Peter 3:8 as a technical temporal mechanism;
- a post-Day-4 immutable block universe;
- a fixed physical script determining every future event.

## Registry governance

New hypotheses are welcome when they occupy an explicit layer and state their dependencies and failure conditions. Novelty is not a methodological defect. Hypotheses may branch and compete.

No Layer 3 or Layer 4 hypothesis may be promoted merely because it protects Layer 0-2 commitments. Promotion requires increased independent constraint, explanatory integration, empirical consequence, and severe-test performance.

Rejected and superseded hypotheses remain recorded for auditability.
