# WP-006: Flagship DFM Paper Rebuild

**Status:** Executed baseline  
**Parent:** DFM Research Programme  
**Canonical input:** `PROGRAMME.md`  
**Target:** `drafts/designed-functional-maturity.md`

## Purpose

Replace the legacy synthesis paper with a programme-faithful flagship manuscript incorporating the formal initialization model, Reconstruction Argument, radiogenic coherent-initialization hypothesis, predictions, severe tests, objections, and unresolved explanatory burdens.

## Problems in legacy draft

The previous manuscript:

- predated WP-002 and WP-004;
- treated DFM primarily as a broad critique of methodological naturalism;
- did not formally distinguish retrodictive and historical age;
- did not contain the Reconstruction Argument;
- did not incorporate the ordinary-decay firewall;
- did not contain the radiogenic heat -> coherent initialization -> orderly-marker architecture;
- did not expose the quantitative concordance burden clearly enough;
- mixed broad topics such as consciousness and macroevolution into a paper whose strongest current development is physical initialization;
- lacked explicit severe tests and degeneration criteria;
- lacked an objections-and-responses section;
- contained an unsourced references placeholder unsuitable for publication.

## Executed changes

1. Recentered the paper on physical initialization and historical inference.
2. Made biblical boundary conditions explicit rather than claiming chronology neutrality.
3. Canonized `S_0 = G(Phi, L, B)`.
4. Added `R = R(O_t | M, A, B)` and the distinction `R != H` by logical necessity.
5. Added `T_R` versus `T_H`.
6. Added the dynamical Reconstruction Argument.
7. Added no-smuggling, no-free-lunch, anti-circularity, and symmetric-auxiliary rules.
8. Added the radiogenic inference architecture.
9. Added radiogenic heat as an independent constraint on U/Th/K initialization.
10. Added coherent radiogenic initialization and orderly-marker/chronometer distinction.
11. Explicitly limited what the heat budget can explain.
12. Elevated U-Pb and non-heat-centric systems as severe tests.
13. Preserved genuine post-initialization chronometry.
14. Added cosmological extension with explicit unsolved burdens.
15. Added programme-level predictions.
16. Added potential defeaters and degeneration criteria.
17. Added objections and responses.
18. Added an explicit outstanding-burdens section.
19. Replaced the vague references placeholder with a controlled source-development statement.
