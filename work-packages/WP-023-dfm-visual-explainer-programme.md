# WP-023: DFM Visual Explainer and Infographic Programme

**Status:** Captured / governed development
**Type:** Public communication / visual architecture
**Depends on:** WP-013, WP-016, WP-017, WP-020, WP-022

## Objective

Establish the ranked development programme, common visual grammar, epistemic guardrails, and approval process for value-added DFM viewpoints and infographics.

This WP does **not** authorize automatic production of the full graphic series. Each visual is to be developed individually with the programme owner, reviewed for conceptual accuracy, and approved before publication.

## Governing principle

DFM graphics are explanatory artifacts, not decoration.

A proposed visual earns inclusion only when it materially improves understanding of a distinction, process, dependency, inference, discriminator, or evidential burden that is less efficiently communicated by prose alone.

## Ranked visual programme

### 1. Deployment vs. Operation
**Priority:** Highest  
**Role:** Canonical gateway explainer

Core architecture:

```text
Deployment / Initialization -> Commissioning Boundary -> Ordinary Operation
          G_i                                      F_L^t
```

Variables:
- `G_i` = supernatural initialization/deployment act for phase `i`;
- `F_L^t` = subsequent ordinary state evolution under law-set `L` for elapsed time `t`.

Canonical line:

> The rules that govern a running system need not be the process by which the system was deployed.

This visual establishes the conceptual grammar inherited by the remainder of the series.

### 2. Six-Day Deployment Architecture
**Role:** Ground the systems concept explicitly in Genesis 1

Desktop form: horizontal cumulative deployment sequence.  
Mobile form: vertical sequence.

```text
S_0 -> S_1 -> S_2 -> S_3 -> S_4 -> S_5 -> S_6
```

Phase labels:
- Day 1: illumination and terrestrial temporal cadence;
- Day 2: environmental/spatial ordering described by the text;
- Day 3: land/sea ordering and vegetation;
- Day 4: astronomical governance and marking;
- Day 5: aquatic/aerial life;
- Day 6: terrestrial life and humanity.

The graphic must emphasize **state inheritance**, not six isolated panels.

### 3. State vs. History
**Role:** Primary public/apologetic epistemology graphic

Inference path:

```text
Observed State
     |
Model + Assumptions + Boundary Conditions
     |
Retrodictive Trajectory
     |
Inferred Age / History
```

Contrasted with actual provenance:

```text
Actual Provenance -> Actual Historical Trajectory -> Observed State
```

The graphic must not communicate "dating is wrong." It communicates that measurement of state and reconstruction of provenance are epistemically distinct operations.

### 4. Consilience vs. Provenance
**Role:** Address the evidential force of convergent historical reconstructions

Visualize multiple observations or clocks converging on coherent reconstruction, followed by the provenance distinction:

```text
Consilience <- coherent traversed history
           or
Consilience <- coherent initialized state
```

Canonical line:

> Consilience establishes coherence; provenance establishes history.

The graphic must acknowledge consilience as genuine evidence requiring explanation. It must not portray convergence as irrelevant.

### 5. Functional State Depth
**Role:** Explain why functional maturity is not superficial appearance

Visual concept: layered or architectural cross-section.

Visible function rests upon initialized dependencies such as:
- structural state;
- chemical state;
- biological organization;
- energy relationships;
- environmental compatibility;
- information/configuration;
- boundary conditions.

Canonical implication:

```text
commissioned function -> required coherent state depth
```

The visual should directly counter the idea that DFM means merely creating things that "look old."

### 6. Day 1 -> Day 4 Governance Handoff
**Role:** Focused Genesis 1 explainer and example of deployment/operation distinction

```text
Day 1:
God-given regulated illumination
        ->
established terrestrial day/night cadence
        ->
Day 4:
luminaries commissioned as ordinary governors/markers
```

Canonical line:

> The day precedes the Sun. The Sun is subsequently commissioned to govern the day.

Optional theological bookend:

```text
God-given light -> Sun-governed light -> God-given light
```

Genesis 1 and Revelation 21-22 may be referenced, but Revelation must not be presented as specifying the physical mechanism of Day 1 illumination.

### 7. Retrodiction Pipeline
**Role:** Technical companion to State vs. History

```text
O_t + M + A + B -> R -> T_R
```

Variables:
- `O_t` = observed state at time `t`;
- `M` = model structure;
- `A` = auxiliary assumptions;
- `B` = boundary conditions;
- `R` = reconstructed trajectory;
- `T_R` = model-conditioned retrodictive duration.

Epistemic boundary:

```text
T_R does not entail T_H
```

where `T_H` is actual elapsed historical duration.

The graphic belongs primarily to the technical track, papers, and deeper explanatory material.

## Preferred composition: 2 x 2 square grid

For public-facing and social DFM infographics, the **default composition is a square 2 x 2 grid**. The format is preferred because it is mobile-friendly, publication-friendly, visually scannable, and forces each graphic to resolve into four high-value conceptual units.

The 2 x 2 format is a default, not an absolute constraint. Use another composition when the subject is inherently sequential, cumulative, or mathematically dependent and forcing it into four panels would reduce explanatory accuracy. Likely exceptions include the six-day cumulative deployment sequence and technical retrodiction or concordance figures.

For Visual 1, the preferred prototype is:

| Panel | Function |
|---|---|
| **1. DEPLOY** | God establishes the system and coherent state required for intended function. `G_i` |
| **2. COMMISSION** | The initialized system is placed into ordinary operation. |
| **3. OPERATE** | The system subsequently behaves according to its designed regularities. `F_L^t` |
| **4. INTERPRET** | Observing those regularities does not, by itself, establish how the system was deployed. |

The canonical footer proposition is:

> THE RULES THAT GOVERN A RUNNING SYSTEM NEED NOT BE THE PROCESS BY WHICH THE SYSTEM WAS DEPLOYED.

Subtle numbering, connector treatment, or directional cues may preserve sequence across the four panels without sacrificing the square-grid composition.

## Common visual grammar

The series should inherit the BWM/DFM visual family while remaining recognizably DFM.

Provisional semantic roles:
- **Cyan:** initialized/deployed state and DFM structural relationships;
- **Amber:** provenance and extraordinary creative/deployment action;
- **Ivory/light neutral:** ordinary operation, observations, or evidential state;
- **Solid arrows:** causal/state/deployment transitions;
- **Dashed arrows:** inference, reconstruction, or retrodiction.

These are semantic conventions, not immutable color specifications. Accessibility and contrast take precedence.

Typography, dark technical palette, line treatment, and layout should remain compatible with the existing DFM Pages visual system and BWM parent identity.

## Development and approval workflow

Each visual is a separate development activity.

For every visual:

1. Define the single explanatory job.
2. Identify the canonical proposition it must communicate.
3. Identify what the graphic must **not** imply.
4. Develop the information architecture before visual styling.
5. Default to a square 2 x 2 information architecture for public-facing infographics unless the subject requires a different topology.
6. Produce an initial concept with desktop/mobile use in mind.
7. Review jointly for theological, scientific, epistemic, and visual accuracy.
8. Revise until explicitly approved.
9. Only then integrate into Pages, papers, social/publication assets, or the BWM layer as appropriate.

No graphic is considered canonical merely because it has been generated.

## Guardrails

- Scripture governs the Genesis deployment sequence; visual metaphors remain subordinate.
- Do not turn engineering analogy into an assertion that creation is literally a software or systems-engineering artifact.
- Do not visually collapse retrodiction into observation.
- Do not visually imply that logical possibility constitutes empirical confirmation.
- Do not minimize genuine consilience or severe evidence.
- Do not imply that ordinary natural operation proves exhaustive naturalistic provenance.
- Do not imply that design analogies independently prove supernatural creation.
- Unresolved burdens remain visually and textually identifiable where relevant.
- Equations and formal notation follow WP-019: variables must be locally explained.

## Publication strategy

The public landing page remains an orientation layer. Not every visual belongs on the homepage.

Candidate disposition:
- Homepage/core explainer: #1, selected form of #2 or #3.
- Accessible/deeper pages: #2 through #6.
- Technical track/papers: #7 and subsequent Wave 3 figures.
- Social/blog derivatives may simplify a canonical graphic but must preserve its claim boundary.

## Immediate next action

Begin with **Visual 1: Deployment vs. Operation** as the design prototype for the series.

Its approved visual language should inform subsequent graphics rather than designing seven unrelated artifacts.

## Success criterion

A reader should eventually be able to recognize a DFM graphic by both its visual language and its epistemic structure, while each graphic remains understandable on its own and makes no stronger claim than the programme warrants.
