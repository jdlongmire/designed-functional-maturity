# WP-030: Minimum Required Cosmic Process Depth

**Status:** Closed / superseded as a mistaken branch of inquiry  
**Parents:** WP-028, WP-029  
**Superseded by:** WP-029 frozen asynchronous Day 4 hypothesis boundary

## Closure rationale

This work package was opened after several candidate physical realizations failed to derive the full synchronized-lapse ratio economically. It then asked whether DFM should reduce the amount of genuinely executed external cosmic history by allocating more observed states to initialization.

That question changed the hypothesis rather than testing it.

The specified asynchronous Day 4 hypothesis is:

```text
stable, encapsulated Earth
        |
        | Delta tau_E ~= one ordinary terrestrial day
        |
external cosmos genuinely executes
the current standard Big-Bang expansion
and thermal history
        |
        | Delta tau_C ~= 13.8 Gyr
        |
synchronization / commissioning
        |
ordinary common runtime
```

Accordingly, stellar evolution, white-dwarf cooling, nucleosynthesis, supernova histories, CMB development, galaxy evolution, and the other coupled consequences of the standard external cosmological history are not being examined to discover how much of that history can be initialized away.

They are requirements the asynchronous deployment architecture is intended to preserve.

## What remains useful

The clock and causal analyses developed while WP-030 was active remain useful only as **verification requirements** for candidate implementations:

- stellar clocks must remain mutually coherent;
- nuclear and stellar histories must remain consistent with the external deployment;
- multi-messenger causal networks must remain intact;
- the standard expansion/thermal history must not be broken by the Earth/cosmos temporal architecture;
- accelerated nuclear decay remains rejected.

These observations constrain candidate mechanisms. They do not redefine the deployment payload.

## Correct research question

The programme returns to WP-029:

> **What asynchronous creation-deployment architecture can permit a stable, encapsulated terrestrial reference domain to experience one ordinary Day 4 while the external cosmos executes the standard Big-Bang expansion and thermal history, followed by coherent synchronization into ordinary runtime?**

## Disposition of prior Tier-1 work

The prior stellar/nuclear clock ledger is not an active minimum-duration programme.

Its substantive observations should be reused later as severe-test checks against candidate mechanisms. No inference should be drawn from it that DFM is seeking to compress, truncate, or initialize away the stipulated external cosmic history.

## Methodological lesson

A failed implementation does not license silent movement of the hypothesis boundary.

The correct sequence is:

```text
fixed hypothesis
  -> candidate implementation
  -> severe test
  -> retain / revise / demote candidate
```

not

```text
fixed hypothesis
  -> difficult implementation
  -> weaken the hypothesis
```

WP-030 is retained as an audit record of that correction.
