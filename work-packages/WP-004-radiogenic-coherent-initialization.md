# WP-004: Radiogenic Coherent Initialization and Orderly-Marker Hypothesis

**Status:** Executed — baseline  
**Parent:** Designed Functional Maturity (DFM) Research Programme  
**Depends on:** WP-002 Retrodiction, Initialization, and Radiogenic Inference  
**Target:** Canonical DFM model revision plus testable radiogenic-concordance framework

## 1. Purpose

Promote the radiogenic heat budget and coherent radionuclide initialization from distributed DFM considerations into an explicit model component, and formalize the distinction between radiometric concordance as an orderly marker of a coherent initialized state and its stronger interpretation as a chronometer of elapsed pre-initialization history.

This WP does not deny measured isotope ratios, experimentally established decay constants, mineral chemistry, concordia/isochron structure, or ordinary radioactive decay after initialization. It asks what additional warrant is required to identify a retrodictive age with actual historical duration.

## 2. Governing initialization model

DFM represents the initialized state as:

```text
S_0 = G(Phi, L, B)
```

where:

- `Phi` = commissioned functional requirements;
- `L` = governing physical laws, including ordinary nuclear decay behavior;
- `B` = physically coherent boundary conditions required by the initialized system.

For the terrestrial radiogenic subsystem:

```text
S_0^rad = G(Phi_thermal, L_nuclear, B_geo)
```

The admissible initialized state must be globally coherent. DFM does not license independent tuning of each isotope system to manufacture a desired retrodictive age.

## 3. Radiogenic heat constraint

Long-lived heat-producing nuclides participate in Earth's thermal architecture. The present radiogenic power is represented by:

```text
H_R(t) = Sum_i M_i h_i exp(-lambda_i t)
```

where `M_i` is isotope inventory, `h_i` is specific heat production, and `lambda_i` is the observed decay constant.

DFM retains ordinary decay prospectively:

```text
lambda_DFM = lambda_observed
```

and rejects accelerated decay as a default chronology-compression mechanism.

The model therefore treats radionuclide inventory as part of a coupled thermal, geochemical, mineralogical, and nuclear state. The heat budget constrains admissible initialization. It does not by itself derive every mineral-scale daughter distribution or isotopic correlation.

## 4. Coherent-initialization principle

Canonical principle:

```text
S_0^rad = G(Phi_thermal, L_nuclear, B_geo) => C_rad
```

where `C_rad` denotes structured coherence among radiogenic systems in the initialized state.

`C_rad` may include correlated parent inventories, daughter inventories where independently warranted, elemental/mineral partitioning, isotope-system relationships, and other physically coupled state variables. These must arise from common generative constraints rather than sample-specific age fitting.

The research burden is to determine which observed radiogenic relationships follow from those constraints.

## 5. Orderly marker versus chronometer

DFM expects an orderly initialized creation. Cross-system convergence is therefore not intrinsically surprising under DFM.

If several isotope systems yield:

```text
T_R1 ~= T_R2 ~= T_R3
```

that observation establishes a structured relationship among present states plus their respective decay/reconstruction models.

DFM provisionally interprets such convergence as an **orderly marker** when it follows from a single independently constrained coherent initialization plus lawful post-initialization history.

It becomes a **historical chronometer** only when there is additional warrant that the reconstructed antecedent trajectory was actually traversed:

```text
C_rad !=> T_H
```

by logical necessity.

More explicitly:

```text
O_iso + M_decay + B_initial + G_history -> T_R
```

and the further claim

```text
T_R = T_H
```

requires historical-provenance warrant.

## 6. Concordance expectation

DFM shall no longer describe multi-chronometer concordance merely as an unexplained anomaly. The model predicts that a single coherent initialization should generate structured cross-system relationships.

However, DFM also shall not claim victory from generic coherence. The stronger empirical requirement is:

```text
Independent functional + physical constraints
    -> admissible S_0^rad
    -> predicted C_rad
    -> observed concordance/discordance pattern
```

without inserting conventional radiometric ages as initialization inputs.

This is the anti-circularity requirement.

## 7. Heat-budget bridge

The canonical explanatory bridge is:

```text
functional terrestrial thermal requirements
    -> constrained heat-producing radionuclide architecture
    -> coupled coherent radiogenic initialization
    -> orderly cross-system markers
    -> ordinary decay and geochemical history thereafter
```

The conventional chronometric interpretation adds a historical premise:

```text
present radiogenic state
    -> backward reconstruction under specified initial/history model
    -> retrodictive age T_R
    + provenance warrant
    -> historical age T_H
```

The programmes therefore can agree on measurements and decay physics while disagreeing over historical provenance.

## 8. Scope discipline

The following distinction is mandatory.

### Constrained directly or indirectly by thermal/function requirements

- bulk long-lived heat-producing-element inventory;
- spatial partitioning relevant to heat production and transport;
- thermal viability;
- compatibility with geodynamic and geochemical function;
- ordinary prospective decay.

### Not automatically derived from the heat budget

- exact mineral-scale parent/daughter ratios;
- U-Pb concordia structure;
- isochron slopes/intercepts;
- extinct-radionuclide signatures;
- closure/reset ages;
- daughter distributions;
- radiation-damage histories;
- every meteorite or terrestrial cross-system correlation.

These remain empirical derivation and discrimination targets. They cannot be declared functional merely because conventional chronology assigns them an old age.

## 9. Predictions and discriminators

### P1: Structured rather than arbitrary initialization

A viable DFM initialization should occupy a constrained family of radiogenic states, not a sample-by-sample collection of free parameters.

### P2: Cross-system order

Where isotope systems participate in the same initialized physical architecture, statistically significant cross-system structure should be expected.

### P3: Lawful post-initialization evolution

Post-initialization isotope evolution should follow observed decay physics except where independent evidence establishes disturbance.

### P4: Disturbance sensitivity

Discordance should correlate with independently detectable mineralogical, thermal, chemical, diffusion, alteration, inheritance, or catastrophic-history variables rather than being assigned ad hoc.

### D1: Age-input circularity

If DFM must use the conventional target age to set each initial isotope state, the orderly-marker explanation fails its independence test.

### D2: Uncoupled concordance

If isotope systems with no defensible common functional, chemical, nuclear, or initialization constraint repeatedly converge with precision that cannot be generated from the admissible initialized-state family, this counts against the current DFM radiogenic auxiliary.

### D3: Event-specific chronometry

If independently known post-initialization events yield precise, reproducible radiometric ages across appropriate systems with independently constrained initial conditions and closure histories, those results constitute genuine chronometric evidence for that post-initialization interval. DFM must preserve them rather than relabel them initialization markers.

## 10. Relationship to WP-002

WP-002 established:

```text

```

and identified cross-system concordance as a principal discriminating burden.

WP-004 advances that work by specifying the candidate common account:

```text
coherently constrained S_0^rad -> C_rad
```

It therefore converts concordance from a generic burden into a quantitative research target while retaining WP-002's no-smuggling and no-free-lunch rules.

## 11. Required quantitative programme

1. Define the admissible terrestrial heat-producing-element inventory from thermal/function constraints without radiometric ages as inputs.
2. Propagate ordinary decay across the DFM historical interval.
3. Couple bulk inventory to geochemical/mineral partition constraints.
4. Catalogue which parent/daughter relationships are entailed, permitted, or unexplained.
5. Test U-Pb, Rb-Sr, Sm-Nd and other systems against the common initialized-state family.
6. Test terrestrial, lunar and meteoritic concordance without fitting each body independently to a target age.
7. Separate initialization markers from demonstrable post-initialization event records.
8. Maintain an unresolved-anomalies ledger rather than filling gaps with unconstrained initial values.

## 12. Canonical model statement

> Designed Functional Maturity treats the radiogenic subsystem as part of a coherently initialized physical state. Long-lived radionuclide inventories participate in the terrestrial thermal budget and evolve thereafter under ordinary measured decay laws. Because initialization is globally constrained rather than isotope-by-isotope, DFM expects orderly relationships among radiogenic systems. Cross-system concordance can therefore function as an orderly marker of coherent initialization. Its interpretation as a chronometer of elapsed pre-initialization history requires the additional historical premise that the retrodictively reconstructed antecedent trajectory was actually traversed. The heat budget constrains the initialization architecture but does not, by itself, derive every mineral-scale isotopic relationship; those relationships remain quantitative tests of the model.
