# Pre-Day Atemporal Initialization Hypothesis (PDAIH)

**DFM work package | 2026-10-25 | Proposed auxiliary; not ratified**  
**Research branch:** research/day4-centrality-residuals-20261025

## Hypothesis
Genesis 1:1–2 describes an ontologically real **pre-day state** preceding the first explicitly enumerated evening–morning cycle of Genesis 1:3–5. DFM proposes that God's initial constitution of Earth may not have been governed by ordinary terrestrial elapsed time. This initial state could include a coupled inventory of radioactive parent isotopes, stable radiogenic daughter isotopes, geochemical reservoirs, and thermal and mineralogical boundary conditions. Day One then establishes the first ordered terrestrial day, preserving DFM's literal-day commitment.

**Exegetical status:** Genesis 1:1–2 identifies creation and a primordial state but does not explicitly assert that this state was atemporal. The atemporality claim is a speculative metaphysical auxiliary, not an exegetically entailed doctrine. The phrase *pre-day* means before the first enumerated day, not necessarily before every conceivable temporal ordering.

## Ontological versus physical chronology
- **Creation / pre-day:** Ontological actualization of a real state; no ordinary terrestrial duration is assigned by the hypothesis. 'Rapid' is not an applicable measured rate if actualization is genuinely atemporal.
- **Day One:** Light and darkness are distinguished and the first evening–morning cycle is established; a literal terrestrial day is preserved.
- **Days Two–Six:** Genuine subsequent creation and ordering events; Day Four's asynchronous cosmic deployment is a separate proposed boundary.
- **Post-creation:** Actual ordinary radioactive decay, mineral growth, geological alteration, and other processes; extraordinary interventions require explicit attribution.

No claim is made that Genesis 1:1–2 must be chronologically separated from Day One by an interval. The model distinguishes logical/ontological priority from measured duration.

## Formal boundary formulation
Let S0 = G(Phi, L, B) denote the constitutive initial physical state. The expression is a model-level representation, not a derived nuclear mechanism or empirically established law. For t >= 0 in the subsequent terrestrial temporal regime, evolve S(t) = F_L(t; S0, H), where H includes actual subsequent events and open-system changes.

For a simple closed parent–daughter pair after t=0:
P(t) = P0 exp(-lambda t)
D(t) = D0 + P0(1-exp(-lambda t)).
D0 may be nonzero. This is not novel in itself: conventional geochronology also admits initial daughter abundances. The empirical challenge is deriving *correlated* P0 and D0 distributions from a shared independently constrained generator, not selecting ratios to mimic desired dates.

## Historical Integrity and Omphalos boundary
Constitutive physical inventories are not automatically fictitious histories. Fossils implying organisms that never lived, or fabricated mineral-event signatures implying crystallization, metamorphism or impact events that never occurred, violate DFM's Historical Integrity Constraint. Event-specific isotope zoning, concordance, discordance, diffusion profiles, and field relations require a real generative mechanism, actual subsequent events, or an explicit unresolved classification.

## Distinct research claims and burdens
1. **Temporal boundary:** A pre-day initial state need not have a measurable terrestrial elapsed duration. This is a metaphysical possibility, not evidence that ordinary time was physically absent.
2. **Radiogenic inventory:** Parent and stable daughter inventories can be boundary conditions, but their observed abundance patterns require a predictive generator.
3. **Geological continuity:** Later mineral histories and terrestrial field relationships must be honored as observations.
4. **Cross-body coordination:** Earth pre-day initialization and Day Four extraterrestrial deployment must be related without simply assuming identical chronological provenance.
5. **Thermal closure:** Actual radioactive decay after the boundary produces heat; any extraordinary nuclear transformation requires energy accounting.
6. **Model identifiability:** The hypothesis must be evaluated against multiple isotope systems and geological observations, with held-out data and complexity penalties.

## Adverse observations and stopping rules
- A model that requires separate arbitrary initial daughter values for each mineral fails predictive economy.
- A model that labels inconvenient event traces as initialized without independent provenance criteria fails Historical Integrity.
- A model that treats lack of a pre-day clock as proof that all deep-time geological ages are invalid overclaims.
- A model that equates atemporal with a finite accelerated physical process without specifying time and energy budgets is internally ambiguous.
- If all testable isotope correlations remain identical under every possible choice of the hypothesis, PDAIH is a metaphysical boundary statement rather than an empirically discriminating scientific model. Record that limitation.

## Relationship to existing work
PDAIH refines **Constitutive Isotopic Coherence** and the **Radiometric Historical Reconstruction Identifiability** protocol. It is not the previously declined accelerated-decay auxiliary and does not require fictitious fossil-bearing Omphalos. The distinction between an initial state and a reconstructed prior history remains a proposed interpretive boundary, not a demonstrated refutation of conventional Earth chronology.
