# WP-013: DFM Hard-Core Tuning — Phased Deployment, Terrestrial Day, and Provenance

**Status:** Executed  
**Type:** Programme hard-core development and repository tuning  
**Depends on:** WP-005, WP-006; incorporates lessons from WP-008 through WP-012

## Objective

Revise DFM's canonical hard core so the programme states its biblical and methodological commitments more precisely.

The prior formulation centered on a single initialization boundary, `S_0 = G(Phi,L,B)`. That remains useful locally but is too coarse for the Genesis 1 narrative. DFM treats Genesis 1 as an actual historical description of a supernatural phased rollout of creation, followed by ordinary designed operation.

## Canonical hard-core additions

### HC-1 Revealed phased deployment

Genesis 1 is treated as an actual historical description of God's ordered supernatural deployment of creation. The days specify a real sequence of divine initialization acts in which domains and systems are instantiated, configured, populated, and commissioned.

### HC-2 Phased initialization architecture

Use:

```text
S_i = G_i(Phi_i, L, B_i, S_{i-1})
```

or schematically:

```text
S_0 --A1--> S_1 --A2--> ... --A6--> S_6
```

where `A_i` denotes supernatural creative/deployment action. The operational laws governing a deployed state need not be the mechanism that generated the deployment transition.

Canonical maxim:

> The rules that govern a running system need not be the process by which the system was deployed.

### HC-3 Terrestrial-day continuity

God supplies the initial light on Day 1 and purposefully constrains it to establish the terrestrial light-dark, evening-morning cycle. The terrestrial day therefore precedes the Sun.

On Day 4 the luminaries are commissioned as ordinary created governors and markers of the already-established cycle.

DFM consequently treats Days 1-6 as terrestrial days continuous in character and ordinary duration, while distinguishing the Day 1-3 illumination source from the Day 4-6 ordinary astronomical governor.

### HC-4 Biblical supernaturalism and methodological designism

DFM is ontologically supernaturalist because biblical Christianity admits divine action as a real causal category.

For ordinary created operation DFM is methodologically designist: it seeks explanations in the stable causal capacities, laws, structures, boundary conditions, and designed regularities of creation.

Therefore:

```text
supernatural initialization != supernatural ordinary operation
```

### HC-5 Coherent pre-seeding

Created maturity is not modeled as disconnected miraculous appearances. Initialization is an integrated physical state whose coupled variables must be mutually coherent and adequate for immediate operation.

The accessible analogy is a pre-seeded virtual world. The analogy illustrates initialization, state depth, coherence, provenance, phased deployment, and transition to ordinary operation. It is not a simulation ontology.

### HC-6 Initialization consilience

Because creation is initialized as an integrated functional whole, cross-domain consistency among initialized states is expected.

Consilience therefore establishes coherent state structure but does not by itself prove that a retrodicted natural trajectory was historically traversed.

Canonical maxim:

> Consilience establishes coherence; provenance establishes history.

### HC-7 Retrodiction and chronometric warrant

Retrodictive extrapolation can be mathematically precise without yielding chronometric certainty.

```text
T_R = R(O_t | M,A,B)
```

does not by itself entail:

```text
T_R = T_H
```

The historical identity requires provenance warrant.

Canonical three-part formulation:

> Measurement establishes state. Retrodiction reconstructs trajectory. Provenance warrants history.

A compact alternative retained for public use:

> Consilience establishes coherence; retrodiction estimates trajectory; provenance establishes history.

## Guardrails

- Genesis 1 is not reduced to a systems-engineering metaphor; the run-book/deployment language is an explanatory model of the historical narrative.
- The virtual-world analogy does not assert simulation ontology.
- “Terrestrial day” is preferred to “solar day” for Days 1-3.
- Ordinary physical law remains the default after commissioning.
- Consilience is not dismissed; DFM must explain its specific mathematical structure where it claims an alternative provenance.
- Retrodictive age is not declared false merely because it is model-conditioned.
- Proven post-initialization chronometers remain genuine historical clocks.

## Completion criterion
