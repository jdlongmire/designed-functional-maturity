# WP-022: Genesis 1 Phased Deployment Architecture

**Status:** Executed
**Type:** Hard-core formalization
**Depends on:** WP-013, WP-015, WP-019, WP-020

## Objective
Formalize the six-day Genesis 1 sequence as the revealed phased deployment boundary for DFM while preventing the systems-engineering analogy from outrunning the text.

## Governing form

```text
S_i = G_i(Phi_i, L, B_i, S_{i-1})
```

Variables:
- `i` = creation-day deployment phase;
- `S_i` = creation state after phase `i`;
- `S_{i-1}` = already-deployed creation inherited by phase `i`;
- `G_i` = supernatural divine creative/deployment act associated with phase `i`;
- `Phi_i` = functionality explicitly or reasonably implied as commissioned in phase `i`;
- `L` = stable created regularities governing ordinary operation once relevant systems are commissioned;
- `B_i` = coherent boundary conditions required for the commissioned state.

This is a model of the revealed sequence, not a claim that Genesis is an engineering document.

## Phase architecture

| Phase | Textual action | DFM deployment reading | Commissioned function | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | Light; separation of light and darkness; naming Day and Night; evening and morning | Establishes illumination and terrestrial light-dark cadence | Day/night temporal cycle | DFM does not require the Sun to exist as the Day 1 governor. Physical mechanism of the initial light is not specified by the text. |
| Day 2 | Expanse; separation of waters | Establishes ordered spatial/environmental domain described by the text | Separation and environmental ordering | DFM does not use the deployment analogy to settle disputed detailed cosmological geometry not specified by the passage. |
| Day 3 | Waters gathered; dry land appears; vegetation produced | Establishes terrestrial land/sea organization and functioning vegetation | Habitable terrestrial domain; plant productivity/reproduction according to kinds | Functional vegetation may require mature state depth. The text does not specify every geophysical or biochemical initialization variable. |
| Day 4 | Luminaries; signs, seasons, days, years; governance of day/night | Commissions astronomical governors/markers of an already-established terrestrial day | Ordinary astronomical timekeeping and illumination governance | Day 4 is a governance handoff, not the creation of the concept of day. DFM must not infer unspecified astronomical deployment mechanics merely from the handoff. |
| Day 5 | Aquatic creatures and flying creatures; blessing to multiply | Populates aquatic/aerial domains with reproductively capable life | Ecological occupation and reproduction | Immediate commissioned function implies biological readiness but does not by itself specify every population-genetic initial condition. |
| Day 6 | Land animals; humanity; image-bearing vocation; dominion; food provision | Completes terrestrial animal population and commissions humanity into its vocation | Reproduction, stewardship, dominion, integrated terrestrial ecology | Human theological status is not reducible to biological function. DFM does not reduce image-bearing to a systems property. |

## Dependency structure

The sequence is cumulative:

```text
S_0 --G_1--> S_1 --G_2--> S_2 --G_3--> S_3
    --G_4--> S_4 --G_5--> S_5 --G_6--> S_6
```

Here `S_0` denotes the creation state immediately prior to the Day 1 phase, `G_1 ... G_6` denote the respective divine creative acts, and `S_1 ... S_6` denote the resulting cumulative states.

The model therefore does not treat Day 6 as a single instantaneous initialization containing six merely descriptive views. Each phase inherits a real prior state and adds, orders, populates, or commissions what the text assigns to that phase.

## Day 1 -> Day 4 continuity

DFM distinguishes the **temporal function** from its later **ordinary astronomical governor**.

```text
Day 1: divinely regulated illumination -> terrestrial day/night cadence
Day 4: luminaries commissioned -> ordinary governance/marking of that cadence
```

The day therefore precedes the Sun as its ordinary governor.

This is a provenance/operation distinction inside the creation week itself: the means by which a function is established need not be identical to the means by which it is subsequently governed.

## Functional state depth

A commissioned function constrains initialization.

Examples:
- functioning vegetation requires more than a superficial visual form;
- reproducing organisms require viable biological organization;
- astronomical governance requires coherent relational state;
- an integrated ecology requires compatible environmental and organismal conditions.

Thus:

```text
commissioned function -> required coherent state depth
```

This implication does not specify every hidden variable. It constrains admissible initialization models and prevents "maturity" from meaning arbitrary appearance.

## Deployment versus ordinary operation

For a subsystem commissioned in phase `i`, DFM distinguishes:

```text
G_i : deployment / initialization
F_L^t : subsequent ordinary evolution under L
```

where `F_L^t` is the state-transition/evolution operator representing ordinary operation under law-set `L` for elapsed time `t`.

DFM does not require:

```text
G_i = F_L^t
```

That is the formal expression of the programme maxim:

> The rules that govern a running system need not be the process by which the system was deployed.

## Hermeneutical guardrails

1. Scripture supplies the sequence and theological/historical boundary.
2. The deployment model organizes that sequence; it does not replace the text.
3. Engineering vocabulary is analogical and subordinate.
4. Do not infer a physical mechanism where Genesis identifies an act/result but not its mechanism.
5. Do not naturalize a creative transition merely because the commissioned system later operates regularly.
6. Do not invoke supernatural action to avoid investigating ordinary post-commissioning processes.
7. Distinguish explicit textual content, model inference, and ancillary hypothesis.

## Research consequences

WP-022 creates the basis for:
- phase-specific state requirements;
- commissioning criteria;
- dependency mapping between phases;
- Day 1/Day 4 governance analysis;
- post-initialization ordinary-operation models;
- identification of which present observations plausibly constrain initial state versus later history;
- visual representations of deployment, handoff, state depth, and provenance.

## Result
The DFM hard core now has an explicit six-phase deployment architecture. The next substantive step is to formalize commissioning and the transition from initialized state to ordinary operation, then derive discriminators rather than merely additional analogies.


## WP-028 clarification: Day 4 deployment

The governance handoff remains textually important, but DFM must not turn that observation into a prohibition on physical deployment during Day 4. The programme now explicitly permits a both-and model: luminaries are commissioned as ordinary governors and markers of the terrestrial day while genuine cosmic processes may also execute under an asynchronous deployment regime. Initialized macrostate and genuine process history may coexist. The particular differential-time mechanism remains auxiliary rather than hard core.
