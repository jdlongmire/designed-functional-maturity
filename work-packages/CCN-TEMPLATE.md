# Chronological Constraint Network Template

Use this record to separate observation, operational physics, reconstruction, and historical interpretation for each major chronological claim.

## Epistemic layers

| Layer | Question |
|---|---|
| O - Observation | What is directly measured? |
| L - Operational physics | What experimentally constrained regularity is used? |
| R - Reconstruction | What prior state, trajectory, or duration is reconstructed? |
| H - Historical interpretation | What actual-history claim is inferred? |

Do not collapse R into H.

## Dependency record

Document the measurement method, physical mechanism, calibration chain, initial conditions, boundary or closure conditions, auxiliary hypotheses, known failure modes, independent cross-checks, predictive history, and residual discordance.

Shared assumptions are counted once.

## Conventional account

Record the specified inputs, model, predictions, comparison with observation, residuals, forbidden observations, and current evidential level.

## DFM account

Record Phi, L, B, generator G, predictions specified before target comparison, residuals, forbidden observations, and initialization parameter cost.

## Anti-circularity

Record whether target values were used as inputs, whether fitted parameters are distinguished from independently constrained parameters, and whether post-hoc auxiliaries are graded appropriately.

## Separate burden ledger

Record conventional burdens and DFM burdens separately. A failure on one ledger is not automatically evidence on the other.

## Discriminators

Record pre-specified observations for which the conventional account and DFM make materially different expectations.

## Appraisal

State:
- what is strongly established;
- what is model-conditioned;
- what remains a provenance inference;
- what would raise or lower DFM standing;
- what would raise or lower the specified conventional causal account.

## Status

Record disposition, review date, and linked work packages or objections.
