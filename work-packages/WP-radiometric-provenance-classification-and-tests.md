# Radiometric Provenance Classification and Test Matrix

**DFM work package | 2026-10-25 | Status: exploratory, unvalidated; not a revision of sibling programme registries.**

## Question and controlling distinction
Do not confuse **sample location** (extraterrestrial versus terrestrial), **formation event** (primordial versus subsequent), **measured isotope state**, and **inferred elapsed history**. The canonical ~4.54 Ga Earth-formation estimate substantially uses meteorite lead-isotope information plus terrestrial lead; it is not a direct date of a preserved original Earth rock. However, numerous terrestrial igneous and metamorphic rocks and zircons yield multi-billion-year dates. Sedimentary sequences are often constrained by dated igneous interbeds and cross-cutting relations. Thus it would be false to claim that most deep-time radiometric observations come from extraterrestrial samples.

**Central DFM hypothesis to investigate:** An initially deployed planetary system may include a physically coherent primordial isotopic state distinct in provenance from isotope states produced by real later geological events. This does not establish that apparent ages are false, or that every 'old' specimen is primordial. It creates a discriminable provenance question.

## Classification axes
For each observation, record all axes separately:
- **Material origin:** terrestrial / lunar / meteorite / other; provenance certainty.
- **Geological context:** candidate primordial material / demonstrable subsequent igneous crystallization / metamorphic reset or cooling / detrital inheritance / sedimentary bracketing / unknown.
- **Isotope system:** U–Pb, Pb–Pb, Rb–Sr, Sm–Nd, K–Ar/Ar–Ar, Re–Os, etc.; mineral, closure behavior, daughter mobility.
- **Causal origin proposed by DFM:** I = constitutive initialization; G = real generative deployment transformation; H = actual post-deployment process; M = mixture; U = unresolved.
- **Trace type:** constitutive composition / process-specific event trace / mixed / undecided.
- **Independent controls:** field relations, petrography, geochemistry, diffusion profiles, multiple chronometers, known-age calibration, energy and mass balance.

No classification as I or G may be made solely because a conventional age conflicts with DFM chronology. Genuine event traces require genuine events, including when events occurred during extraordinary deployment.

## Three constructive DFM auxiliaries (candidate, not adopted)

**A. Systemic functional maturity.** Function belongs to a coupled planetary system, not necessarily an isolated isotope. Investigate whether geochemical stability, phase equilibria, atmospheric composition, thermal evolution, and mineralogical integrity constrain daughter abundances. A qualitative appeal to 'coherence' does not establish the quantitative ratios.

**B. Generative isotopic deployment.** Genuine nuclear/geochemical transformations could occur during extraordinary deployment. This satisfies Historical Integrity only if the events really occur. Any proposed rate change or time compression must account for decay energy, radiation, and final thermal conditions; it cannot silently reinstate the accelerated-decay auxiliary previously declined by Christian Designism.

**C. Coherent isotopic initialization.** Derive correlated isotope inventories from a shared boundary-state generator rather than independent specimen-by-specimen fitting. Predict covariance across systems, bodies, and mineral classes using independent constraints.

A hybrid may be evaluated, but additional flexibility counts against predictive economy unless independently constrained.

## Proposed comparative tests and failure criteria

**R-01: Earth-age provenance audit.** Separate the meteorite–terrestrial Pb model for Earth's formation from dates of individual terrestrial rocks. **Pass:** accurate accounting of which observations come from each body and the inferential dependency. **Failure:** describing terrestrial deep-time data as predominantly extraterrestrial.

**R-02: Primordial-versus-event holdout.** Predeclare a set of P1/P3 and E1/E2 specimens; construct the initial-state rule without fitting the withheld isotopic ages. **Progress:** predicts holdout isotope covariance and field constraints. **Failure:** requires arbitrary daughter inventories tuned to each target age.

**R-03: Historical Integrity provenance ledger.** For zircon growth zones, impact structures, metamorphic overprints, eruption ash, and sedimentary unconformities, identify an actual causal event or explicitly mark unresolved. **Failure:** assigns specific event traces to nonexistent events.

**R-04: Cross-system concordance.** Derive both isotope ratios and covariance across U–Pb, Pb–Pb, Rb–Sr, Sm–Nd, and Ar systems, accounting for mineral chemistry and closure. **Failure:** 'integrated specification' remains an unsupported assertion or requires conventional inferred age as input.

**R-05: Thermal feasibility.** For generative deployment, track total nuclear energy and radiative/thermal sinks and ensure coherent final states. **Failure:** unexplained heat disposal or disguised accelerated decay.

**R-06: Boundary identifiability.** Specify an independently observable criterion distinguishing initialization from genuine ordinary elapsed history. **Failure:** classification is assigned only after seeing a disagreeable date, or no possible observation discriminates the classes.

**R-07: Bilateral comparison.** Record conventional successes (known-age calibration, field ordering, isotope cross-validation) and limits (specific initial-condition and closure assumptions); apply the same burden of independent constraint to DFM. **Failure:** either side is dismissed solely because of its worldview.
