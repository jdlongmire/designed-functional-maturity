# DFM v0.4 Cross-Repository Reconciliation Matrix

**Status:** Research synthesis / pre-revision control document, 2026-10-25. **Scope:** repository-governed conceptual and empirical claims. **Not:** ratification of any auxiliary, successful scientific test, or modification of sibling programmes.

## Authority and conflict rules
1. Scripture and adopted exegetical commitments govern the historical framing; the Christian Designism programme records umbrella epistemology, hard core, and Lakatosian appraisal. DFM's own PROGRAMME.md governs DFM-specific deployment architecture. Domain repositories govern their *own* physical hypotheses, tests, and status.
2. Do not promote a sibling's proposed auxiliary to DFM hard core merely because it appears in a related paper. FCD is chronology-neutral; CAC is a chronology-specific specialization. Preserve that dependency direction.
3. Where sibling repositories disagree or their documentation is stale, record the discrepancy and defer harmonization. An asserted mathematical possibility is not an empirical success.
4. No scientific claim is called validated without reproducible evidence, registered decision rule, and independent appraisal.

## Bilateral explanatory-burden matrix

| Question | Conventional physical/historical reconstruction | DFM / designist reconstruction |
|---|---|---|
| Present physical measurements | Explain observations with quantitative, mutually consistent models; conventional cosmology and geochronology have substantial demonstrated successes | Accept the same observations and reproduce quantitative patterns |
| Boundary provenance | Specify initial-state assumptions and distinguish fitted parameters from explanations of ultimate origin; ultimate origin need not be claimed by a bounded physical model | Specify functionally necessary mature states and independently constrain initialized inventories |
| Historical time | Defend model-dependent extension of laws, closure assumptions, and causal sequences using independent tests and cross-validation | Provide credible terrestrial/cosmic clock relation, authentic event histories, and lawful post-deployment trajectories |
| Cosmological anomalies | Explain or bound dark-sector properties, Hubble discrepancy, and early-galaxy tensions without overstating unresolved status | Derive persistent/transient/inherited signatures, not merely rename anomalies |
| Geological chronology | Derive ordering, isotope covariance, stratigraphy, event sequences, and exceptional catastrophes | Derive same observables with real Flood and other actual events, constrained initialization and physical budgets |
| Model economy and progress | Expose free parameters, systematic uncertainties, rival models, and novel predictions | Register falsifiers and novelty in advance; do not use new miracle exceptions or unlogged auxiliaries |
| Philosophical scope | Methodological naturalism is an operating rule, not a proof of metaphysical naturalism; a bounded theory need not explain ultimate existence | Scriptural commitments are stated as prior historical authority, not claimed to be empirical deductions |
