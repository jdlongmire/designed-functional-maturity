# WP-001: Flesh out the original artifact

**Target artifact:** `drafts/designed-functional-maturity.md`
**Status:** Not started

## Framing

This is the methodological paper: methodological designism as a more explanatory posture than methodological naturalism, illustrated by (not asserting the historicity of) the DFM systems reading of Genesis 1. The historical claim — that Genesis 1 describes an actual six-phase deployment — is deliberately out of scope here; it is the subject of a separate, more explicit companion paper.

## Objective

Take the initial cut from a conceptual synthesis to a defensible draft: every empirical claim grounded in a real, checkable source, each with a confidence signal, and each section's argument tightened against the strongest version of the naturalist reply.

## Scope

1. **Citation pass.** Replace the placeholder References section with real primary-source citations for every empirical claim in §3 (logic/information, low-entropy initial conditions, fine-tuning, quantum measurement, origin of life, consciousness, macroevolution, biomolecular preservation, radiometric non-homogeneity, JWST high-z galaxies). Apply the source protocol from `meta-harness/mind.md`: primary over secondary, HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW/UNCERTAIN confidence signaling per claim, "as cited in" format where primary access isn't feasible.
2. **Steelman pass.** For each §3 subsection, add or strengthen the strongest naturalist response before explaining why it remains a residual gap rather than a resolution — currently the draft asserts the gap without engaging the best counter-argument.
3. **§2 development.** The DFM framework section is currently a compressed summary; expand the forming–filling symmetry argument and the internal-dependency argument with the actual textual/systems reasoning, not just the claim that it exists.
4. **§4 tightening.** Make the "explanatory advantage" claims in §4 track 1:1 back to the specific gaps identified in §3, rather than reading as a parallel restatement.
5. **Scope discipline.** Preserve §1 and §5's explicit disclaimer that this paper does not adjudicate Genesis's historical status — that boundary is what distinguishes this paper from the companion historical paper and should not erode as the draft grows.

## Out of scope

- Asserting or arguing for the historical/chronological reading of Genesis 1 (reserved for the companion paper).
- New sections beyond tightening the existing six.

## Definition of done

- Every empirical claim in §3 has a real citation with a confidence signal.
- Each §3 subsection engages a steelmanned naturalist counter-argument.
- §1/§5 methodological-not-historical framing is intact.
- Draft committed to `drafts/designed-functional-maturity.md` on its own branch, reviewed, merged.
