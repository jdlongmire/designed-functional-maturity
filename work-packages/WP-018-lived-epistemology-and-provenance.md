# WP-018: Lived Epistemology and Provenance

**Status:** Executed
**Type:** Programme epistemology / public communication
**Depends on:** WP-015, WP-016, WP-017

## Objective
Demonstrate that exclusive naturalistic provenance is not entailed by the way humans ordinarily reason about law-governed systems, while avoiding an invalid artifact-to-universe proof.

## Core distinction

DFM distinguishes:

- **Operational naturalism:** ordinary investigation of a running system through its stable natural causal capacities and regularities.
- **Provenance naturalism:** the stronger historical thesis that the system and its relevant initial state must themselves be exhaustively explained by natural processes.

Canonical formulations:

```text
present regular operation != demonstrated naturalistic provenance
law-governed state != demonstrated law-generated state
```

> **Operational naturalism is commonplace in lived experience. Provenance naturalism is an additional historical thesis.**

## Lived-epistemology argument

Engineering, software operations, archaeology, and forensics routinely distinguish present operation from initialization, configuration, agency, deployment, and event history. This establishes that operation/provenance separation is a normal epistemic practice.

It does not prove that the universe has a designer.

## Guardrail

Artifact analogies are used to establish the legitimacy of the explanatory distinction, not to infer supernatural creation merely by resemblance.

DFM's positive provenance claim remains grounded in its canonical/theological hard core and remains subject to empirical and explanatory constraints.
