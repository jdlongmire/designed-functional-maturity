# DFM-PAM-002: Track A Radiometric Accommodation Ledger

**Status:** Initial case inventory and conditional appraisal, 2026-10-27. **Parent:** DFM-PAM-001, RCIM-001, RCIM-002.  
**Important:** This is an analytical planning ledger, not a completed literature review or empirical validation. Conventional model descriptions below are general; each must be anchored to specific datasets and publications before a case is scored.

## Common method
For every case distinguish (1) measured observations and analytical uncertainty, (2) conventional inference and independently supported auxiliaries, (3) DFM initialized-state and post-boundary account, (4) conservation/physical constraints, (5) free parameters and independent justification, (6) unexplained residuals, and (7) Track A decision. The same accommodation/ad-hoc rubric applies to both programmes. Do not treat inferred conventional ages as raw measurements; do not discard them as irrelevant corroborated inferences either.

## Case 1: U-Pb dual-chain concordance in zircon
**Observations:** Measured U-238/Pb-206 and U-235/Pb-207 ratios (with corrections), their joint concordia/discordia structure, mineral textures, chemistry, and field relationships.
**Conventional account:** Ordinary decay after mineral formation, Pb exclusion or common-Pb correction, possible inherited zircon domains, metamorphism and Pb loss. Independent support may include petrography, cross-cutting relationships and other clocks.
**DFM Track A account:** Initial coherent U/Pb distributions plus ordinary subsequent decay, mineral growth and disturbances. Special initialization may supply pre-existing daughter isotopes; their mineral-scale distribution must be coupled across both U chains rather than chosen independently per measurement.
**Obligations:** Explain why paired chains produce observed concordia geometry, why some zircons are discordant, and why distinct geological populations cluster. Preserve isotope mass balance, crystal chemistry, and known-age post-boundary controls.
**Status:** **Not yet established.** Closed-system equations exist, but no independently bounded common initial-state generator or measured-dataset demonstration. Merely assigning an initial Pb inventory to match apparent ages is inadequate.
**Next gate:** Select grain-level dataset, characterize mineralogical constraints, define shared initial Pb model before ratio fitting.

## Case 2: K-Ar and Ar-Ar
**Observations:** K content, Ar isotopic composition, mineral context, step-heating release patterns, and known-age lava comparisons.
**Conventional account:** K-40 branching decay, mineral closure, excess/trapped Ar, loss, thermal history, atmospheric corrections and standards.
**DFM Track A account:** Initialized atmospheric, mantle and mineral Ar inventories, with subsequent ordinary K decay, degassing, diffusion, escape and thermal disturbances.
**Obligations:** Distinguish initialized from later-produced Ar, explain mineral-specific retention and Ar-Ar spectra, preserve reservoir mass balance, and accommodate post-initialization known-age events.
**Status:** **Analytical baseline only.** An atmospheric partial-pressure estimate does not demonstrate functional Ar-40 necessity or a coherent K-Ar history.
**Next gate:** Coupled reservoir model and published measured-isotope calibration cases.

## Case 3: Rb-Sr isochrons
**Observations:** Parent/daughter ratios across cogenetic minerals or rocks, linear or nonlinear arrays, mineral partitioning, and geologic context.
**Conventional account:** Isochron slope interpreted through ordinary decay when initial Sr ratio was shared and the system remained sufficiently closed; mixing and disturbance can produce alternative patterns.
**DFM Track A account:** Initial source or mineral isotope structure plus ordinary decay and later mixing; common initialization rules may produce structured arrays.
**Obligations:** Explain both slopes and intercepts across multiple samples and distinguish inherited initial structure from mixing and decay without independently tuning each point.
**Status:** **Conceptual only.** No physical generative model yet.
**Next gate:** Quantify an initial-state partitioning mechanism and test whether it can produce isochron families.

## Case 4: Radiogenic heat and terrestrial thermal history
**Observations:** Present heat flow, U/Th/K inventories, mineral geochemistry, geoneutrino constraints and thermal properties.
**Conventional account:** Radiogenic heating and secular cooling within geological and geophysical evolution models, with uncertainties in mantle and crustal partition.
**DFM Track A account:** Functionally mature initial thermal and nuclide inventory, with ordinary post-boundary radiogenic heat and heat transport; no requirement for accelerated decay to produce daughter inventories.
**Obligations:** Specify initial thermal state, present heat production and heat transport; distinguish stable daughters from heat-generating parents; explain relevant geological thermal signatures.
**Status:** **Plausible mechanism class, quantitatively unassessed.** Avoid asserting that a chosen initial state solves all thermal history constraints.
**Next gate:** Parameterized energy-budget and transport model with measured present-state bounds.

## Case 5: Independently constrained geological events and calibration
**Observations:** Historically dated eruptions, stratigraphic relationships, independently measured sedimentary/volcanic sequences, mineral alteration and laboratory standards.
**Conventional account:** Ordinary decay and calibrated analytical methods, with corrections and geological event interpretation.
**DFM Track A account:** Post-initialization events should be subject to ordinary decay, mineral closure and transport. Initialized inventories must not be used to arbitrarily erase event-specific evidence.
**Obligations:** Account for reliable known-age calibration and geological ordering; clarify which isotopic signals are inherited and which arose during post-boundary events.
**Status:** **Open and critical.** No empirical DFM calibration suite completed.
**Next gate:** Assemble measured known-age cases and assess DFM model predictions without using dates as adjustable targets.

**Overall:** Track A is a *structured plausibility research programme*, not yet a demonstrated physically and empirically adequate radiogenic plausibility model. This does not require Track B comparative superiority, but it does require explicit, bounded accommodations for the principal observations.

## Auxiliary parity audit questions
1. Is the auxiliary supported independently of the target isotope result?
2. Does it apply across a family of samples or only a single discordant result?
3. Is its magnitude physically constrained and uncertainty documented?
4. Does it respect post-boundary physics and known geological relationships?
5. What observations remain unexplained?
6. Are the same questions applied to the conventional and DFM accounts?
7. Does an accommodation rely on a conventional age as a premise, or only use age inference as a comparison outcome?
