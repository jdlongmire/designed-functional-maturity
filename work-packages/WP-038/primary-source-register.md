# WP-038 Primary-Source Register

Status: bibliographic validation pass 1 complete for entries 1-2 (2026-11-15). Dataset and accession validation pending.

1. Yohn CT, Jiang Z, McGrath SD, Hayden KE, Khaitovich P, Johnson ME, Eichler MY, McPherson JD, Zhao S, Paabo S, Eichler EE (2005), *Lineage-Specific Expansions of Retroviral Insertions within the Genomes of African Great Apes but Not Humans and Orangutans*, PLoS Biology 3(4): e110. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.0030110 . PMID 15737067; PMCID PMC1054887.
   - Correction record: the provisional register listed this as PNAS with DOI 10.1073/pnas.0508714102; that identification is wrong and is superseded by this entry.
   - Relevance: PTERV1 lineage specificity. 287 integration sites mapped in African great apes and Old World monkeys, absent from humans and orangutans; about 95.8 percent of insertions at non-orthologous loci between chimpanzee and gorilla, which the authors read as independent, concurrent infection 3-4 million years ago. Directly relevant to the D3 targeting calibration and to C0 lineage-specific event counts.
2. Lewinski MK, Bisgrove D, Shinn P, Chen H, Hoffmann C, Hannenhalli S, Verdin E, Berry CC, Ecker JR, Bushman FD (2005), *Genome-wide analysis of chromosomal features repressing human immunodeficiency virus transcription*, Journal of Virology 79(11): 6610-6619. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1128/JVI.79.11.6610-6619.2005 . PMID 15890899; PMCID PMC1112149.
   - Correction record: the provisional register listed this as 2006 with the title word "integration"; the paper is 2005 and concerns repression of HIV transcription at integration sites, not integration targeting itself. Superseded by this entry.
   - Relevance: chromosomal context effects on integrated provirus expression; background for integration-site context, secondary for D3.
3. Lewinski MK, Yamashita M, Emerman M, Ciuffi A, Marshall H, Crawford G, Collins F, Shinn P, Leipzig J, Hannenhalli S, Berry CC, Ecker JR, Bushman FD, Malim MH (2006), *Retroviral DNA Integration: Viral and Cellular Determinants of Target-Site Selection*, PLoS Pathogens 2(6): e60. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.ppat.0020060 .
   - Added at validation pass 1 as the primary candidate for integration target-site selection (HIV versus MLV preferences, integrase and Gag determinants, DNase I hypersensitive site analysis). Bibliographic details verified against the publisher record; full-text extraction pending.
   - Relevance: D3 targeting null calibration.
4. Count sources of record for shared human-chimpanzee ERVs (traced and scoped 2026-11-15; full verification in claims-verification.md):
   - IHGSC (Nature 2001): about 203,000 ERV insertions (classes I-III) in the human genome.
   - Chimpanzee Sequencing and Analysis Consortium (Nature 2005, Table 2): 82 human-specific and 279 chimpanzee-specific ERV insertions; the remainder are orthologous.
   - Polavarapu, Bowen and McDonald (Genome Biology 2006): 425 full-length chimpanzee ERVs, 42 families, 40 with human orthologs.
   - The '205' figure is the HERV-W family count (Grandi et al., BMC Evolutionary Biology 2018): 205 of 211 human HERV-W loci orthologous in chimpanzee. Family-level, not genome-wide.
   - The '336' figure is a filtered full-length sample (Retrovirology 2015, entry 6): 336 orthologous full-length pairs selected for divergence analysis. Not a total.
5. Tarlinton RE, Meers J, Young PR (2006), *Retroviral invasion of the koala genome*, Nature 442: 79-81. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/nature04841 .
   - Relevance: documents germline endogenization in progress (KoRV), the reference against claims that the process has never been observed.
6. *Orthologous endogenous retroviruses exhibit directional selection since the chimp-human split*, Retrovirology 2015 (PMC4477479).
   - Relevance: 336 flank-verified orthologous full-length pairs with divergence measurements; seed dataset candidate for benchmark class 1, and the source for the C-3 divergence finding.

**Source policy:** Prefer original papers, accessioned genomic data, and author-supplied supplementary material. Record reference genome build, orthology definition, filtering, uncertainty and study limitations. Secondary summaries must be labeled secondary. No numbers are accepted solely from debate rhetoric.
