Yohn CT, Jiang Z, McGrath SD, Hayden KE, Khaitovich P, Johnson ME, Eichler MY, McPherson JD, Zhao S, Paabo S, Eichler EE (2005), *Lineage-Specific Expansions of Retroviral Insertions within the Genomes of African Great Apes but Not Humans and Orangutans*, PLoS Biology 3(4): e110. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.0030110 . PMID 15737067; PMCID PMC1054887.
   - Correction record: the provisional register listed this as PNAS with DOI 10.1073/pnas.0508714102; that identification is wrong and is superseded by this entry.
   - Relevance: PTERV1 lineage specificity. 287 integration sites mapped in African great apes and Old World monkeys, absent from humans and orangutans; about 95.8 percent of insertions at non-orthologous loci between chimpanzee and gorilla, which the authors read as independent, concurrent infection 3-4 million years ago. Directly relevant to the D3 targeting calibration and to C0 lineage-specific event counts.
