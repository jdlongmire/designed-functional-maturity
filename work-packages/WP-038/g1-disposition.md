## Required corrections

**F1. Independent holdout units and leakage. Accepted; design changed.** The v1 locus-level stratified split is superseded by a group-aware split (data/pilot-split-v2.csv) in which the ERV family is the clustering unit and whole families are assigned to one side only. Rule: families shuffled with seed 20261116; families larger than 70 loci excluded from the held-out side so no single family dominates it; families accumulated to the held-out side until 130 of the 342 frame loci were assigned. Result: 12 held-out families (130 loci), 212 training-v2 loci. Contamination accounting, disclosed per the finding: 78 of the 130 held-out-side loci were examined during v1 training extraction and are quarantined, excluded from all scoring; the clean held-out set is 52 never-examined loci. The 206-locus pilot extraction and reclassification were performed before this freeze, on v1 training assignments; those results are development results, not prospective held-out predictions, and are labelled as such everywhere they are reported. The published 2015 presence matrix covers all 342 frame loci, so pattern-level information about held-out loci exists in the public source; the freeze governs this programme's extraction and fitting, and no model parameter has been fitted to any held-out locus. Scores will be reported locus-weighted and family-cluster-weighted, with sensitivity to the split.

**F2. Parity needs an actual decision rule. Accepted; protocol frozen.** scoring-protocol.md preregisters: the predictive metric (mean per-locus log predictive density on the joint evidence), a noninferiority margin of 0.1 nats per locus justified before any results, family-cluster bootstrap uncertainty, a missingness policy with bounds, and a burden ledger. Equivalence is decided by the confidence interval of the difference against the margin, never by overlapping intervals or a nonsignificant difference.

**F3. Event-count ties are not predictive fit. Accepted; already the package's position, now enforced.** The pilot event-count table stands in the record as descriptive accounting with its supersession addendum. The scoring protocol requires joint predictive scoring over flanks, TSD, orientation, shared derived marks and LTR divergence; event counts alone cannot establish parity for any model, D3 included.

**F4. Graded orthology and verified absence. Accepted; rule written.** The benchmark design now states a graded orthology rule: Grade A requires two-flank synteny with boundary and TSD evidence where recoverable; Grade B requires two-flank synteny plus internal homology where TSD is not recoverable; absence requires a verified empty-site sequence; a chain-lift or mapping failure is recorded as missing, never as absence. Scoring runs sensitivity analyses under the strict (TSD-required) and graded rules. Recorded honestly: the extraction practice already operated this way (flank geometry plus internal homology, absences requiring positive support); the written rule now matches the practice.

**F5. Document consistency and one canonical dataset. Accepted; reconciled.** The benchmark design's stale pre-extraction status is replaced with a dated status and preregistration chronology. Canonical scored datasets are declared: for training-side development, data/training206-verdicts-final.csv (206 loci, 144 resolved, 62 declared unresolved); for held-out scoring, the 52 clean loci of data/pilot-split-v2.csv, to be extracted under the frozen protocol at scoring time. Earlier census snapshots (including the intermediate merged file and the census addendum) are retained as historical evidence and are not scored. Outcome-dependent selection risk is disclosed: extraction effort was directed first at loci whose 2015 patterns were non-nested, which is why the priority subset is not a random sample; scoring uses the full resolved training set, not the priority subset, for fitting.

**F6. AHPP commensurability. Accepted; burden becomes a ledger, not a count.** The formalization's comparative rules now require a burden ledger per model with separated dimensions (integration and loss events; boundary placements; initialized families or elements; rate and population parameters; externally fixed assumptions with rationale), reported as a vector alongside any predictive score, with a coding-length sensitivity analysis. C0's phylogeny, branch rates, ascertainment model and population assumptions are entered in its ledger on the same terms as D-model initializations. No single equal-weight count is used as a headline.

**F7. D3 independence and targeting. Accepted; formalization amended.** HIV and MLV integration preferences are analogues, not calibrations, for ancient germline ERV families; transferability assumptions must be stated with uncertainty and an effective target-space size per family. Shared derived mutations acquired after integration are priced independently of integration targeting: targeting cannot be charged for them, and a D3 account of an exact shared disabling mutation requires its own independently priced mechanism.

## Model-specific findings

- **C0.** Accepted. Its protective belt (insertion polymorphism, incomplete lineage sorting, loss, recombination and gene conversion, orthology uncertainty, ascertainment) is entered as declared auxiliaries in its burden ledger rather than being folded into a single deletion charge.
- **D1.** Accepted. Functional initialization warrants must be preregistered independently of the ERV patterns under test; a functional annotation alone does not warrant a specific initialized integration scar. Recorded in the formalization amendments.
- **D2.** Accepted; already satisfied. The formalization requires explicit ancestry disclosure at each boundary placement, and a D2 version entailing human-primate genealogical ancestry is not counted as a no-common-ancestry alternative.
- **D3.** Accepted via F7. Experimentally bounded likelihoods are required by the scoring protocol.
- **D4.** Provisionally admitted as a separate candidate, on GPT's G1 recommendation, with conditions recorded in the formalization amendments: a rooted phylogenetic direction test with outgroups and model uncertainty; predeclared chronology, generation-time and rate distributions; LTR conversion and recombination effects modelled; endogenous-to-exogenous emergence distinguished from later ordinary reintegration. One correction is recorded against the original D4 text: the expectation that the least degraded endogenous copies are the oldest families is not entailed without a specified degeneration process, and it is downgraded from a prediction to a hypothesis pending that specification. JD's approval of this disposition (2026-11-15) is recorded as the admission ruling for D4's provisional status.
