# WP-038 Graded Harvest Register: Creationist and ID Research Base

Date: 2026-11-15. Purpose: to record what the creationist and intelligent design literatures actually contribute to this programme, graded by evidentiary weight, so that nothing is cited above its grade and nothing usable is missed. Companion documents: creationist-literature-map.md (argument families and dispositions) and claims-verification.md (claims C-1 to C-3 checked).

## Grading rules
- **Tier A:** primary data or reproducible method that can enter scoring: accession-level datasets, experimental measurements, or computations a third party can rerun from stated inputs.
- **Tier B:** technical argument or model proposal with scholarly apparatus (specialist journal, academic press, or technical book chapter). Harvestable as models, discriminators and burdens. Not citable as data.
- **Tier C:** popular or summary presentation (magazines, websites, videos, apologetics summaries). Leads only. A Tier C claim enters the programme only after it has been traced to a Tier A or B source or converted into a claims-register entry and checked.
- A source keeps the grade of its content, not of its author or venue reputation. A peer-reviewed paper making an argument is Tier B for the argument and Tier A only for the data it actually reports.

## Headline finding
For ERVs specifically, **no Tier A dataset from the creationist or ID side has been located**: no genome-wide orthology recount, no locus-level flanking-sequence dataset, no original ERV accession-level analysis. The harvest value of that literature is concentrated in Tier B model proposals (exogenization/VIGE, functional initialization) and in claims that, once checked, mostly failed or proved non-discriminating (claims-verification.md). The mechanism-critique shelf is stronger and includes Tier A and B items in mainstream venues, but those items address mechanism sufficiency in general and do not score ERV loci.

## Harvest protocol going forward
1. New creationist or ID material enters through this register with a grade before it is used anywhere else in the package.
2. Tier C items generate claims-register entries; they are never cited directly in scoring or public material.
3. Tier B items may generate models, discriminators or burdens, each recorded with the model it affects.
4. Tier A status is granted only after the programme reproduces the dataset or computation from stated inputs.
5. Known gaps to pursue when time allows: full-text search of Answers Research Journal, Journal of Creation and Creation Research Society Quarterly for ERV, HERV, retrotransposon and syncytin items beyond those above; Jeanson's Traced (2022) for human-origins rate claims adjacent to D4; any creationist accession-level genomics work published after 2020.
