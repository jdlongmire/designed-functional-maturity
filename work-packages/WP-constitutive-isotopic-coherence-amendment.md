# Constitutive Isotopic Coherence: DFM Daughter-Product Amendment Proposal

**2026-10-25 | Proposed DFM auxiliary refinement | Research branch only | Not ratified in Christian Designism**

## Decision requested
Replace the *individual functional necessity* criterion as the sole warrant for initialized radiogenic daughters with a **system-level constitutive coherence criterion**. Preserve the Historical Integrity Constraint and explicitly retain multi-system isotope concordance, geological event traces, and thermal feasibility as unresolved quantitative burdens.

## Proposed position text
At the creation boundary, Earth was constituted as an integrated, physically operable and life-supporting planetary system. Its initial state could include radioactive parent isotopes that supply subsequent radiogenic heat, stable isotopes including daughter nuclides, mineralogical and geochemical reservoirs, and correlated elemental/isotopic inventories. Such inventories are not assumed to have been generated through an antecedent terrestrial history solely because ordinary later processes can produce the same nuclides.

Constitutive coherence concerns the coupled physical state of the system, not proof that every constituent is individually indispensable. The claim that a stable isotope participates in chemistry does not demonstrate that its particular abundance is uniquely necessary for habitability. The system-level criterion therefore licenses an investigable initial-state hypothesis, not an unrestricted assignment of arbitrary daughter ratios.

A measured isotope ratio does not, by itself, uniquely reveal whether a nuclide was included in an initial state or produced later. Nonetheless, mineral growth zoning, isotopic concordance and discordance, reference-isotope correlations, diffusion signatures, textures, field relations, and cross-chronometer agreement can substantially constrain candidate histories. DFM must explain these jointly, not dismiss them as mere appearance.

## Physical model sketch
For parent P and radiogenic daughter D in a closed system after the boundary:
P(t) = P0 exp(-lambda t)
D(t) = D0 + P0(1-exp(-lambda t)).
DFM allows nonzero D0 as a constitutive initial condition, but this is also allowed by conventional geochronology; it is not a distinctive prediction. The distinguishing task is to derive D0 and its correlations across minerals/reservoirs from a **shared constrained generator**, rather than assigning each specimen an initial value chosen to imitate its conventional date. Open-system processes and actual deployment transformations require separate terms and mass/energy accounting.

## Distinctions
- **Radiogenic heat budget:** initial parent abundances determine later heating alongside other heat sources. Initial stable daughters do not themselves supply ongoing decay heat.
- **Constitutive daughters:** isotope identity does not encode production history. Their distribution, however, can encode constraints on possible histories.
- **Systemic coherence:** coupled geochemical functionality may constrain abundance patterns, but life-friendly necessity for any particular daughter ratio remains to be demonstrated.
- **Historical integrity:** real traces of crystallization, impact, metamorphism, and sedimentation require real causes; no fabricated event history.
- **Ordinary chronology:** standard models often already estimate nonzero initial daughter isotopes (e.g., isochrons); DFM cannot claim their mere existence as a defect of radiometric dating.

## Unresolved tests and falsification pressure
1. **Cross-chain concordance:** derive coupled U-238/Pb-206 and U-235/Pb-207 distributions and predict concordia/discordia across distinct minerals without specimen-level target-age fitting.
2. **Cross-system agreement:** explain U-Pb, Pb-Pb, Rb-Sr, Sm-Nd, K-Ar/Ar-Ar and independent field-order constraints under the same causal history.
3. **Common initial-state generator:** predeclare physically constrained rules for primordial isotope covariance across terrestrial and extraterrestrial reservoirs, with holdout datasets.
4. **Geological disturbance:** forward-model real metamorphism, fluid alteration, Pb loss, mineral growth and inherited grains, including their textures and differential closure.
5. **Heat and mass balance:** compute heat production from parent inventories and any extraordinary transformations, including realistic sinks; do not silently resurrect unconstrained accelerated decay.
6. **Provenance classification:** define primordial versus subsequent event attribution without sorting by disagreement with conventional numerical ages.
7. **Model comparison:** compare prediction error and auxiliary complexity against established chronometers and known-age calibration.

## Governance and disposition
- **Proposed:** adopt *Constitutive Isotopic Coherence* as a DFM-level research auxiliary.
- **Not claimed:** demonstrated initial isotope generator, proven life-necessity of daughter ratios, alternative numerical chronology, or empirical falsification of radiometric dating.
- **Parent programme:** propose amendment to Christian Designism A-001 and reconsider wording of I-0006 only after independent review. The issue should remain **OPEN**, reframed around deriving structured isotope correlations rather than requiring individual daughter-product necessity.
