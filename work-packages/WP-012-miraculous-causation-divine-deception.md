# WP-012: Miraculous Causation and the Divine Deception Objection

Status: Proposed | Programme: DFM | Date: 2026-10-09

## Research question
Under what conditions does a created state supporting an incorrect inference about antecedent history constitute divine deception?

## Problem
The unrestricted objection claims that mature created states whose ordinary production would require a long history deceive observers. If applied consistently, this principle also challenges miracles producing effects ordinarily associated with prior causal processes. A stronger objection concerns highly specific apparent records of events that never occurred.

## Definitions
- **Functional maturity:** Immediate commissioned capacity without ordinary development.
- **Initialized causal conditions:** Genuine directly established properties also producible by ordinary processes.
- **Apparent historical records:** Evidence apparently encoding particular events that did not occur.
- **Executed extraordinary history:** Real causal processes under extraordinary deployment conditions.
- **Retrodictive inference:** Reconstructed history from state, model, and boundary assumptions.
- **Deception:** Culpable false representation or intentional inducement of false belief; explicit speech is not necessary.

## Competing theses
**Candidate:** A false inference from genuine created properties alone does not prove divine deception. Attribution of misleading communication or intentional inducement requires further argument.

**Strongest objection:** Systematically arranged, detailed fictitious event records could be misleading even absent verbal falsehood. Functional maturity does not automatically justify fabricated chronology.

## Biblical comparisons
Genesis 1–2 (mature commissioned life); John 2:1–11 (wine); John 6:1–14 (food); John 9:1–7 (sight); John 11:38–44 (restoration). Assess divine truthfulness using Numbers 23:19, Titus 1:2, Hebrews 6:18, and creation's revelatory role using Psalm 19:1 and Romans 1:20.

Exegetical restraint: Scripture does not establish measured chemical age for Cana wine or detailed growth markers in multiplied fish.

## Argument schema
1. Ordinary history H can produce observed property P under model M.
2. Miraculous creation C can, in principle, produce P without H.
3. Therefore P alone does not deductively entail H.
4. Mistaken inference of H from P is insufficient, by itself, to prove deception.
5. Additional analysis must consider agency, intention, communication, specificity, and reasonable evidential reliance.
6. No general immunity from historical evidence follows.

## Miraculous Causation and Divine Deception Test (MCDDT)
For each case record: (1) independently measured state; (2) retrodictive model and assumptions; (3) specified creation alternative; (4) independent functional or physical constraints; (5) specificity of apparent event records; (6) independent provenance; (7) possible attributable misrepresentation; (8) canonical consistency; (9) empirical discriminators; (10) ad hoc explanatory cost.

Classify: functionally motivated initialization; genuinely executed history; unresolved apparent record; or inadmissible rescue. Classification does not establish divine intent.

## Asynchronous-runtime subproblem
Distinguish terrestrial 24-hour chronology, proper time on cosmic worldlines, accelerated physical processes, and software clock-speed analogy. Develop causal models, light propagation, synchronization, energy and entropy budgets, and falsifiers. The analogy does not itself supply a physical mechanism.

## Adverse evidence
Detailed records without independent functional motivation; isotope patterns requiring arbitrary encoded conventional histories; astronomical transients incompatible with real-event models; theological cases of deception without explicit falsehood; unfalsifiable auxiliary proliferation.

## Acceptance criteria
Do not infer that all miracles deceive. Do not exempt DFM from evidence. Distinguish measurement, retrodiction, provenance. Identify at least one tempting DFM rescue that the test rejects. Document primary evidence, uncertainties, and unresolved physical mechanisms.

## Provisional finding
The unrestricted deception objection overgeneralizes. Its strongest version concerns detailed apparent records of events that never occurred. DFM must address this through independent constraints, actual extraordinary causal history where warranted, and severe testing.
