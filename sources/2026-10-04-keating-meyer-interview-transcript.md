## Relevance to DFM work packages

- **WP-026 (Oklo natural reactor severe test) / CCN-002:** Keating springs the Oklo "trap" — a functioning natural fission reactor with no human input. Meyer concedes design detection is not "complex + functional => designed"; the inference requires specification plus complexity exceeding available probabilistic resources. Directly informs the severe-test framing.
- **WP-015 (symmetric evidential standard):** Meyer defends the ID inference as abduction from uniform and repeated experience (minds generate specified/functional information), not argument from ignorance; extended primer on inference to the best explanation.
- **WP-018 (lived epistemology and provenance):** Meyer's model-collapse argument — LLMs trained on human text produce progressively incoherent output when iterated on their own outputs, evidencing dependence of specified information on a conscious source. Directly adjacent to the operational-naturalism / provenance distinction.
- **WP-007 (primary-source citation pass):** Named references (Quastler, Dembski, Nagel, Vilenkin, Hawking, ENCODE) usable as citation leads.
