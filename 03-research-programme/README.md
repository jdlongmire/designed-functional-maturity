# DFM Research Programme

DFM is managed as a progressive research programme. Hard-core commitments are distinguished from revisable hypotheses, mechanisms, initial-state proposals, and domain models.

## Research controls

The programme shall maintain a decision register, predictions/discriminators register, anomalies/unresolved-accounting register, evidence ledger with primary-source preference, hard-core/protective-belt ownership, and terminology control.

## Evaluation criteria

A development gains warrant through independent constraint, physical or logical necessity, cross-domain integration, known-case calibration, novel or risky consequences, reduction of dataset-specific auxiliaries, or improved concordance without bespoke adjustment.

A development incurs explanatory cost when weakly constrained, dataset-specific, introduced solely to protect preferred chronology, or repeatedly revised after contrary observations without independent gain.

Ad hoc character is an explanatory cost rather than automatic disqualification. The same accounting applies to competing programmes.
