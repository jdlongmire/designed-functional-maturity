# Decision Register

## DFM-DEC-001: Repository normalization

**Status:** Accepted for implementation  
**Date:** 2026-09-24  
**Source:** WP-004

DFM is treated as a research programme rather than solely a paper-development workspace. Canonical foundations, framework rules, research controls, domain investigations, work packages, and publication artifacts shall be structurally separated.

## DFM-DEC-002: Provenance-preserving promotion

**Status:** Accepted for implementation  
**Date:** 2026-09-24  
**Source:** WP-004

Mature conclusions developed in work packages are promoted into canonical surfaces. Historical work packages remain intact as provenance records.

## DFM-DEC-003: BWM boundary

**Status:** Accepted for implementation  
**Date:** 2026-09-24  
**Source:** WP-004

DFM owns its methodology and research programme. BWM owns broader world-model integration.
