# DFM Research Programme Charter

## 1. Programme identity

Designed Functional Maturity (DFM) is a Christian methodological-designist research programme that treats Genesis 1 as an actual historical description of God's ordered, supernatural, phased deployment of creation. It investigates how functionally mature created states are instantiated and commissioned into ordinary operation, and what follows for historical inference when post-deployment laws are extrapolated backward across supernatural initialization boundaries.

DFM operates inside the Biblical World Model (BWM). Scripture supplies the governing creation-history architecture. Natural observation supplies genuine constraints on the physical states, ordinary operations, and post-initialization histories modeled within that architecture.

## 2. Research question

Given an observed present state `O_t`, governing laws `L`, and a model of system history, what observations warrant identifying a reconstructed trajectory with the unique actual historical trajectory?

DFM focuses especially on cases in which a present mature state admits a long naturalistic reconstruction but may also be physically admissible as an initialized state.

## 3. Hard core

1. **Revealed phased deployment:** Genesis 1 records a real ordered sequence of supernatural creative/deployment acts.
2. **Sequential initialization:** successive creation phases instantiate, configure, populate, and commission real domains and systems.
3. **Functional maturity:** created systems may begin operation in mature states adequate for their commissioned roles.
4. **Coherent pre-seeding:** initialized state is integrated and physically coherent rather than a collection of arbitrary appearances.
5. **Terrestrial-day continuity:** God establishes the terrestrial light-dark, evening-morning cycle on Day 1 by supplying and purposefully constraining the initial light; on Day 4 the luminaries become its ordinary created governors and markers.
6. **Biblical supernaturalism:** divine action is a real causal category and cannot be excluded a priori from the revealed creation sequence.
7. **Methodological Designism:** ordinary created operation is investigated through designed natural capacities, stable regularities, laws, structures, and boundary conditions.
8. **Ordinary prospective physics:** observed physical laws govern subsequent operation by default.
9. **Retrodictive non-identity:** retrodictive accessibility does not establish historical traversal by logical necessity.
10. **Empirical accountability:** observations remain genuine constraints, and the protective belt must generate independently constrained, empirically progressive explanations.

The hard core is protected methodologically, not immunized empirically. Failure of an auxiliary is not automatically failure of the hard core, but repeated protective-belt failure without increasing predictive content counts against the programme.

## 4. Positive heuristic

DFM should:

- identify the commissioned function of a system;
- identify the minimum coherent physical state required for immediate function;
- determine which additional state variables are forced by physical coupling;
- derive observable consequences without importing the conventional historical answer;
- propagate the initialized state forward under ordinary physics;
- compare predicted present states with observation;
- record unexplained residuals;
- design severe tests capable of discriminating initialization from traversed history.

## 5. Negative heuristic

DFM should not:

- invoke accelerated processes merely to compress a conventional history;
- assign arbitrary initial values sample by sample;
- call every unexplained datum “created maturity”;
- deny measurements because their conventional interpretation conflicts with DFM;
- treat disagreement among conventional models as automatic evidence for DFM;
- use a conventional retrodictive age as an initialization input and then claim the recovered age as a prediction;
- protect an auxiliary indefinitely when it repeatedly fails independent tests.

## 6. Phased initialization formalism

The canonical Genesis-scale architecture is:

```text
S_i = G_i(Phi_i, L, B_i, S_{i-1})
```

where `S_{i-1}` is the already-deployed creation, `Phi_i` is the commissioned functionality introduced or enabled in phase `i`, `B_i` is the relevant coherent boundary state, and `G_i` represents the divine initialization/deployment act.

Schematically:

```text
S_0 --A1--> S_1 --A2--> ... --A6--> S_6
```

The local subsystem form remains:

```text
S_0 = G(Phi, L, B)
```

The distinction between deployment and operation is fundamental:

> **The rules that govern a running system need not be the process by which the system was deployed.**

For historical reconstruction:

```text
R = R(O_t | M, A, B)
```

DFM denies the logical identity `R = H` without historical-provenance warrant.

Likewise:

```text
T_R = R(O_t | M,A,B)
```

does not by itself entail:

```text
T_R = T_H
```

A retrodictive extrapolation may be mathematically precise while its chronometric interpretation remains conditioned on provenance and historical assumptions.

## 7. Functional versus deceptive maturity

DFM distinguishes functional initialization from fabricated evidence of nonexistent events.

A state is a candidate for functional initialization when it is independently required or strongly constrained by coherent operation, physical coupling, boundary conditions, or the commissioned role of the system.

A feature is presumptively historical when its best explanation is a contingent event trace that is unnecessary for initialized function and not forced by coherent state constraints.

The research burden is greatest where observations resemble detailed contingent event records.

## 8. Radiogenic architecture

For the radiogenic subsystem:

```text
S_0^rad = G(Phi_thermal, L_nuclear, B_geo)
```

and the working hypothesis is:

```text
S_0^rad -> C_rad
```

where `C_rad` is structured radiogenic coherence.

The programme expects a physically ordered initialized state. It therefore does not treat concordance among radiometric systems as intrinsically surprising.

However:

```text
C_rad !=> T_H
```

by logical necessity, and conversely the heat budget does not entail every observed isotope relationship. Quantitative derivation remains required.

## 9. Reconstruction Argument

Let `F_L^t` denote evolution under governing law `L`. A conventional reconstruction commonly asks for a prior state satisfying:

```text
F_L^t(S_prior) = O_now
```

DFM permits an initialization boundary `S_0` that need not itself lie on the backward natural trajectory generated by extending post-initialization dynamics indefinitely:

```text
S_0 not-in Range(F_L^{t<0})
```

The inability to generate `S_0` by backward extension does not make `S_0` physically incoherent if it satisfies the state constraints of the governing laws at initialization.

This is the formal core of the distinction between retrodictive age and historical age.

## 10. Explanatory burdens

Current high-priority burdens include:

- deriving multi-system radiometric concordance from common initialization constraints;
- accounting for non-heat-producing chronometers without age-target fitting;
- distinguishing mineral-scale historical traces from initialized state information;
- treating extinct radionuclide systems;
- defining the cosmological deployment model precisely enough to test redshift and light-propagation claims;
- addressing supernova remnants and nucleosynthetic inventories;
- specifying low-entropy initialization quantitatively;
- integrating Flood and post-Flood disturbances only where independently warranted;
- distinguishing genuine DFM predictions from observations merely compatible with DFM.

## 11. Predictions

DFM predicts at programme level:

1. Mature function may appear earlier than gradual assembly models expect.
2. Foundational physical states should exhibit global coherence rather than arbitrary “appearance of age.”
3. Multiple measurements coupled to the same initialized architecture may yield mutually ordered retrodictive markers.
4. Post-initialization processes should obey ordinary physical laws and produce genuine event histories.
5. Where conventional age estimates depend materially on different boundary or provenance assumptions, retrodictive ages may separate from independently established historical ages.
6. A successful DFM model should reduce initialization degrees of freedom as physical coupling is modeled more completely.

Predictions must be sharpened into quantitative forms before they count as strong corroboration.

## 12. Falsification and degeneration criteria

An auxiliary should be downgraded or abandoned when:

- it requires independent parameter tuning for each observation;
- it cannot reproduce observations without inserting the desired conventional age;
- it conflicts with well-established prospective physics without independent evidence;
- repeated new observations require unrelated rescue hypotheses;
- a competing model uniquely predicts observations that DFM only accommodates after the fact.

The programme as a whole becomes degenerative if its protective belt grows while independent predictive content does not.

## 13. Comparison standard and consilience

DFM and competing programmes receive symmetrical treatment:

- observations are observations;
- reconstructions are reconstructions;
- auxiliaries are allowed but costed;
- independent constraints increase evidential weight;
- cross-domain consilience matters;
- post-hoc flexibility reduces discriminating force;
- severe successful predictions increase programme credibility.

DFM expects substantial consilience in an initialized state because creation is modeled as an integrated functional whole. Consilience therefore does not embarrass supernatural initialization. It is a requirement of coherent initialization.

However, DFM must not infer from this that every particular consilient pattern has thereby been explained. Where a competing historical model derives a specific mathematical relationship, DFM bears the burden of deriving its alternative rather than merely labeling the observation coherent.

Canonical epistemic maxims:

> **Consilience establishes coherence; provenance establishes history.**

> **Measurement establishes state. Retrodiction reconstructs trajectory. Provenance warrants history.**

## 14. Research outputs

DFM development should produce:

- formal model specifications;
- evidence ledgers;
- auxiliary-hypothesis registries;
- prediction and discriminator registries;
- quantitative notebooks/models;
- adversarial test matrices;
- peer-facing papers;
- accessible public explainers;
- a canonical objections-and-responses register with stable IDs, dispositions, residual burdens, and links to severe-test work.

## 15. Objections and adversarial testing

DFM maintains a canonical [Objections and Responses Register](OBJECTIONS-AND-RESPONSES.md). Objections are stated in their strongest fair form and assigned explicit dispositions. A response does not close an objection merely by demonstrating logical possibility.

Where appropriate, the programme uses the formulation:

> **This objection does not defeat the argument, but it does identify a genuine explanatory burden that remains.**

Hard-core challenges, auxiliary defeaters, severe challenges, and ordinary explanatory burdens are tracked separately so that protective-belt revision is not confused with revision of the programme's theological commitments.

## 16. Governing maxim

> Physical maturity is a state property. Historical age is a trajectory property. Inferring the latter from the former requires a warranted history model.

> The rules that govern a running system need not be the process by which the system was deployed.

> Consilience establishes coherence; retrodiction estimates trajectory; provenance establishes history.
