DESIGNED FUNCTIONAL MATURITY

What if the rules that govern a running world are not necessarily the process by which that world was deployed?

DFM is a Christian methodological-designist research programme that treats Genesis 1 as an actual historical description of God's ordered, supernatural, phased deployment of creation, followed by ordinary operation through the designed regularities investigated by science.

AN ACCESSIBLE ANALOGY

The pre-seeded world

Imagine a virtual world initialized with terrain, rivers, atmosphere, ecosystems, astronomical states, and the internal variables necessary for those systems to operate coherently. Once released, it follows its ordinary rules.

An observer entering later could measure its present state and extrapolate those rules backward. The calculation might precisely answer how much ordinary runtime would be required to generate a feature from assumed antecedent conditions. It would not, by itself, establish how the world was actually deployed.

DFM does not claim reality is a simulation. The analogy illustrates initialization, state depth, coherence, deployment, operation, and provenance.

THE WAY WE ACTUALLY REASON

Operation is not the same question as provenance.

We live among initialized systems. We distinguish operation from deployment, state from provenance, and mechanism from agency every day. A computer can operate according to physical laws without those laws explaining who configured it. Software can change according to its internal rules without those rules explaining its initial state. An artifact can weather naturally without weathering explaining the artifact.

ORDINARY OPERATIONWhat does the system do under its current rules?
PROVENANCEHow did this particular system and state come to exist?

DFM applies the distinction, not the conclusion, to creation. Natural processes can explain how a created system operates without necessarily explaining how that system was initialized.

Nature can describe what a system does after it is running. That does not, by itself, tell us how the system came to be running.

Important limit: engineered artifacts have independently known designers. Their existence does not prove that nature was designed. The analogy shows that operation and provenance are legitimately distinct explanatory questions. DFM's positive claim of supernatural initialization requires independent warrant.

TECHNICAL TRACK

Phased initialization and historical inference

Sᵢ = Gᵢ(Φᵢ, L, Bᵢ, Sᵢ₋₁)

Variables: i = deployment phase; Sᵢ = state after phase i; Sᵢ₋₁ = already-deployed state; Gᵢ = initialization/deployment act; Φᵢ = commissioned functionality; L = governing law-set; Bᵢ = coherent boundary state.

Each phase receives the already-deployed creation and establishes newly commissioned functionality under coherent boundary conditions. The local subsystem form remains S₀ = G(Φ, L, B).

Tᴿ = R(Oₜ | M, A, B)   ⇏   Tᴿ = Tᴴ

Variables: Tᴿ = retrodictive duration; Tᴴ = actual historical duration; R = reconstruction function; Oₜ = observed state at time t; M = model structure; A = auxiliary assumptions; B = boundary conditions.

A retrodictive age can be mathematically precise while its identification with actual elapsed history remains conditioned on model, initial state, boundary conditions, auxiliaries, and provenance.

compatibility→accommodation→independent derivation→novel prediction→successful severe test

DFM and competing historical programmes are evaluated under the same epistemic standards. Consensus status does not itself promote a claim up this ladder. Conversely, weaknesses in a competing programme are not positive evidence for DFM.

CURRENT STATUS

A research programme, not a completed physical theory

DFM has unresolved technical burdens. Multi-system radiometric concordance remains a severe test. The programme has not yet derived the full decay-constant-indexed structure of concordance from an independently constrained initialization generator. That limitation is recorded rather than hidden.

The immediate programme priority is formalizing the Genesis-scale phased-deployment architecture before returning to the deferred radiogenic identifiability challenge.
