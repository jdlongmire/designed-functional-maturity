## Promotion rule

A work package can develop or test a proposition. Once accepted as stable programme doctrine, the proposition is promoted to the appropriate canonical surface. The work package remains intact as provenance.

## Current normalization

WP-004 is establishing canonical surfaces. During normalization, WP-002 remains the detailed source for retrodiction and initialization rules until promotion is complete.
