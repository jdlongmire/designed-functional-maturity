# Repository Charter

## Mission

This repository is the canonical home of the Designed Functional Maturity research programme.

DFM investigates whether observed physical states can be coherently understood under an intentionally designed and initialized creation boundary while maintaining rigorous separation among measurement, model-conditioned reconstruction, initialization, and historical provenance.

## Scope

This repository owns DFM definitions, methodological commitments, hard-core and protective-belt distinctions, formal inferential rules, research controls, DFM-specific domain investigations, work-package provenance, and DFM publication artifacts.

The broader Biblical World Model owns cross-model integration. DFM interfaces with BWM rather than absorbing it.

## Epistemic ordering

Scripture supplies the canonical historical and theological boundary claims adopted by the programme. Nature supplies the presently observable state. Models connect observations to proposed histories and mechanisms. DFM shall identify which category a claim occupies.

## Research posture

DFM permits model development, auxiliary hypotheses, and revision under the same standards applied to competing programmes. Progress is measured by increasing constraint, explanatory integration, empirical reach, and discriminating consequences.

## Provenance

Work packages preserve development history. Canonical documents own current formulations. Promotion from a work package preserves a link to the originating work.
